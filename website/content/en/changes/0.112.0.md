# 0.112 — A document is written in one tab: the complete service criteria agreement and basis of design, and what is shared stays in step

## New

- **One tab to write a document: «Document structure»**, the first group of the navigation, the same in the service criteria agreement, the basis of design and the report.
  - At the left the **tree**: chapters › sections (one `.typ` file each) › the **objects** of each file — headings, paragraphs, tables, lists to tick, choices, signatures. A box on every chapter and section to print it or not, multiple selection (Ctrl, Shift) and **drag and drop**: a section or a chapter anywhere in the tree, an object within its file or into another, above or below another object.
  - In the middle **the preview of what is chosen, and nothing else**: a chapter prints only its sections, a section only itself, an object only its own words, and with nothing chosen the whole document appears. It is a small page compiled in half a second, not a live preview.
  - At the right **the options of what is chosen**, by its kind: a table's rows (add, remove, up, down) and the fields of the row chosen, each column shown or hidden — **hidden but kept** —, its caption and notes; the live loads' category; the options of a choice or a list; the signatories; the norms to name; a paragraph in a plain text box, as it is written; the title of a section or a chapter and whether it is printed.
  - ⚠️ **Woodcore never shows Typst code.** To read or write code, open the file in VS Code: the tab reads again what you write there. The former «Sections» tab with its code editor is gone.
  - Nothing is deleted: a section removed is archived in `Sections/archives/`, and an object removed is copied there first.
- **Every heading, every paragraph and every table of a service criteria agreement or a basis of design is an object**, between two comment lines — `// woodcore:object <type> <id>` and `// woodcore:/object <id>` — that print nothing. The `.typ` file stays the source of truth: Woodcore writes an object again **between its tags and nowhere else**, in one form, and **warns before rewriting** an object you wrote by hand in another form.
- **Eleven new commands**, in the «Document objects» category of the Commands tab and of the reference: `#doc-table`, `#live-load-table`, `#dead-load-table`, `#doc-value`, `#doc-field`, `#doc-checklist`, `#doc-choice`, `#doc-image`, `#doc-plan`, `#doc-signatures` and `#show-norm-list`. Your own words (a cell, a caption, a label) are never translated; a narrow table column is hyphenated; **a table cell never carries a footnote**, whatever the level.
- **The service criteria agreement and the basis of design are complete, in French, German and English**, for a new project: **59 sections** for the agreement (48 printed by default) and **72** for the basis (66), built from templates of the BFH and of an engineering office and from SIA 260, with generic texts and no client data. **Seventeen sections are shared** between the two — the live loads, the norms, durability, earthquake, the accepted risks … — and each document ends with its own «Revision history». What is in square brackets, `[…]`, is to be replaced.
- **The live loads** are chosen by **category of SIA 261 Tabelle 8**: `q_k` and `Q_k` fill themselves from the export, and a number written over them shows **in orange**, «Revert» going back to the norm. ⚠️ **The factors ψ are never shown for live loads.** SIA 260 Tabelle 2 is transcribed for the load combinations to come, not to be shown.
- **What the documents share stays in step as you decide.** A shared section is a file copied into the other document; what lies between its `// woodcore:shared` tags stays the same. **A change only ever writes the copy of the document it was made in** — in VS Code or in Woodcore's right pane —: the other documents then show that file or object **orange** (changed elsewhere), **red** (changed on both sides: a conflict, which asks which version to keep) or grey (ignored). **Import changes** takes the other version, between the tags only; **Ignore** and **Reactivate** are remembered per document. Nothing is ever overwritten.
- **Import from another document of the project**: you choose the agreement, the basis or the report, tick sections and objects, and they stay **linked** to their source unless «Independent copy» is ticked. A section arrives at the end of the tree, an object taken alone in a section «Imported», from where you drag it to its place. In a section's right pane, **Share with…** copies it into another document, linked, and **Stop sharing** makes each copy independent.
- **Document templates and styles.** **Save as template…** keeps a document as you wrote it — sections, texts, what is switched on — in your templates folder or the office's; «New project» offers, for each of the two documents, the templates that exist in the project's language. The **style** — the `config.typ` the three documents share — is **neutral** or **compact**, chosen at creation (`woodcore-cli new --style`) or switched in the project's properties, which keep the document's language.
- **The catalogue of norms grows from 13 to 43 entries**: thirteen SIA norms, eight of which the library does not have (118, 181, 181/1:2026, 262:2025, 264, 266, 271, 500), and the VKF Brandschutznorm 1-15 and directives 10-15 to 28-15 as they stand in force. SIA 262:2013 has been superseded since 01.11.2025.
- **No terminal window opens for a fraction of a second** any more when Typst compiles: Woodcore now starts all its processes without a console.
- **A new project opens on the service criteria agreement.** A project already used keeps the document last shown.
- ⚠️ **Not done yet**: the preview does not follow VS Code's cursor; the report's files are not tagged until you ask; the design values of SIA 262:2025 are to be filled in, the library not having that norm.

## What changes in your reports

- **The text of your report does not change**, and its files receive no tag until you choose **Tag this file**: the tree then shows their headings, which cannot be moved or shared alone.
- **The engine of your projects becomes older** (a new version, a new file `lib/document.typ`), so Woodcore offers to update it the next time you open them; the eleven commands exist only afterwards.
- **A `sync.json` file appears in the folder of each document that shares something**, the first time Woodcore looks at what is shared: it is the memory of what is in step, and Woodcore alone writes it.
- **A project made with 0.111 keeps its two short documents** — a title page, a «Purpose» section and the revision history —: the complete documents are those of a new project.
- **Shared sections carry the same file name** in both documents (`live-loads.typ`) and cannot be renamed: the same identifier is the same section.
