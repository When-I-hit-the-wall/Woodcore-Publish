# 0.122 — The Ctrl+K palette, the Project tab, the interface's zoom

## New

- **The command palette, Ctrl+K** (or the field «Search… Ctrl K» at the top of the window): a tab, an action, a position or a check, a variable, a norm, a Typst command or a warning in a few letters. The categories under the field are chosen by a click or by their character: `>` actions, `@` project objects, `#` variables, `§` norms, `!` warnings. A click chooses and shows the detail and the actions on the right (open, copy the Typst call, show on the plan, new check); a double click opens; Enter opens the first result; Esc or the cross close it. A position shows what uses it: «3 checks, η max 0.91».
- **The Project tab**, first row of the navigation: the title page (carried over at once for the next PDF), the project's language, the layout template, each document's content template, the Woodcore version pinned, the folders and the last export. Woodcore opens on this tab, and **every change of document shows it**: no more compiling the whole document on arrival. Its «Templates» view shows a document with the content template and the layout template of your choice, writing nothing into the project. *File → Project properties* opens this tab.
- **The interface's zoom**: Ctrl+ and Ctrl− (Ctrl+= and the keypad too), Ctrl+0 back to 100 %, from 70 to 200 %, or Ctrl+wheel over a form or a list. It is kept from one session to the next and the status bar says it. The new **View** menu offers it, with *Appearance* moved there.
- **The Typst previews behave as in VS Code**: the page follows the pane's width, Ctrl+wheel zooms it under the pointer, and «Fit to width» puts it back to the width.

## What changes in your reports

- **New project** asks only the project's **name**, **place** and **language**. The language is final afterwards. The title page, the layout template and each document's content template are chosen in the Project tab, where the new project opens; **each document is created when you want it** («Create the document…»), and its content template no longer changes afterwards. While a document is not created, its tabs are greyed.
- **«Switch content template…» is retired** from «Document structure»: a document's content template is chosen once, as it is created.
- **A tab always opens in its working view** (*Calculation*, *Drawing*, *Entry* or *Data*), never in *Display*: arriving on a tab no longer compiles anything. The view chosen holds while you stay in the tab.
- The navigation's group called «Project» is called **«Data»**.
- Folding a tab's left pane gives its room to the centre pane at once.

## What to do

- Nothing in your existing projects: their language, their documents and their templates stay as they are; the Project tab says «unknown» for the content template of a document created before this version.
- If you had folded the navigation's group «Project», fold «Data» again.
