# 0.121 — The Typst commands rebuilt: a uniform core, one file per domain, native figures

## What changes in your reports

- ⚠️ **`#unit` now holds for the next value only.** `#unit("N")` changes the unit of the next result of that dimension, then the default unit comes back. For the rest of a domain write `#unit("N", scope: "domain")`, for the rest of the document `scope: "document"` (the former behaviour). A `#unit(` without `scope:` is reported at the export.
- **The Typst commands are rebuilt.** A calculation of Woodcore's — a timber or steel check, a load, a connector, a polar moment, a script — is written out whole into a file of its own and printed by `#include domain-file(kind, id)`. A drawing is an image in a numbered Typst figure: `#figure(image-positions-plan("Pavillon"), kind: image, caption: [Plan]) <fig-pav>`, cited by `@fig-pav` (likewise `image-fire-plan`, `image-static-model`, `image-schema`, `image-extract`). A position is named by `#position("T001")`. `#var` is the one command that prints a value: `#var("s_k", domain: ("snow-load", "SL"))` reads a value of the export. What a line prints is set by fine arguments (`display`, `label-position`, `descr-position`, `norm-position`), the level being only a preset of them, and `#calc-defaults(…, scope:)` sets them for a line, a domain or the document. The documents and the export sheets are written in raw Typst: a table is a `#table` the window edits cell by cell.
- **The retired commands** — `#show-calc`, `#show-input`, `#val`, `#var-unit`, `#var-line`, `#var-domain` (now `#domain`), `#member`, `#calc-block`, the `verify-*`, `#show-plan` and the other `show-*`, the `doc-*` and `sheet-*` objects — stop the export at the line that calls them, saying what to write instead; the full table is in the commands' documentation. Your projects are not rewritten automatically.
- **What is no longer printed**: the torsion of a timber check, the manual selection `level: -1, shown:`, an excerpt's automatic caption (the caption is your figure's); a sheet's list of the positions and its quantities are the whole project's.
- **A ratio without a unit, in per cent or in per mille**: without `unit:` a ratio such as η stays a number (0.741), `unit: "%"` prints it in per cent (74.1 %), `unit: "%%"` — two % signs one after the other — in per mille, printed ‰ (741 ‰).
- **The force arrows on a polar moment's figure are back**, through a new command, `#image-polar-moment`, in a numbered figure: the file Woodcore writes for a polar moment sets it right after N, V and M, and the arrows follow the values you write there (a number, or an `#eq` of your forces such as `#eq("N", `N_Ed`)`). Elsewhere: `#figure(image-polar-moment("VU-MP-1", N: 7.5, V: 25, M: 6.6), kind: image, caption: […])`.
- **`#distance`: `label:` is each table's title** (auto the norm's, `none` none, a text replaces it); `tab:`, `title:` and `norm-ref:` are no longer read: write `table:`, `label:` and `norm-section:` (the export says so at the line).

## What to do

- **Replace the retired commands in your sections**: the export names each file and each line, with what to write. The window's «Copy» buttons already write the new forms.
- Add `scope: "document"` to the `#unit(…)` that were meant for the whole report.
- **A polar moment already exported** carries its figure at its head and shows the red «form» line: click «Régénérer» in the Formules tab to get the figure with its arrows after the forces; your own lines (an `#eq` in place of N, V or M) are kept.
