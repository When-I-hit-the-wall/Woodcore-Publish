# 0.116 — The plans reworked: folders told from files, several frames per page, instant layers, the static models' snapping, plan types, superposition, export sheets with PDF bundles and the list of the positions

## What's new

- **Folders and files are told apart** in the left-pane lists. A folder has a folder icon (open or shut) and heavier text; a file at the root stands flush with the folders, and only the files **inside** a folder are indented, with the tree's lines. **Several selected files are dragged together** into a folder.
- **Each document belongs to the tab that imported it.** The "Position plan" tab does not show the documents imported in "Fire protection plan", and the other way round; the same goes for the export sheets. A fire plan can still **follow** a position plan, chosen in its "Background" card.
- **Several frames on one page.** The tick under a page makes the first plan (the whole page); every further frame drawn on that page with the Crop tool makes **a new plan** (P02, P03…). A **Select** tool in the document view chooses frames (Ctrl and Shift for several, Delete to archive them, a double click to open the plan); choosing a frame lets you **name it** at once in the right pane: name, description, storey, numbering character.
- **Layers switch on and off almost instantly.** Measured on a set of 18 pages and 74 layers: about 1 to 1.6 s a layer before, under a second now, and about 0.2 s for a combination seen before. The export of the plans on the page follows 1.2 s after the last change, in the background.
- **The static models' snapping in both plans.** Rest the mouse on a snap point for a moment: it is highlighted, and dotted snap lines run from it horizontally, vertically and at 45°. The cursor locks to them; **Tab** types a distance, Enter puts the point down. The ends of the storey lines and the four corners of the storey boxes are now snap points.
- **The right pane follows the selection.** Nothing selected: the plan (name, description, ID, type, storey, numbering character), the background, the **crop** above the **scale** (with "Calibrate…"), the symbols' size and the superposition. An object selected: only its card, with its own size, and the symbols' size. **Crop and calibration are no longer tools** of the bar: they are buttons of those cards. The left pane's boxes are resized with the mouse and the pane scrolls; the "Plans" list is called "Position plans".
- **A plan type** (under the ID): *Floor plan* (storey and numbering character), *Elevation section* (the storey line, the storey box and the **load transfer level**) or *Load path plan* (every usual tool, plus **the six load symbols** of STE Compact 02: point or line load, vertical in orange, horizontal in violet or both, numbered P… and L…). **A load is not a position**: it appears neither among the positions nor in the rows to copy for Excel. The **load transfer level** is a red line with an arrow pointing down at each end and its movable label "Load transfer level (x0xx)". The storey box carries STE's designation **at its centre**, x1xx for the first floor, and no longer the storey's name at its corner.
- **Show another plan over the open one.** With nothing selected, the "Overlay" module: choose another plan, with or without its elements, in normal colours, in grey or in one of five colours (green, blue, yellow, red, pink), at the opacity you like. **"Align…"**: two clicks, a point of the plan shown, then the matching point of the open plan. The plan shown cannot be selected, its points serve for snapping, and **it is never printed**.
- **The export sheets**: a sheet is **of one kind**, position plans or fire protection plans, never mixed. Above each plan **its title** is printed, "Name — description, storey (scale 1:n)", with a switch to hide it (`title: none`). The right pane: nothing selected, the **sheet** and its **options** (show the title block, the legend; add a plan view, a section, a text or a list of the positions, each by choosing which); an object selected, **only its module**. The position to highlight is chosen in a list of the positions on the plan.
- **PDF bundles.** In "Export documents", a folder is a **PDF bundle**: the sheets you drag into it (several at once, in the order you want) give **one PDF of several pages**, named like the folder. Each sheet keeps its own PDF. Buttons "New PDF bundle" and "Export the PDF bundle".
- **The list of the positions**, in three forms. A movable sheet object ("Options" → list of the positions), a button **"Create a list of positions"** that makes an A4 portrait sheet holding the list alone, and the Typst command **`#show-position-list(scope: "all", plans: (), storey: none, title: auto)`** for the report or the basis of design (`scope: "plans"` the positions drawn on plans, `"storey"` those of one storey; on a sheet, `sheet-position-list(…)`, by default the positions of the sheet's plans). Columns: ID, type, material, strength class, section type, dimension, units, moisture class, description; the table style is Woodcore's, and **the ID cell is coloured by the position's type**.
- **A position's type follows the language.** It is shown in German, French or English according to the language of the project (Positions tab) or of the compiled document (report, lists, legends); **the ID keeps the STE letter** (T stays T in French). A type typed freely stays as typed.
- **The "Fire protection plan" tab** gets everything that concerns the drawing engine — snapping, right pane, several frames, layers, documents per tab, sheet options and PDF bundles, superposition — but neither plan types, nor sections, nor the list of the positions.

## What changes in your reports

- **Nothing changes** in the text of an existing report, except in three respects.
- **An existing export sheet** now prints each plan's title above it; add `title: none` to the plan view to get the old page back. A sheet written before this version is read as a sheet of position plans, unless it holds fire protection objects.
- **Sections**: the storey box shows `x1xx` at its centre and no longer the storey's name at its corner.
- **In a French or English report**, a position's type (`#member`, a `calc-block` header, the lists, the legends) is printed in the document's language; in German nothing changes.
- **The engine of your projects becomes older**, so Woodcore offers to update it when the project opens, as usual.

## What to do

- **Nothing is required**: no file migration.
- Documents already imported go to the "Position plan" tab, except those that carry only fire protection plans, which go to "Fire protection plan". To use a document in the other tab, import it there.
- For a load path plan: open the plan, choose the type **Load path plan** under the ID, then the load tools.
- For a list of the positions in a report: `#show-position-list()` in a section of the report or of the basis of design.
