# 0.124 — The snow tab rethought, the first model tab of calculation

## New

- **The norm under each datum**: a ⓘ beside every field of the snow tab. On hover, the clause of SIA 261:2020, its words, then the image of the excerpt cut out of the norm. A click pins it on the right; Ctrl+click opens chapter 5 in Woodcore's reader, the clause framed. For Δh, Ctrl+click opens the snow map of 261.sia.ch.
- **The norms are shipped with Woodcore** (folder `norm-library/`): the PDFs of your norms, without the literature and without the SZS C4 and C5 tables.
- **The right pane made of modules**: the calculation's state (red, orange, green; a click takes you to the field), the results, the arrangement of Figure 3, an interactive Figure 2 and the excerpts pinned. Reorder them by drag and drop, fold them (▾), open one in a window that zooms (⧉). The order is kept on this machine.
- **The form**: the Building section (length, width) at the top; C_e as three buttons (sheltered 1.2, normal 1, exposed 0.8) and a value of your own; the five roofs drawn in black and white like Figure 3.
- **«Affichage» line by line**: the case's file compiled, the hidden lines shown pale; the domain's level (auto, 1, 2, 3); the list «Elements of the file» with 0 1 2 3 per line and each line's arguments. Your settings stay at the next export.

- **Delete no longer leaves a ghost**: a deleted object (a snow case, say) goes to “Archives”, and the form shows a new, empty object with no “unsaved” dot; it is saved only once you type into it. Until now that draft saved itself and came back at every “Delete” (issue #97).
- **A preview that fails to compile says when the project's engine is older**: under Typst's message, Woodcore names the version of the project's engine and reminds you to answer “Update” when the project opens or at an export (issue #98).

## What changes in your reports

- **A snow case's file** prints at its head the column of Figure 3 of its roof (load models 1 and 2). s_k is written with equation (10) at level 3. The cornice line (5.3.7) is no longer written: the tab says it.
- **German words in a French report corrected**: «Figure 3», «Annexe D», the symbols of the roof form and of the sliding, μ₁. This also touches the files of the wind and the permanent loads, refreshed at the next export.
- Above 2000 m the tab no longer says «computed all the same».
