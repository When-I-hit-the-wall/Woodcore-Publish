# 0.125 — Materials, permanent loads, wind, member checks; the Domains, Variables and Commands tabs rebuilt; the load workbook no longer read

## To do once

- **Click “Import data” once in every project.** The data Woodcore 0.120 to 0.124 wrote (`data/calculations.json` at 1.8, `data/project.json` at 1.0) is no longer read — this version writes 1.9 and 1.1: until the import is run again, the report does not compile and says so (“written by an earlier version of Woodcore … run «Import données» once”); the Variables tab and `woodcore-cli check` say it too.
- **The load workbook is no longer read.** Wind, snow and permanent loads now come only from the tabs “Wind”, “Snow” and “Self-weight and superimposed load”. The `WL-*`, `SL-*` and `EL+AL-*` sheets of a “Belastungsannahmen” workbook are not converted: enter your cases again in the tabs. The file stays in the folder and is never opened; each export names it in a warning for as long as it is there.

## New

- **The member checks, redone**: Tension, Compression, Bending and Shear in accordions, with the state of the calculation, the results and the norm's figure on the right. **Buckling and lateral torsional buckling** become the last accordion, «Stability», of the compression and of the bending: ticking «check the stability» makes the check a buckling (or a lateral torsional buckling), and its file changes folder with it.
- **The material comes from the library** of the Materials tab: solid timber, glulam, **panels of SIA 265/1** and your own timbers. A click on the material's bubble opens the menu, with a search («OSB», «panel»…) and, on each line, the resistance the tab reads. Grey, it follows the position («C24 — T001»); orange, it is written over, «Take back» returns to the position.
- **The loading is always said**: for a panel, its bubbles («as a plate ∥ 9.6», «as a diaphragm ⊥ 4.1»…), its range of thickness — the smaller side of the section — and the line «The check reads …»; for a member, one ticked bubble.
- **The norm's factors**: for a member, the moisture class (η<sub>w</sub>) and the action (η<sub>t</sub> 1.0 or 1.4), γ<sub>M</sub>/η<sub>M</sub> shown; for a panel, the moisture class, the load duration and the situation, which give η<sub>mod</sub> (Table 9) and γ<sub>M</sub> (Table 6, 1.0 in an accidental situation) of SIA 265/1.
- **Shear in three figures**: shear, shear-off, with a stress ⊥ to the grain (and its diagram of Figure 8). A stress σ<sub>90,d</sub> outside its interval is said in orange, the check computed.
- **The results in the order of the calculation**, the verdict first and η last; the row that governs a maximum is framed with a dashed line. The bending shows its **scheme in the style of SIA**, with the stresses at both edges.
- **Display**: the page compiled by Typst, the command `#include domain-file("check", …)` to copy, the level 0 to 3, the member line, the verdict in % or as a ratio, the description and the title — kept in the check.
- **The list by position**: the tab's checks under their position («T001 · Beam», «No position»), each with its rate in percent, green up to 100 %, red above; «By rate» orders them from the most to the least loaded. A check that cannot be calculated has no rate and says why.
- **The five cards of f<sub>c,90,d</sub>** in compression at an angle: in general, with end distance, end support, with or without consequence (the bracketed value), each with its value of Table 8 or 9 for the class; a card without a value is greyed (D30).
- **The norm behind every ⓘ**: the clause and figure of SIA 265 each datum follows — 4.2.1.1 to 4.2.9, Figures 7 to 11 and 46 — and the material's table (Tables 19a and 19b for OSB/3); a click pins them under «Results».
- **The window's bar** names the tab open: «Structural safety › Shear».
- **The Domains tab, rebuilt**: three modes on the same file, **Form · Code · Display**. The pills are gone.
- **The Form**: a row for each line of the file, with its line number. A variable: its symbol, its value, its unit, its label. An equation: its calculation **typed with the symbols** (`sigma_(m,d) / f_(m,d)`), its unit, its result. A check: its two values and ✔ or ✘. The row chosen shows its key, label, norm, clause and description under it.
- **A new line**: type «h = 240 mm» at the foot of the form; the pill says «var auto», the fields fill in, Enter writes it. The **+** between two rows adds a line there.
- **Delete and Undo**: a row's × deletes it, «Undo» gives the file back as it was. **Reorder** by the grip ⋮⋮; Ctrl+click chooses several lines, which move together. A move that would put a line above what it is computed from is refused, and says why.
- **A click on a variable or a result** on the right writes its name into the calculation being typed, where the cursor was.
- **Link a variable…**: boxes, Ctrl+click, Shift+click, then «Confirm» links everything ticked at once. Typing a name, the list offers the project's variables too.
- **The domain's status**, in your language: a fault said once, at the line and column where Typst sees it, with the number of lines depending on it; in orange a check that fails. A click leads to the line.
- **The Code**: the .typ file coloured, each line's result on its right; the command of the cursor shows all its arguments, editable. A file that does not read back is not written.
- **The Display**: the domain compiled, its `#include` to copy, its level, **the domain's options to tick** (as a block, positions, style). A new domain prints **as a block**, one line a value.
- **«Clause» writes `norm-section:`** (it wrote `norm-ref:`, which the compile refuses beside it). The norm's bubbles for eight clauses of SIA 260, 261 and 265.
- **The Variables tab, tidied**: on the left a menu of Woodcore's tabs («Every tab» by default), each entry with its number of variables, then the source — All, Export, Files, Report — and the domains under the title of their tab. A domain no document prints is grey.
- **The filter in the search bar**: the tab, the source and the domain chosen are tags there, taken off by × or ⌫; what you type is searched in that filter, every word («kn snow»).
- **The list always grouped by domain**: a title line per domain, its variables under it, one line each; the symbol as the report prints it; column widths that no longer move at every search, adjustable and kept. A result's value is framed.
- **Several variables at once**, as in Windows: Ctrl+click, Shift+click, the boxes; «Copy the n commands» puts one `#var` line per variable.
- **The card**: the command to copy and what to include before it, where the value comes from in words, «Open the … tab» bringing forward the tab where it is computed on its case, and the same calculation in the other sources. This tab's «Display» view is gone.
- **The Commands tab, condensed**: every command has its short page, written by hand in English, French and German — one sentence saying what it does, «The essentials» in three or four cards, then its arguments as a table, grouped, each with its sentence and two or three examples.
- **The line of the command at the top**: every argument and its default. Hover an argument: what it does. Hover its value: every possible value, and for each the Typst call and **what it prints**, compiled once by Woodcore. A click keeps the bubble open, Escape closes it.
- **On the right**: the minimal and the complete call to copy, the list of the examples (a click leads there), and the calls of the command **in your project**.
- **The categories by task**: Calculating in the report, Printing a drawing or an object, Reading the project's data, The document and its citations, Writing templates. The search reads each command's name and sentence.
- **An example printing the same page at every level** shows one, and says so.
- **The website** shows the same condensed page and the same bubbles, with the pointer as with a finger.
- **Dead and superimposed loads, redone**: one compact line a layer; the material is typed into its cell, the category named first («insulation», «timber»…); Tab goes from the material to the thickness, the spacing, the width, g/a and the kN/m², skipping the cells that do not serve. Four layings: continuous, spaced, per surface, load entered.
- **Lines linked on one level**: «Link to the line below» puts the insulation between the rafters; by default it fills what the pieces leave on 1 m (rafters 60/625: 904 mm), its width in italics. Two lines a level at most.
- **The retained value is computed**: with nothing typed, the tenth above or equal to the sum (0.909 → 1.0). A layer's load is replaced in its cell, in orange, with «Restore». ⚠️ The report now always prints the retained value.
- **The right pane, in order**: the warnings (a click takes you to the line; battens counted twice under tiles weighed «with battens» are said), «Layer n», «Section | Contributions», then the results. The section is at its true proportion, 1 m wide, and a click on a layer opens it.
- **Templates and overview**: «New» offers eight templates from Lignum, *Holzbau mit System* (with the page and what the template supposes), your templates saved on this computer, or an empty build-up. «⊞ Overview» shows every build-up of the project and its red warnings.
- **Library corrected**: the workbook's «Glas» weighed 5000 kg/m³, it weighs SIA 261's 25 kN/m³; the façade's «Scobalit» weighed 0, it weighs 500 kg/m³. A build-up's file prints the section from level 2 and the table of the layers at level 3.
- **A Materials tab, at the very bottom, under «Others»**: your library of materials, for all your projects, saved on your computer (`%APPDATA%\Woodcore\materials\`) and not in a project. On the left the list by category, which the search finds by name, category or type in English, French and German; in the centre the material's sheet, in folds; on the right its state, its source, the tabs it is offered in and where it is saved.
- **Member, panel or other**: «+ New» asks for the type. A member has its family and its six strengths, characteristic and design side by side; a panel its wood-based product, its optional thickness and SIA 265/1's grid per range of thickness, ∥ and ⊥; an «other» a weight only. A design value left empty is deduced (SIA 265 (3), SIA 265/1 Tables 6 and 9) or taken from the standard (shear and tension ⊥, SIA 265 3.3.2.2 and 3.4.2.2): in italics, its rule on hover. A value typed always wins.
- **Woodcore ships, read only**: the classes of SIA 265 Tables 8 and 9, the 20 panels of SIA 265/1 Tables 11 to 23 (plywood, particleboards, OSB/2, OSB/3, OSB/4, fibreboards, MDF), the weights of SIA 261 Tables 28 to 30 and the workbook's library («External»). Clicking the source label shows the excerpt of the standard's table on the right; ⤢ opens it large. «Duplicate into my library» makes a copy you can change.
- **A material's source** is a website or a PDF: the PDF chosen or dropped is copied into the library, never read where it was.
- **Dead load creates no material any more**: a layer chooses a «Material» from one list, searched by typing, or a «Load entered». A project keeps a copy of your materials it uses, so it computes alike on another computer; if your library has changed since, the tab says so and offers to take the new version or keep the copy. Materials a project had typed into its own `materials.json` move into your library when it is opened, a message naming them.
- **A new snow case compiles again**: in a French or English project the preview stopped on «dachform has a symbol but no value». The roof's form and the sliding print with their translated names.
- **The case's file follows the calculation**: changing the roof and saving writes the file again in the new form, keeping what you set in «Affichage». «Regenerate» leaves the calculation tabs, and switching from «Calcul» to «Affichage» saves the case.
- **A second click on a ⓘ removes the excerpt from the right pane**, as its cross does. The ⓘ of a pinned excerpt is filled, and the bubble says what the click will do.
- **Δh (Annex D) takes a value of your own**, in metres, right of the map's five buttons, as C_e does.
- **Inputs and results light up together**: hovering a result highlights the inputs it depends on, hovering an input the results it goes into.
- **The snow form is an accordion**: Building, Site and Roof fold one by one, each with a summary of its values and a green, orange or red dot.
- **C_e and C_T are written with their subscript** in the form and in the state of the calculation.
- **«Search… Ctrl K» is at the top left**, just right of the number of values and the export's date.
- **The norm's window opens inside the screen**, its title bar visible, on a laptop's screen too.
- **The search (Ctrl+K) no longer stacks its buttons**: clicking one entry after another, the right pane keeps only the buttons of the last one chosen. Before, the «Run» and «Open» of the earlier entries stayed drawn on top of one another and over the entry's line.
- **The Snow tab is now built from shared building blocks**: the list of cases, the form, the pane of modules and «Display» are written once, and the next calculation tabs (Self-weight and superimposed load, Wind) will take them as they are. Nothing changes on the screen.
- **The Self-weight and superimposed load tab takes the Snow tab's form**: on the right, the state of the calculation, the results followed by the table of the layers, and the drawing of the build-up, which ⧉ opens in a window of its own. «Display» shows the case's file line by line, and that file follows the case as the snow's does: when the calculation changes its form (a value chosen beside the sum, for instance), it is written again at the save, without a question, and a change made by hand in that file is lost.
- **The wind's file is written anew**: the global forces are printed as Q_k [kN] with their numbers, the lines q_k per m² kept above them; the local pressures keep only the openings' case chosen, in a wide and low table — a column per surface, a row max, a row min, cut into blocks of at most twelve surfaces — and now include c_d: q_k = q_p · (c_d · c_pe − c_pi). No German word in a French or English file. Existing `Variable-loads/Wind/*.typ` files are written again at the first save, and a change made by hand in them is lost. A case saved before this version, like a sheet of the workbook, takes uniformly distributed openings.
- **In «Affichage» a table of the file is set like a figure**: «Show the table», «Title» and «Note», with no level; a hidden table stays hidden at the next saves, and its numbers follow the calculation, so that they are right on the day it is shown again (for instance the table of layers of a permanent load).
- **The Wind tab is rethought throughout**, like the Snow tab: four sections — basic data, the building of Annex C, reduction and force coefficients, pressure coefficients —, the 37 buildings of Annex C drawn in 3D (turned with the mouse, zoomed with the wheel, Side A, Side C, Iso, Top, the wind's direction φ), the norm's figure beside it, the norm's tables in colour, q_k per direction in detail, and two 3D results (global forces, surface loads) that can be pinned to the right and opened in a window. Hovering over a number lights what gave it: its surface on the building, the coefficients c_pe and c_pi framed. The case's file prints the norm's figure at level 3, cut at the save into `Variable-loads/Wind/Exports/Figures/`.
- **The wind's right pane is the prototype's**: under the values, the global forces Q_k max and min with their direction φ, then the tables Walls, Roof and Local areas with max pressure and max suction, each with its φ; hovering a value highlights its surface and the coefficients it came from. A «Building» module shows the table and the building. The Building section switches between the norm's figure and the 3D model with «Norm | 3D model», and the table's title is shown in your language.
- **The «Project» and «Document structure» tabs are built from shared building blocks**, like the calculation tabs: nothing changes on the screen, in the files or in the reports.
- **The «Variables», «Quantities», «Dependencies» and «Commands» tabs are built from shared building blocks**, like the calculation and document tabs: nothing changes in the files or in the reports. On the screen, the banner that says an export is missing or older than its sources is the same in the three tabs that have one: the «Quantities» one takes the orange frame of the other two, and the «Variables» one now stands above the bar «Read the documents again».

## Fixed

- **A project open read-only refuses «Write the file» and «Add»** of the card «The list's file» in the «Quantities», «Journal» and «Positions» tabs, with the usual message: they wrote the list's `.typ` file in spite of it.
- **«Affichage» no longer keeps the rows of before**: the command to copy, its button and the levels, built again at every save, stayed in the card behind the new ones; in the member checks, where the card is built again at every option, Woodcore could close abruptly. They are now taken out each time.
