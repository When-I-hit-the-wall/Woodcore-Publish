# 0.87 — Everything that changed since 0.83

Published on 22.09.2026. The first installable version since 0.83.0: freer formulas, variables in the text, SIA 265's minimum distances, a PDF beside the report, and tables and calculations set as the books set them. The version that asks for the most reading through.

## New in the report

- **`#var` prints in the text**, like a word: `symbol = value unit`. Its label, description and citation go into a footnote. The **`note:`** argument chooses: `note: "footnote"` (one footnote, the default of `#var`, `#var-unit` and `#val`) or `note: "line"` (on the line, the default of `#eq`).

```typ
The section is #var("b", 100, "mm") by #var("h", 200, "mm").
#var("R_k", 7, "kN", label: "Manufacturer's resistance", note: "line")
```

- **`level: 0` prints nothing** and computes everything: to declare a value a formula names further down. Levels 1 to 3 always print something: 1 the value, 2 with its label, 3 with its description and citation.
- **`label:`, `descr:`, `norm:` and `norm-ref:`** on [`#var`](~/docs/var/), [`#val`](~/docs/val/), [`#eq`](~/docs/eq/) and [`#var-unit`](~/docs/var-unit/): they replace what the value already carries.
- **[`#find`](~/docs/find/)** lists what there is to name in a call — the domains and their keys, or one domain's variables. You call it while writing, read it, and delete it.

```typ
#find(object: "variable", domain: ("snow-load", "SL"))
```

- **`#var(…, domain: …)`** reads a value from the export (`domain: ("connector", "SD_01")`) or from a `var-domain` of the report (`domain: "S10"`).
- **[`#distance`](~/docs/distance/)** prints SIA 265's minimum distances and timber thicknesses (Tabellen 24, 27, 28, 32, 33, 36 to 41), in mm for the diameter given: `#distance(12, "dowel")`.
- **Formulas easier to write**: an unquoted word in a symbol (`"G_(1,Ed)"` instead of `"G_(1,\"Ed\")"`), `*` to multiply in `#eq` and `#verify` (printed `·`), and every key accepted (`M2`).
- **`#eq(…, style: "double")`** rules a result twice; **`#verify`** at level 3 prints a formula in letters, in numbers and its value.
- **A formula too wide for its line** is broken at its `=` signs, aligned, instead of running into the citation column.
- **A polar moment publishes its forces**: `#val("F_max", domain: "VU-MP-1")` quotes the largest force after `#show-polar-moment`.
- **A dowel connection** prints SIA 265's formulas under headings, as the Bemessungsbeispiele do — R_d before R_d,Verb, and K_ser,Verb from level 1.

## New in the window

- The **Commands** tab speaks French and German.
- When a project opens, Woodcore offers to **update its engine** (see [What's new](~/changes/)).
- **Fasteners**: the dowels' layout comes before the dowels, and their number follows the ticked markers.
- **Static models**: a load has a colour (red, green, orange, blue, violet); a dimension shows its value, its name, both or nothing; the lists' columns widen by dragging; the tool bar is half as tall.
- A position's dimensions (`dimension`, `units`) are optional in `Positionen.xlsx`.

## What changes in your reports

- **`#var` calls on consecutive lines form one paragraph.** Until now each one made a line.
- **`level: 1` no longer hides a declaration**: it prints it, as the client's report should show it. The label prints at level 2, the citation at level 3.
- **The PDF is written beside `Statischer Rapport.typ`**, in `Statischer Bericht (typst)/`, no longer one folder above.
- **Tables have three rules** and no grid; a column of numbers is aligned right.
- **A negative number** prints with a real minus sign, «−0.25».
