# 0.117 — Member checks saved in Woodcore: the «Tension», «Compression» and «Bending» modules and two new navigation groups

## What's new

- **Position plan — dimensions and a ruler**: horizontal, vertical, aligned, chained and angle dimensions, with the same tool symbols as the static models and the schemas; the value is computed from the plan's scale. A **ruler** measures two points and saves nothing (on the fire-protection plan too). Dimensions are shown or not in each view: «Dimensions» box in *Drawing*, options `dimensions:` and `lengths:` in *Display* and *Export*.
- **Position plan — numbering**: every object drawn takes the next number of its letter and storey (S101, S102 …; S201 on the 2nd floor). Positions are made when the plan is saved. Deleting a position renumbers the following ones everywhere — plans, checks, schemas and Typst files —, after a preview in which each replacement can be unticked; a position still cited is refused, or kept as «S110-old» with «Copy the values».
- **Position plan — options and section**: choosing a tool shows the object's options at once, set for the next one drawn; the *Section* field, above the position, takes another position's section (as a copy) or is typed in fields for every type, steel profiles included. Columns turn with the selection's circle.
- **Quantities**: by material and section — pieces, lengths, timber volumes — with `#show-plan-quantities` in a report and «Add the quantities» on an export sheet.
- **Fixed**: a plan's «Scale bar» on an export sheet no longer stops the compile.

- **Fixed**: a position plan on a CAD drawing (DXF, DWG) is shown in an installed Woodcore — a library the drawing needs was missing (Pillow). The scale list of a plan on an export sheet stays open even when the compile ends while it is open.
- **A new «Formulas» tab (Reference group)**: write a calculation block by block — `var`, `eq`, `verify`, `val`, `var-unit` — in fields (Tab to the next, Enter or a click elsewhere in the middle pane to validate, Escape to give up), and **between the blocks, Typst of your own**: a title, a sentence, a formula, coloured as in the Python tab, printed as it stands. A command of the palette goes where the cursor stands. A validated block shows what matters of it: an equation on two lines, in letters then in numbers; a double-click opens it again, **a single click shows its options** on the right, written as soon as they change. A calculation is typed with the names shown and units (`12 N / 3000 mm`) and **computed at once**; what the report would refuse is refused straight away, with the reason.
- **Every calculation is in a domain**, a file `Formules/<id>.typ` of the project, **its ID the file's name**: on the left the domains — with **folders**, **the order you give them by drag and drop**, «Archives» and duplication, as in the Schemas tab —, in the middle the domain as a text, on the right its variables (a click writes the variable into the calculation being typed), the options of the block chosen, the domain's card (its **ID**, which can be changed: the file is renamed and Woodcore lists the calls to correct) and the commands to add, all in one column that scrolls. The file can be edited in VS Code too: the tab reads what is written there.
- **Units are typed in the calculation**: `12 N / 3000 mm` is recognised and converted, and the result shows at once; the output unit is chosen on the right (kN or N, kN/m or N/mm…). A calculation the report would refuse — a force plus a length, an unknown variable — is refused with the same reason, and nothing is written.
- **«Import…»** takes a variable from the export, another domain or a document: the «Variables» tab opens in a choosing mode, the other tabs unavailable, and the variable chosen comes into the domain.
- **The «Formulas» tab's Display view**: the domain's page as the report prints it, and «Copy» gives the line `#include "/Formules/<id>.typ"` to place in a section.
- **Two navigation groups, in the report alone**: *Structural safety* (SIA 265 4.2: Tension, Compression, Bending, Shear, Torsion, Buckling, Lateral-torsional buckling) and *Serviceability* (Deformations, fatigue included, and Vibrations). The convention d'utilisation and the basis of design show neither. «Bending» is bending, **alone or combined with a normal force**; «Shear» holds the shear and the compression perpendicular to the grain.
- **«Tension» (SIA 265 4.2.1), «Compression» (4.2.2) and «Bending» (4.2.3, 4.2.4) are built.** The six other modules say they are to come, what they will hold and which command of the report does the work meanwhile (`#verify-compression`, `#verify-bending`, …).
- **«Bending» checks the section alone, without lateral torsional buckling or buckling** — those are the modules «Lateral-torsional buckling» and «Buckling». You give the angle α between the member's axis and the grain, the moments about y and z and the **signed normal force N**: tension +, compression −, 0 for none. Without a normal force, equation (14) along the grain, (15)/(16) at 90° and, between, (17)/(18) with k_mc,α and k_mt,α of (19), (20); with a tension (21), with a compression (22). At an angle the moment about z is closed: (17) to (20) read the one about y alone. **A normal force at an angle** is allowed: (21) or (22) then take f_t,α,d of (9) or f_c,α,d of (13) and k_mt,α or k_mc,α, on the side where the normal stress adds to the bending, the other side being checked by (17) or (18) alone — the norm does not write this case, and the page says so.
- **`#verify-bending` and `#verify-bending-normal` take `stability: false`**: the section alone, with no «Stabilität» part and no buckling length. `#verify-bending-normal` also takes `alpha:`.
- **A tension check is an object saved in Woodcore** (`Woodcore-Datei/checks/<id>.json`), with a list on the left (folders; «Delete» archives), a form in cards in the middle (name, description, ID; the position; the material; the section; the effort) and, on the right, **the results**, calculated instantly (see below). The **Display** view (see below) shows the page the command prints, its options (check level, member line, description, title, unit) and the coloured call with «Copy».
- **A value follows its position, and you can write over it.** The strength class, the section and the moisture class are taken from the position (they read «C24, taken from T001»); a value you type stays written over, in orange, with «Take back». Without a position you state the strength class, the section (b/h or d) and the moisture class yourself. The design force N is typed in kN; the angle α between the force and the grain and the impact factor η_t (SIA 265 2.2.6) are options of the check.
- **The Typst command `#show-check-member(id, level:, member-level:, description:, title:, unit:)`** prints a saved check: the member's line with `#member`, then `#verify-tension` — or, for a compression, `#verify-compression` (with `stability: false`) or `#verify-compression-perp` — with its verdict (✔ green, ✘ red). The report computes in Typst, as every check of SIA 265 4.2: Woodcore saves and resolves the values; the window's instant calculation is for display only. `#check-member-ids()` lists the checks saved, for a loop. A check that cannot be calculated (a position gone, no section, C16 along the grain, which SIA 265 Tabelle 8 does not allow for tension members) is exported all the same, and the report refuses it **with its reason**.
- **`#verify-tension`**: N and, at an angle, α now stand on one line (`N_t,d = 20 kN; α = 10 °`), where two inline variables side by side ran into each other («20 kNα = 10 °»).
- **«Compression» has a single angle α** between the force and the grain, from 0 to 90°. At **α = 0** the section alone is checked, σ_c,0,d = N/A ⩽ f_c,0,d (4.2.2.1), **with no buckling** — buckling will be the «Buckling» module —, thanks to the new **`stability: false`** argument of `#verify-compression`. At 90° equation (11) applies; between the two (12) with f_c,α,d of (13), through `#verify-compression-perp`.
- **The resistance f_c,90,d is chosen among five entries** of SIA 265 Tabelle 8/9: *General*, *With end timber* (at least 100 mm of timber on both sides) and *End support*, the last two *with* or *without consequence for the load-bearing member* — «without consequence» is the bracketed value (larger indentations provably without influence on the load-bearing member). A grade without that value (D30) greys the two entries out. The bearing width w follows the section's width; typed, it stays written over, in orange, with «Take back».
- **The results appear with no wait**: an instant Python calculation gives every value (symbol = value unit), the SIA 265 clause, the equation and the verdict η ⩽ 1 ✔ (green) or ✘ (red). The Typst page is in the **Display** view, compiled only while it is shown. The report still prints Typst's calculation, which Woodcore holds equal to Python's by a test.
- **Woodcore's website has a new address: https://woodcore.pages.dev/** (it was on the Bern University of Applied Sciences' GitLab). The installer is no longer in the site but published on GitHub (public repository `woodcore-releases`); the download page leads there and gives its SHA-256. From this version on, Woodcore looks for its updates at the new address and accepts an installer only from that repository or from the site itself.
- **One task at a time: a tab's view is chosen at the top right**, left of FR · DE · EN. The position plan and the fire protection plan have four views: **Import** (the imported documents alone), **Drawing** (the plans, the elements and axes, and the drawing tools), **Typst** (a plan chosen on the left, its compiled page in the middle, every option of `#show-plan` on the right) and **Export** (the export documents and their folders). Tension, Compression, the loads, the fasteners and the polar moments have **Calculation** and **Display**; the static models and the schemas **Drawing** and **Display**; the positions and the journal **Entry** and **Display**; the variables **Data** and **Display**. Woodcore remembers each tab's view.
- **The Display view** shows in the middle the page as the report prints it and, on the right, the command, chosen among those that can show what is chosen on the left — for a variable `val` or `var` —, all its options, then the call to copy. The calls to copy have left the other views. A command that prints nothing, or reads the whole document, says so in place of the page; an object changed since the last export says so too (click «Import data»). The positions tab now offers `#show-position-list`.
- **No more «Save?» question**: changing tab, view, document or going to another application (VS Code, Excel) saves what can be saved. What cannot keeps its white dot, and the status line says why. A change of ID is never saved without you (the «Save» button); a new object left empty is not written. «Import data» and «Export PDF» save what is in progress first.
- **Position and fire protection plans**: each box of the left pane has its own height, dragged by its bottom edge without changing the others; the pane scrolls up and down, and an arrow at the bottom left folds it away.
- **No more «no load workbook» warning** for a project without a load workbook: the export says so in one line, with no warning window.
- **Woodcore shows itself at once**: a start screen appears half a second after launching — logo, version, a bar from 0 to 100 % and the list of what is loading — where nothing appeared for 4 to 30 seconds. Woodcore is on the taskbar from that screen on. Once open, no tab loads anything the first time it is opened.
- **Faster**: the window builds in 11 s instead of 12 to 21 s, and rebuilds after an export in 7 to 11 s instead of 12 to 20 s; the export itself takes 2.6 s instead of 6.6 s. Zooming and scrolling the plans is smoother: the page in view is rendered sharp first, the export sheet is no longer rendered whole while the window paints, and a plan's symbols are no longer redrawn at every frame.
- **Errors at the top right, no longer in windows**: a red octagon counts the errors, an orange triangle the warnings, each grey at zero and blinking for three seconds when a new one arrives. A click opens the list under them: each message opens, copies (with the file, the line and the technical text, ready to send) and deletes; «Delete all» empties the list. A Typst error names its file, its line and its column, and «Open the file» opens it at the line in VS Code. The messages follow the window's language; the technical text stays as Typst or Python wrote it.
- **What works in the background is said** left of the indicators («Loading…»), and an operation that blocks shows in the middle what it is doing, for how long and, where it knows, how far it is.
- **No more terminal window**: from the sources, `Woodcore.pyw` opens Woodcore with no console at all, where `Woodcore.bat` showed one for an instant.
- **Position plan**: snapping is set in the right pane when nothing is selected; a column can be turned (an angle field, «↻ 90°» or the R key); the length of the walls, the beams and each side of the slabs is shown for the whole plan or element by element; «Section…» opens the section chosen with the plan's storey already chosen and the storey line ready to draw, «← P01» going back to the plan; an object's position reads in the window's language; the tab is called «Plan de positions» in French, and its «Typst» view is called «Display».
- **Export view**: choosing an export document shows all its pages and, on the right, its id, its name, its description and its storey — taken from the first plan put on it as long as you do not change them; the scale list no longer closes by itself; a list of the positions and a legend are widened with the mouse.
- **The far-left pane**: its groups fold with a click on their title and its width is set with the mouse. **The panes' widths are kept** by tab, by view and by screen: the laptop alone and the laptop on its external screen each have their own.
- **« — T001 »** replaces «from T001» wherever a value follows its position.
- **«Actions» has two sections**: *Permanent actions* (Self-weight, Non-structural self-weight) and *Variable actions* (Imposed load, Snow, Wind, Earthquake). The six tabs are empty for now; «Load combinations» stays below them.
- **«Area» with a position**: as soon as the tool is chosen, the right pane shows its options — the drawing mode, the position and, for a rectangular section, the dimension that is drawn, width or height; the other is the position's, fixed. The element drawn carries the position.
- **A «Dimension» section in the Positions tab**: a box for each number of the section type — width and height for `rec`, thickness for `pla`, diameter for `cir`. A dimension you have not touched is never rewritten.
- **The `#show-position-list` table** no longer goes past the right margin, runs over several pages (whole rows, the heading repeated) and takes `columns:` (the columns printed), `widths:` (a column's width, in mm or %) and `legend:` (a text under the table). In «Display», the old «Table caption» is called «Title», the columns are ticked with their width, and the options chosen are kept while the window is open.
- **Saving a position no longer freezes the window**: only the tab in front is refreshed, the others when you open them, with their state. The first visit of a tab costs its construction (0.5 to 1.1 s).
- **The «Variables» tab in three columns**: the domains on the left, the chosen domain's variables in the middle, the detail on the right. It now lists **every** variable of the project with its **provenance**: the **Excel** workbooks, what **Woodcore** computes (loads, connectors, polar moments, scripts, the domains of the «Formulas» tab) and what the documents compute in **Typst** (CU, BP, RS). The box **«Every domain»** carries the filter over every variable; **«Results only»** keeps the results, marked ★. The detail shows the level, the norm, the formula, the choices and the workbook's cell, with «Open the workbook».
- **The «Loads» tab is retired**: every one of its values is in «Variables», with the same commands in the Display view (`val`, `var`, `show-input`, `show-calc`, `show-check`, `show-live-load`, the loop and the two summary tables). A call is copied from the Display view: a double-click and a right-click no longer copy.

- **Every tab's left pane folds away** by the arrow at its foot, as the plans' does, and Woodcore remembers it from one session to the next. It also narrows much further: to about 120 px, its buttons shortened (their whole text in the tooltip) and its titles wrapped.

- **The notifications** stand between the views' switch and the languages'. A closed row shows up to three lines of the message (instead of two words); a click opens it whole. The text can be selected and copied: right click «Copy», Ctrl+C on the row, or «Copy all» for all of them.

- **Positions tab — lengths and quantities**: a column «Length [m]» gives the length each position is drawn with on the position plans (all its elements added up). A card «Quantities» on the right totals by material and section — pieces, lengths, volumes — for everything drawn, one plan, or the positions selected (Ctrl or Shift + click in the list).

- **Annexes tab — every page and three ways to extract**: a source is shown whole, scrolls and zooms (Ctrl + wheel, buttons −, +, «Page width»); zoomed out, several pages side by side. The section «Extract» of the left pane offers **Frame** (every frame drawn makes an excerpt, anchored on the title above it; Shift + drag redraws the frame of the excerpt selected), **Pages** (from… to…: one page, one excerpt) and **Frame found** (Woodcore offers the frames drawn in the PDF under the pointer; Alt + wheel or a right click takes the frame round it, a click extracts it).

- **Position plan — Move, Copy, construction lines**: «Move» takes the object selected by a base point and puts it elsewhere; «Copy» puts down a copy at every click until Escape (a copy keeps its position number). Four **construction lines** — horizontal, vertical, through two points, parallel at a distance — help drawing and snapping; they are saved with the plan and never printed. At the foot of the left pane, «Selected object» offers «Duplicate» and «Delete». Move, Copy, Duplicate and Delete exist on the fire-protection plan too.

- **Level of detail −1 — manual selection**: `level: -1, shown: (A: 2, W_y: 1)` prints only the variables named, each at its own level of detail; the title and the verdict are always printed. For the tension, compression and bending checks (`#show-check-member`, `#member`, `#verify-*`), the fasteners (`#show-connector-resistance`, `#show-calc`) and the polar moment (`#show-polar-moment`). In Woodcore, in the Display mode, choose «Manual selection»: every variable has a box and, right of it, its level of detail; 0 unticks it. You never type a name.

- **Formulas tab — Calculation and Display apart**: in Calculation, a variable or an equation chosen offers only its label, description, norm and clause; in Display, choose it in the list on the left to set everything about how it is printed — level of detail, **display type** (formerly «Label and citation»), style, summary table —, written into its file at once. The `val`s, which show a variable again without making one, are in italics in the list.

- **«Level of detail» everywhere**: that is what the level is called now in the whole window, the PDF menu and the commands' reference.

- **Fixed**: entering the Display mode no longer shows the title page «Statischer Bericht» for half a second before the right page.
- **Self-weight and superimposed load, Snow and Wind are computed in Woodcore** (issues #35, #36, #37), each in its tab of the *Actions* group, from SIA 261:2020 rather than from the *Belastungsannahmen* workbook — whose `SL-*`, `WL-*` and `EL+AL-*` sheets are still read beside them. Each case is a file `Woodcore-Datei/loads/<dead|snow|wind>/<id>.json`, with a list, folders and «Archives»; its result shows at once and the report prints it with `#show-calc`.
- **«Self-weight and superimposed load»: one tab** (it replaces the reserved *Self-weight* and *Superimposed* tabs). You enter the layers top to bottom, each **self-weight (g_k) or superimposed load (a_k)**; the material comes from **SIA 261 Annex A**, from the **Woodcore materials library** (236 materials, with their source) or from **your project's library**, where you add unit weights of your own. Battens or rafters at a spacing are given by their width and spacing, the insulation between them by «Beside».
- **The build-up is drawn**, the way Ubakus draws it: the layers to scale, the **heights dimensioned** on the left, the **width and spacing** of spaced members below; the same drawing is printed in the report (from level 2).
- **«Snow»**: altitude and Annex D's correction, exposure, C_T, the roof form of Figure 3 — flat (with Figure 4 over 20 m), mono-pitch, duo-pitch, shed — and **the curved roof, the third column of Figure 3**, with μ₃; both load models and their sketch. Hindered sliding takes μ₁ = 0.8 on every side **before** model 2 halves it.
- **«Wind»**: q_p0, terrain category, b, d, h and **the Annex C table of any building** (31 to 60b, 63, 64 — flat, mono- and duo-pitch roofs, sheds, open buildings, a grandstand, platform canopies, canopies, tall buildings, hemispheres, semi-cylindrical roofs). **c_red is computed** from Figure 9 instead of being read off a scroll bar; the global forces per reference area and the local pressures, external and net, show at once.
- **Buckling, Lateral torsional buckling and Shear are built** (issue #38): three more saved checks, with the instant result and the report's page. Buckling (SIA 265 4.2.8) reads the system lengths and their coefficients β; lateral torsional buckling (4.2.9) the distance a between lateral supports or a σ_m,crit, and a signed normal force — with a compression, equation (37) and its buckling lengths; shear (4.2.5) V_z, V_y and the pith. E₀,₀₅ is multiplied by η_w,E, the moisture class included.
- **A detail schema that does not read is said**: the fasteners' and polar moments' tabs left it out without a word; a warning now names the file and what is wrong, and offers to open it.
- **Sharing from a terminal**: `woodcore-cli sync status|share|import|move` does what the «Document structure» tab does — see what is shared and where each block stands, share a section, import the changes, move a section. A conflict is never settled for you: without `--choice` the import is refused and nothing is written. `woodcore-cli check` reports a `sync.json` that does not read, naming the file and the field.
- **For whoever works on Woodcore's code**: `python tests/bootstrap.py` prepares a machine for the whole test suite in one command — the pinned packages, Qt's libraries on Linux and the Typst named in `typst-version.txt`, refused unless its SHA-256 is the one recorded. The suite runs on GitHub at every push (Linux, and Windows on `master`). The code is split into modules of 1500 lines at most, with nothing changed in what Woodcore does or prints; the documentation in `docs/` is reorganised (a file per tab, the test catalogue apart, a status on every decision, an index) and a test checks its references. On Linux and macOS, a document unchanged since its PDF is no longer compiled again.

- **A value can follow a variable** (Tension, Compression, Bending, Buckling, Lateral torsional buckling, Shear, Static models): the small 𝑥 button to the right of every number field opens the «Variables» tab in its choosing mode, the other tabs greyed out, and shows only the variables whose unit fits — for a force in kN, the variables in N, kN or MN; for a length, those in mm or m. The value chosen is converted into the field's unit, may be taken with a factor and an offset (N = 1.5 × q_k), and **follows the variable**: at every export the field takes its value of the moment. Under the field, the variable and its domain, and «Unlink» to go back to a typed value. A variable that has gone keeps the last value known, in orange, with a warning — the report still compiles. The moisture class, the strength class and the other lists are not linked; nor is a section that follows its position, until it is stated.
- **Static models**: a selected point has a card «Coordinates» (x, z), and its coordinates, the springs' stiffnesses and the influence width b_e can follow a variable. A load's magnitude is chosen in the «Variables» tab («Choose…») instead of the three lists domain, case, variable.

- **Connectors, polar moments and loads follow a variable too**: the 𝑥 button is on a connector's diameter, f_u,k, α, t, a₁, b, slot and t₁/t₂; on a polar moment's hand-placed centre, its directions and its trial forces — **now saved with the calculation**; on every number of the Self-weight and superimposed load (layers included), Snow and Wind tabs. A load follows only another load or a domain of the «Formulas» tab; a connector or a polar moment a load, a script or a «Formulas» domain. A link that would send loads round in a circle is refused as it is chosen, and the export stops, naming the circle.
- **A «Text» tool in all four drawings** (Schemas, Static models, Position plan, Fire-protection plan): a click places a text, which you write in the right pane, move and delete as the rest of the drawing, and whose size follows the «Text» slider. **«Insert a variable»** puts any value of the export or of a «Formulas» domain where the cursor is; for each you choose «value» (`17.5 m`) or «symbol = value» (`L = 17.5 m`). The value follows the variable at every export; a variable that is gone prints its last value, with a warning. An `_` sets what follows as a subscript, as in the labels. A drawing that carries a text no longer opens in an older version of Woodcore.
- **«Formulas» tab — commands as pills, in the text** (issue #40): a command validated is shown as a small rounded rectangle — the shown variable, the value, the unit (an equation's answer; a check's two values and ✔ or ✘). It stands **in the sentence** as Typst prints it — text on the left, the pill, text on the right —, and **on a line of its own** once `note: "line"` asks for it. Drag a pill to move it in the text; a double-click opens it as fields on a line of its own. Enter beside a pill starts a new paragraph. For a `var`, the **shown variable comes first** and the computation variable writes itself (Tab to replace it).
- **Level −1 «Manual selection», more compact** (issue #41): each variable has four joined buttons `0 | 1 | 2 | 3` instead of a box and a drop-down — 0 leaves it out —, and the variables stand side by side as the pane's width allows. A check's result offers `1 | 2 | 3` only.
- **`sep:` on `#var`, `#eq` and `#var-unit`**: the separator typed after the call (`#var("b", 208, "mm") ;`) is written `#var("b", 208, "mm", sep: ";")` and printed right after the value and its footnote; a value its detail level hides takes its separator with it. In the «Formulas» tab, «Separator (inline)» under «Display». Refused with `note: "line"` and inside a `var-line`, which writes its own.
- **«Formulas» tab — no more lost lines**: a domain changed on the disk (in VS Code, say) is never written over by the tab any more; it says so in a warning and reads the file again. Another unreadable file of `Formules/` no longer gives an «Unexpected error»: the domain is saved and the reason shown as a warning.

## What changes in your reports

- **The schemas' labels move slightly**: they are now set by the static models' text engine (the same letter widths, the same subscripts). Nothing else changes in a schema; check a label that only just cleared a line.
- **A unit in quotes is never read as a variable**: with a variable `m` declared, `#eq("L", `m + (3 "m")`)` is computed (three metres), where the line died on «cannot be computed». Nothing changes in a report that already compiled. In the «Formulas» tab a variable named `m`, `N` or `s` keeps its name instead of becoming `m_1`.
- **A domain of the «Formulas» tab is printed by `#include "/Formules/<id>.typ"`** where the line stands: its name is printed as a title, one level below the section's (`#var-domain("<id>", title: "<name>")`, a new argument `title:`), and each command at its own level or the report's. A value of the domain is read further down with `#val("M_d", domain: "<id>")`: renaming the domain breaks no call.
- **The RFEM tab and the reading of RFEM exports are retired**, and so is the old «Formulas» tab (keyword lines). The commands `#rfem-max`, `#show-rfem-forces`, `#show-rfem-combination`, `rfem-ids`, `rfem-bars`, `rfem-situations` and `#show-static-model`'s `diagram:` argument are gone; `#member` and the `#verify-*` stay.
- **Table titles and figure captions** read «Table 1 — The reference documents» and «Figure 1 — Structural system» (Tableau/Figure, Tabelle/Abbildung), in the three documents.
- **A plan's legend** gives under its symbols its **quantities**: the walls and the beams in metres, the slabs in m², the columns as pieces — summed on a sheet of several plans.
- **An export document's bundled PDF** is named after its id (Plans/<id>.pdf): the id of the first plan put on it, unless you give one.
- **`#verify-bending` at an angle** publishes each side's utilisation as `eta_m_t` and `eta_m_c` (formerly `eta_t` and `eta_c`, which overwrote the member's impact factor η_t): a hand-written `#val("eta_t", domain: "…-verify-bending")` now reads `eta_m_t`. The verdict `eta` is unchanged.
- **Nothing changes** in a report that does not call `#show-check-member`.
- `#verify-compression` has a new **`stability:`** argument (default `true`: nothing changes in your reports); `false` checks the section alone, with no buckling length.
- The tension check at an angle prints N and α on one line, in `#verify-tension` and so in `#show-check-member`.
- A new export file, `data/checks.json`, is written at every export (empty if you have no check); the engine of your projects becomes older, so Woodcore offers to update it when the project opens.
- `#show-position-list(scope: "plans")` with no plan named **prints nothing any more**, instead of stopping the report.
- The list of the positions wraps and hyphenates long words where the page needs it: nine columns do not fit an A4 portrait page, so choose fewer columns or give widths.
- The Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 shortcuts of the tabs after «Actions» move by six places: the six new tabs of «Actions» take theirs.

- **The `verify-*` checks print the ratio of the action to the resistance**: `σ_t,0,d / f_t,0,d = 0.41 ⩽ 1 ✔` at level 1, with the numbers at levels 2 and 3, and in % `σ/f = 41 % ✔` (without «⩽ 100 %»). Neither the utilisation η nor the form `σ ⩽ f` is printed any more; η stays the value `#val("eta", …)` and the summary of the checks read. The pane of the Tension, Compression and Bending tabs shows the same line.

- **A plan saved by this version is version 1.6** (its construction lines): an older Woodcore no longer opens it. Turning a plan's page now turns its dimensions too.

- **`#eq` prints inline by default**, as `#var` does: it may follow a `#var` in the same sentence, its label, description and norm gathered into one footnote. Every `#eq` already written without `note:` therefore looks different, and `#eq`s on consecutive lines make one paragraph; `note: "line"` keeps the line of its own, the norm in the right-hand column. The engine's checks (tension, compression, bending…) do not change.
- **A check's result is always printed at the level of detail −1** (issue #39): the stress (σ_t,0,d for a tension) prints at level 1 where `shown:` does not name it, at 1, 2 or 3 where it does; level 0 is refused. In «Display» its box stays ticked and greyed, its level runs from 1 to 3, and the manual selection is no longer lost when the draft changes.
- **`#verify-shear` takes level −1 and `shown:`**, as the other checks do; its footnote on a round section says that 1.5 · V/A and 4/3 · V/A are mechanics (SIA 265 equation (23) compares τ_d with f_v,d).
- **`#show-check-member`** also prints a saved buckling, lateral torsional buckling and shear check; **`#show-calc`** prints the drawing of a build-up and the sketch of a snow load entered in Woodcore (at level −1: `figure-1`).

## What to do

- **A project that still names an RFEM export** opens and exports as usual; a warning says so at every export. Take `rfem_models` and `rfem_export` out of the `.woodcore` file to stop it. A section that called an `#rfem-…` command needs changing.
- To use it: open the report, choose **Structural safety › Tension**, press «New», name the position or state the section, the strength class and the moisture class, type N, save. Copy the call and paste it into a section: `#show-check-member("Z_01")`. For a compression choose **Structural safety › Compression**: `#show-check-member("DR-01")`.
- For all the checks: `#for id in check-member-ids() { show-check-member(id) }`.
- The call names the check by its ID: **save the check** (saving also exports it) before compiling the report.
- **If Woodcore 0.116 or older is installed on your machine**: this version and the next ones will **not** be offered by the automatic update — they are announced at the new address, which your Woodcore does not ask. Download the 0.117 installer from [woodcore.pages.dev](https://woodcore.pages.dev/en/download/): it installs beside your version, replaces nothing, and it then looks for the new versions itself.
- For a table that reads better: `#show-position-list(columns: ("id", "type", "dimension", "description"), widths: (id: 20mm))`, or tick the columns in «Display».

- **To keep an `#eq` on a line of its own**: add `note: "line"`, or choose «On the line» as its display type (Formulas tab, Display mode).
- **To separate two variables on one line**: replace `#var(...) ;` with `#var(..., sep: ";")` — the «Formulas» tab refuses a `;` typed after the call and suggests this spelling.
- **In your *Belastungsannahmen* workbook**, correct what the comparison with the norm found: the wind coefficients of Table 35 (0°, c_pi D −0.85), 39 (90°, c_pe C 0.85), 40 (90°, c_pe F −0.8; directions 0°, 45°, 90°, 180°), 41 (180°, c_pi A −0.85), 42 (0°, c_pe F −0.6), the directions of Tables 42 and 43; facade glass at 5000 kg/m³ in the material library (SIA 261: 25 kN/m³); and in the snow sheets, model 2's half load that ignores hindered sliding (N37/N38).
