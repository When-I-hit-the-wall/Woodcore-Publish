# 0.96 — A window like the website, the positions and your templates

Woodcore now looks like this website. It writes Positionen.xlsx from a «Positions» tab and holds your project templates. The window is called Woodcore wherever it was called «Katalog». In your reports, `#member` replaces `#show-position`.

## New

- **A window like the website.**
  - The modules sit in a side navigation: Project, Actions, Structure, Connections, Reference.
  - The button at its foot folds it to its icons. Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+9 open a module.
  - The top bar shows the project's name and the language as FR · DE · EN. A chip appears when a level or a language is forced for the next PDF.
  - The start page shows its three actions and your recent projects as cards.
- **Forms in cards** in the Position plan, Static models, Schemas, Fasteners and Polar moments of inertia tabs.
  - A label too long for its row goes above its field.
  - The two pieces of a connection stand side by side where there is room.
- **The Typst calls are coloured** as on the website, and their button says «Copied ✓».
- **Symbols as mathematics.** The symbols of the loads and the results are shown with their subscripts.
- **A «Positions» tab** (Project group) lists and edits Positionen.xlsx.
  - You can add, change, rename, duplicate and delete a position.
  - The ID is proposed from the type and the floor.
  - The dimension is checked against the section type.
  - Your Excel drop-down lists are kept.
  - After each save, Woodcore runs **Import from Excel**.
  - In the position plan, the alert «no position of this letter» offers **New position…**.
  - In the schemas, a **+** sits beside Position.
- **A «section type» column in Positionen.xlsx**, after the strength class, with its drop-down. Each type and how its dimension is written:
  - `rec`: «b/h»
  - `cir`: «d»
  - `pla`: «t»
  - `clt`: its layers, e.g. «20/30/20/30/20»
  - an SZS C5 steel profile (`IPE`, `HEA`, `ROR` …): e.g. «101.6x10»
- **`#member` takes any position**, and calculates round timber too: `#member("S003")` reads its section from Positionen.xlsx, `#member("R1", d: 200, grade: "GL24h", moisture-class: 1)` states it.
  A position it cannot calculate (steel, a panel, CLT) is described, and the `verify-*` checks say why they refuse.
- **File › Settings…** (Ctrl+,):
  - project templates: the Standard template, your own, and the office's in a shared folder;
  - the default author, office and checker;
  - the languages, the appearance and the updates.
  **New project** offers a template, and so does `woodcore-cli new --template <name>`.
- **The «Commands» tab** is laid out like a command's page on this website, in the same order, «Calculating in the report» first.
  - In an English window its examples are printed in English.
  - The examples that show `level: 0` have their 1 · 2 · 3 buttons.
- **Static models and Schemas**: every tool is its icon alone, as tall as the plan's tools, and the drawing gains the height the bar took.
  - Resting on a tool shows a tooltip with its name and how to use it.
  - «Schemas» now sits under «Structure», and Ctrl+5 opens it.
- **Licence.** Woodcore is © 2026 Florian Lamprecht.
  - The installer asks you to accept the licence.
  - **File › About Woodcore…** opens the licence and the list of third-party software.
  - This website's **Legal notice and licence** page publishes both.
- **The name.** The window is called Woodcore wherever it was called «Katalog»: its titles, its messages, the commands' documentation and the report's error messages.
  - Step 5 of the tutorial is now «Import and explore in Woodcore's window», at `tutorial/window/`; the old address `tutorial/katalog/` still leads there.
  - A project keeps its empty folders under the `gui` key of its `.woodcore` file: the old `katalog` key is read, then replaced at the first change of folders.

## What changes in your reports

`#show-position` is gone. Replace

```typ
#show-position(id, description: true)
```

with

```typ
#member(id, level: 1, description: true)
```

The printed line is the same. A `calc-block` shows `#member`'s line at its head, and you do not need to change the call.

The numbers in the report do not change: a section is read from the same text as before.

A position with no section type whose dimension reads «b/h» is read as `rec`. The export names it and says so. Existing workbooks therefore export as before.

## What to do

- When each project opens, accept **Update** for the engine.
- Replace `#show-position` with `#member(…, level: 1, description: true)` in your sections.
- Give each position its section type, then click **Import from Excel**. The **Positions** tab adds the column at its first save.
- Close Positionen.xlsx in Excel before saving from Woodcore. Otherwise click **Retry**.
- Optionally, make your own project template: **Settings › Templates › New**, or **Save this project as a template**.
