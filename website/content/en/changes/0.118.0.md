# 0.118 — The fire resistance of timber members

## What's new

- **Woodcore is free software.** It is published under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later: you may use it for any purpose, study it, change it and pass it on, provided a version you pass on stays under the GPL with its source code. The source code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/When-I-hit-the-wall/Woodcore-Publish). *File › About Woodcore…* and the [Legal notice and licence](~/legal/) page say the same.
- **A «Fire resistance» tab**, the last of the *Structural safety* group. It checks a timber member in the design situation fire of SIA 265:2021 (4.5.2): bending, shear, buckling, and compression or tension of the section.
  - The ideal residual section is reduced by d_ef = β_n · t + d_red on each face you tick (bottom, top, left, right). β_n is SIA 265's Tabelle 13, by the strength class; for a hardwood (D30) you choose beech or oak.
  - The resistance is R_d,fi = 1.8 · R_d (equation 51), without k_h.
  - The minimum width of 4.5.2.4 (80 mm for R30, 140 mm for R60) is checked as soon as several faces are exposed.
  - The forces are those of the fire situation, E_d,fi. Each may follow a variable.
- **The required duration comes from the VKF / AEAI directive 15-15.** A card *Building* describes your building once for the whole project: height category, use, concept and storeys.
  - Each check takes its duration R from it: for instance R 60 for housing of medium height (table 2). Notes [1], [5], [8] and [9] and the exemptions of 3.2.3 (single-family house, building of reduced size) are applied.
  - A duration you type stays written, in orange, with «Take back».
- **In the report**, `#member(…, fire: (duration: 60, sides: ("bottom", "left", "right")))` writes the member down as its residual section. The usual `verify-*` commands then check it in the fire situation.
  - `#show-check-member` prints the checks saved in the tab.
  - The summary of the checks marks them «(fire)».
- **Dowel steel.** In «Connectors», the list *Dowel steel* (S235 to S460) fills f_u,k with the value of SIA 263 Table 1. The default stays S355 (510 N/mm²). SN EN 14592:2022 asks only for a yield stress of 235 N/mm² and gives no f_u. A value typed by hand replaces the steel's.
- **Bearing of the plates.** For a steel-to-timber connection, enter the plate thickness, the end and edge distances e1 and e2 and the hole diameter d0. Woodcore then computes F_b,Rd per dowel to SIA 263 Table 16 (76), capped by clause 6.2.5. The spacings p1 and p2 are read off the drawing. The result `F_b_Rd` is printed beside R_d: verify it against the force on one dowel.
- **Snow, wind and dead load: choose what the report prints.** In the *Display* view of the three load tabs, the level of detail now offers **-1 «Manual selection»**. Each variable, each table, and the dead load's drawing, gets its level `0 | 1 | 2 | 3` (0: not printed). The call copied is `#show-calc("snow-load", "SN-01", level: -1, shown: (…))`.
- **Snow above 2000 m.** An altitude h₀ above 2000 m is now accepted and computed by equation (10). The «Snow» tab warns that SIA 261 (5.1.4) does not cover this case; the report says nothing of it.
- **Snow: a «Height step» box.** It reminds the report of the extra load of Figure 5 (5.3.6). Above 800 m the report also recalls the cornice of overhanging roof parts (5.3.7). Neither is computed.
- **Lateral torsional buckling:**
  - A round section can now be saved, with M_y, M_z or both and no distance a (k_m = 1).
  - A section wider than high tips about z.

- **A «Steel» section** in the navigation, before *Reference*, with the checks of SIA 263:2013 (first step: buckling, lateral torsional buckling and connections will follow).
  - **Profiles**: SZS C5's tables (1038 profiles IPE, HEA, HEB, UPN, angles, tubes…) are now in Woodcore, read from the book and checked against the reference workbook. You can also draw a **free profile** in rectangles, from a model (I, U, T, L, tube, plate) or a C5 profile, and name it in a position with the type `USR`.
  - **Section class** after tables 5a and 5b, with the governing part. A class 4 section is reported, not calculated.
  - **Tension, Compression, Bending (with a normal force), Shear**: the profile and the steel grade are taken from the position, and you can write over them.
  - In the report, `#show-check-steel("ST-B01")` prints the calculation with the norm's clauses; it appears in the summary of the checks.
- **The verdict at level −1 «Manual selection».** In the right pane of «Display», each verdict of a check (the line σ/f ⩽ 1) now has its buttons `0 | 1 | 2 | 3`, like the other lines, under the symbol of its utilisation: η, η_s, η_c,y, η_m,t… In the report it is named by that key: `#verify-tension("T", N: 40, level: -1, shown: (eta: 1))`. At level 1 the verdict is brief; at levels 2 and 3 it shows its numbers too. The stresses σ and τ are ordinary lines again, no longer locked.

## What changes in your reports

- **Fire changes nothing as long as you do not use it.** A check without fire is saved and exported byte for byte as before; fire changed no existing example page.
- The reference workbooks served as a check.
  - *Bemessung im Brandfall Biegung* is reproduced exactly.
  - *Bemessung im Brandfall Knicken* gives the same section, the same slenderness and the same k_c. Its resistance (k_fi · f_k, EN 1995-1-2), however, is some 15 % above SIA 265's (1.8 · R_d), which Woodcore applies.
- **Wind:** in an oblique wind direction (15°, 22.5°, 45° …), c_red is now the larger of the two (SIA 261 6.3.3). A global force governed by an oblique row may rise by a few percent.
- **Snow:**
  - The 2000 m limit applies to the site's altitude h₀, no longer to h₀ + Δh.
  - **The sketch of the load arrangement is no longer printed**, at any level; it stays visible in the tab's *Calculation* view. A report that printed the snow at level 2 or 3 loses this drawing at the next export.
  - The clause numbers the reading of the *SL-* sheets cites are SIA 261:2020's.
- **Pith (Markstück):** f_v,d is no longer halved for glulam; Table 8, note 6, applies to solid timber only. Such a check gives a lower utilisation, and the report says so.
- **The 0.75 reduction in a truss:** the box and the report now state its condition, «connections of the infill members with a high bending stiffness, simplified calculation» (SIA 265 5.3.6.2.2).
- **The materials library:** it is now called «Woodcore library», the name the report gives as the source of a weight. Aluwellblech, Blech Aluminium and Blech Aluminium 2mm carry the values of SIA 261 (4 kg/m², 2700 kg/m³, 5.4 kg/m²).
- **Level −1: a verdict not named is no longer printed.** Until now a check at level −1 always printed its stress and its verdict. Now only what `shown:` names is printed, the verdict included.

## What to do

- **Fill in the card *Building*** of the «Fire resistance» tab so that your fire checks follow VKF 15-15, or type each check's duration.
- The *Biegung* workbook shows a minimum width of 80 mm for R60, read from an external link; SIA 265 asks for 140 mm. Correct your template if you still use it.
- For your steel-to-timber connections, enter the plate (t, e1, e2, d0) in «Connectors», then verify `F_b_Rd` in the report.
- If a dowel is not S355, choose its steel.
- **Your calls at level −1** (`level: -1, shown: (…)`) print no verdict any more: add its key, for instance `eta: 1`, or `eta_s: 1, eta_y: 1, eta_z: 1` for a buckling check. In Woodcore, set the verdict in the right pane.
