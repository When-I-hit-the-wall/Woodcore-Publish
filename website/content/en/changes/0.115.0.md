# 0.115 — Annexes: excerpts of your analysis reports, in each of your three documents; and the fire protection plan

## What's new

- **An Annexes tab** (Project group, after the Journal), shown in the service criteria agreement, the basis of design and the report. Its list holds two fixed folders, **Sources** and **Extraits** (excerpts).
  - **"Add a PDF…"** copies a PDF into the project — first of all the print report of a finite-element program, RFEM's for example — to `Woodcore-Datei/annexes/sources/<id>.pdf`.
  - **"Replace the PDF…"** writes a new version over it; every excerpt is then looked for again in the new version, and the card says how many were found.
  - **"New excerpt…"**: choose the source, click the title of the diagram or table on the page (or pick it from the PDF's list of bookmarks), then drag the frame over the page.
- **An excerpt is anchored on the bookmark of its title**, without the chapter number before it (5.16 or 6.4, it is the same). A new version of the report with more pages and renumbered chapters is therefore followed by itself.
- **Two Typst commands.** `#show-extract("id", width: 100%, caption: auto)` prints the excerpt as a numbered figure in vector quality; `#show-annexes(heading: 2)` prints, for every source, a cover page and then the pages the excerpts come from, full page, with the extracted region framed and linked to the excerpt. Data access: `extract-ids()` and `annex-ids()`.
- **Each document prints the pages of its own excerpts.** An excerpt may appear in the service criteria agreement, the basis of design or the report; the annexes of each document carry only the pages of the excerpts **that** document shows. The letters (Annex A, B…) stay the project's. An excerpt shown in a document that prints no annexes is simply not a link.
- **Two objects in the "Document structure" tab**, offered by "+ Object": **"Extract from an annex"** (`doc-extract`: the excerpt chosen from the list, the width, the caption) and **"Annexes of the project"** (`doc-annexes`: the heading level of each annex's name).
- **An "Appendices" section in the three content templates**, switched off by default: `009-anhang.typ` in the report, `annex-pages` in the agreement (Appendices chapter) and in the basis of design (before the journal). Switch it on where the annexes are to appear.
- **If an excerpt cannot be found in the new version of its source**, the export warns and the document **refuses to compile**, naming the excerpt and the reason: a wrong diagram is worse than no report.
- **A "Fire protection plan" tab** (Project group, right under "Position plan"), shown in the basis of design and in the report. It is the position plan's tab a second time, on the same documents: a PDF, an image or a CAD drawing is imported **once**, and ticking a page in this tab makes a fire protection plan of it, as ticking it in the other tab makes a position plan. The crop, the calibration, the ±90° turn, the layers, "Show the whole page", the saving when you leave, the size and the opacity of each entry (orange, with "Take back"), the "Typst command" builder and the export sheets work as they do there.
- **It draws what the top of the VKF's sample legend lists, and nothing else**: surfaces (vertical escape route, horizontal escape route, surface to be kept clear), walls by fire-resistance class, doors (swing or sliding, EI 30, E 30 or without fire resistance), the length of an escape route, the emergency exit, the width of a passage, and a legend box. Everything is drawn by hand: the program delimits nothing and checks no VKF limit.
- **A fire plan follows a position plan, or has a frame of its own.** Ticking a page that carries exactly one position plan makes a fire plan that **follows** it (its background, its scale and its crop, with a crop of its own allowed and "Take back" returning to the position plan's); any other page gives it a frame of its own. A calibration drawn on a page where the fire plan follows is refused: calibrate it in the position plan.
- **Both kinds of plan stay in step.** Turning a page, from either tab, turns the plans of both kinds drawn on it; the layers and the archiving export both; the other kind's crops are shown muted in the document view. Unticking or archiving a position plan that fire plans follow says so and **detaches** them first: the frame they followed is copied into them.
- **Typst.** `#show-fire-plan("id", width: 100%, legend: true, scale-bar: true, caption: auto)` and `fire-plan-ids()`; on an export sheet `sheet-fire-viewport(…)` (its `legend:` is the legend drawn on the plan) and `sheet-fire-legend(…)`, a legend of its own that can be moved; and, in the "Document structure" tab, the object **"Fire protection plan"** (`doc-fire-plan`). The "Typst command" builder writes the call for you.
- **Plans saved by Gian's earlier version keep working**: a fire plan of schema 1.0 is read as it is, and written as 2.0 only when you save it.

## What changes in your reports

- **Nothing changes** in an existing document as long as it holds neither an excerpt nor the annexes.
- **The engine of your projects becomes older**, so Woodcore offers to update it when the project opens, as usual.
- **New projects** get the Appendices section, switched off, in their three documents.
- **Nothing changes** in an existing document unless it holds a fire protection plan: the pages of position plans and of export sheets are byte for byte what they were.
- The words of the fire legend follow the report's language.

## What to do

- **Nothing is required.**
- For an excerpt: the **Annexes** tab, "Add a PDF…", "New excerpt…"; then, in "Document structure", "+ Object" → "Extract from an annex". Switch on the document's **Appendices** section to print the pages.
- **A tip for RFEM.** Export the print report from RFEM's report viewer as a PDF: it carries text and bookmarks. Do not print it through "Microsoft Print to PDF", which produces images only, with no bookmarks.
- **Nothing is required.** For a fire protection plan: the **Fire protection plan** tab, import the document (or take the one already imported for the position plans), tick the page, draw; then "+ Object" → "Fire protection plan" in a section, or `#show-fire-plan("id")`.
