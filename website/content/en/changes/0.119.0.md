# 0.119 — Tension checks written out in full; schemas as Typst figures

## New — a project laid out by folders

- **A new project is laid out by folders**, the same in every language:
  - `000-Typst-Config`: `config.typ`, `commands.typ`, `title.typ`, `references.bib` and the form (`Template`);
  - `000-Woodcore`: what is Woodcore's — the export, the engine, the sources and the archives;
  - `100-Service criteria agreement`, `200-Basis of design`, `300-Statical report`: the three documents;
  - `Domains`: your domains; `Positions`: the positions and a `Positions.xlsx` written again at every change;
  - one folder per kind of domain: `Variable-loads/Snow`, `Variable-loads/Wind`, `Permanent-loads`, `Timber-checks/Tension`…, `Steel-checks/…`, `Connectors`, `Connections`, `Scripts`, `Plans/Position-plan`, `Plans/Fire-plan`, `Statics`, `Details`, `Appendix`.
- **Every domain has its Typst file.** A snow load `neige` is printed by `#include "/Variable-loads/Snow/neige.typ"`; complete the file in VS Code, it is yours. An unfolded tension check is written in `Timber-checks/Tension/<id>.typ`.
- **The «Formulas» tab is «Domains».** It also lists the unfolded checks, in groups of their own («Tension» …).
- **As an existing project opens, Woodcore offers to lay it out by folders.** Nothing is deleted; the three documents are compared before and after, and everything is undone at the slightest difference.

- **Imported plans are copied into the project.** A PDF, an image or a DWG imported in «Position plan» is copied into `Plans/Position-plan/Imported Plans` (`Plans/Fire-plan/Imported Plans` for the fire plan); the original is not touched. Replace that file by one of the same name: Woodcore reads it again when the tab is opened and at the export. Importing a file of the same name with another content again asks for your consent; the plans drawn on it then follow it.
- **The export's figures lie in their tab's folder.** Plans, fire plans, static models, schemas, connections, connectors and dead loads: their SVGs and plan backgrounds are in `<folder>/Exports/Figures`, the one copy, read by Typst; `000-Woodcore/data` keeps the JSON alone. A file you put there is never deleted.
- **The annexes are in `Appendix`**, under their PDF's name; their excerpts in `Appendix/Exports`. A PDF replaced by a file of the same name is read again and every excerpt looked for in it again.

## New — copy and paste between plans

- **Several objects selected.** In «Position plan» and «Fire-protection plan», Ctrl+click adds an object to the selection or takes it out; Ctrl+A selects everything drawn but the building outline. «Move», «Delete», «Copy» and «Duplicate» act on the whole selection.
- **Copy from one plan to another.** Ctrl+C asks for a base point to click; open another plan (the ground floor after the basement, say), Ctrl+V: the objects follow the pointer and are put down at the click, their base point there. Ctrl+Z takes them back at once.
- **New numbers.** Every element pasted takes the next number of its prefix on the target plan's storey (S201, S202… on the 2nd floor) and keeps the section of the position copied.
- Between two plans of different scales, the objects are scaled to the target plan. A building outline pasted (chosen by hand, never by Ctrl+A) replaces the plan's. What the target plan cannot hold is left out, and Woodcore says so.
- A position plan is never pasted on a fire-protection plan, nor the reverse. The «Copy» button is unchanged.

## What changes in your reports (folders)

- Nothing they print. Every line `#import "/commands.typ": *` becomes `#import "/000-Typst-Config/commands.typ": *`.

## New

- **A tension check is written out in full in the report.** When it is first saved, Woodcore writes it into `Formules/<id>.typ` (for instance `Formules/ZT-01.typ`), a domain of the «Formulas» tab:
  - the member's line, every input value (force, section, η_w, η_t, the value of SIA 265 Table 8 or 9), every `#eq` with its clause, the `#verify` and the utilisation in %;
  - you read and edit every line in VS Code as in Woodcore: a table, a sentence or a line of calculation is added as anywhere else.
- **A value can no longer age unseen.** Every value written carries where it comes from: `#var("N_t_d", 25, "kN", domain: ("check", "ZT-01"))`. Where the export says otherwise, the report prints it in red with «stale: 28 kN in the export». At the export, Woodcore refreshes a value you did not touch itself.
- **What you change in VS Code comes back into Woodcore.** In the «Formulas» tab, the card «Unfolded check» lists the lines changed outside Woodcore, with «Import» or «Import all»: a force or a section changed goes back into the «Tension» tab; a value of the norm becomes your own (in orange, with «Take back»); a line taken out is «Reinserted»; where the angle changes the form of the calculation, «Regenerate» writes it again and keeps your lines.
- **The detail schemas are Typst figures.** `schema("D-01")` gives the drawing; the figure, its caption and its label are written as any figure is: `#figure(schema("D-01"), kind: image, caption: [Post connection]) <fig-d-01>`, cited by `@fig-d-01`. «Copy» in the «Schemas» tab copies that figure.
- **Your layout stays.** Where Woodcore changes a line of a «Formulas» domain (the tab, an export, an import), it replaces only the value or the option that changed: a command over two lines and your comments stay as you wrote them.
- **From the command line**, `woodcore-cli unfold status|import|reinsert|take-back|regenerate|migrate` does what the tab does.

## What changes in your reports

- When the project is opened, Woodcore rewrites once, keeping a copy of each file in `_Archiv/unfold-migration-<date>/`:
  - `#show-check-member("ZT-01")` of a tension becomes `#include "/Formules/ZT-01.typ"`. The command's options (`level:`, `title:`, `unit:`…) are now set line by line in the file;
  - `#val(…, domain: "ZT-01-verify-tension")` becomes `domain: "ZT-01"`;
  - `#show-schema(…)` becomes `#figure(schema(…), kind: image, caption: […]) <fig-…>`, the caption it printed written out.
- A tension is printed line by line, each value on its line with its citation on the right. The verdict and η are the same.
- Compression, bending, shear, buckling, lateral torsional buckling and tension in the fire situation are printed as before.

## New — commands inside a line

- **A command written in the middle of a line of a domain is read.** In a `Formules/<id>.typ` file edited by hand, `#var`, `#eq`, `#var-unit` and `#verify` may follow a text or a list item (`- $0°$ bis $30°$: #eq(…)`) or be followed by a text (`#var(…) --- Auflast`). Woodcore computes them, and when the «Formulas» tab saves the domain again, each command stays on its line, in its list item. In the middle pane it shows as a pill in its sentence.

## What changes in your reports (commands inside a line)

- Nothing. A domain that could no longer be read («Export failed») exports again, and the report prints what it printed.
