# 0.88 — Checking timber members after SIA 265

Published on 23.09.2026. The checks of SIA 265, chapter 4.2 — tension, compression, buckling, bending, lateral torsional buckling, shear — are written straight in the report, with `#member` and eight `verify-…` commands.

## New: a member described once, checked by its name

`#member` writes a member down: its section, strength class and moisture class are read from the position of the same name in `Positionen.xlsx`, and the design values follow from them. An argument replaces what the position says, or describes a member that is not a position:

```typ
#member("S001")
#member("R1", b: 120, h: 240, grade: "GL24h", moisture-class: 1)
```

Each check then names the member and gives the actions:

| Command | Checks | SIA 265 |
|---|---|---|
| [`verify-tension`](~/docs/verify-tension/) | tension, parallel, perpendicular or at an angle to the grain | 4.2.1 |
| [`verify-compression`](~/docs/verify-compression/) | compression parallel to the grain, then buckling about both axes | 4.2.2.1, 4.2.8 |
| [`verify-compression-perp`](~/docs/verify-compression-perp/) | compression perpendicular or at an angle — a support, a plate | 4.2.2.2, 4.2.2.3 |
| [`verify-bending`](~/docs/verify-bending/) | bending, then lateral torsional buckling | 4.2.3, 4.2.9 |
| [`verify-bending-normal`](~/docs/verify-bending-normal/) | bending with a normal force, then buckling or lateral torsional buckling | 4.2.4 |
| [`verify-shear`](~/docs/verify-shear/) | shear and torsion | 4.2.5 |
| [`verify-shear-off`](~/docs/verify-shear-off/) | shear over a sheared area (Abscheren) | 4.2.6 |
| [`verify-shear-perp`](~/docs/verify-shear-perp/) | shear with a stress perpendicular to the grain | 4.2.7.1 |


## How to write them

- **The normal force is signed, tension positive**: `N: -40` is a compression of 40 kN. It chooses the equation: `verify-tension` refuses a compression.
- **An action is written** as a number (kN, kNm, mm), a value with its unit (`N: "40 kN"`), the name of a variable declared above with `#var` or `#eq`, or `var-of(…)`.
- **Lateral torsional buckling needs a length**: `a:` (the buckling length) or `sigma-m-crit:`. Without one, the page says in orange that it is not checked.
- **Two parts**, «Tragsicherheit» then «Stabilität», each with its clause. Level 1 prints the utilisations, level 2 the stresses and the standard's relation, level 3 every formula in numbers.
- **The results stay named** in `"<member>-verify-<check>"`: `#val("eta", domain: "S001-verify-compression")` quotes the utilisation further on in the text. `unit: "%"` prints it in percent.


## What changes in your reports

- **Separating dashes are long** everywhere: headings, captions, notes («Biegung mit Druck — S001»). Ranges keep a short dash («1–4»).
- **A table is cited without §**: «SIA 265 Tabelle 8».
- In `#verify`, a number written with its unit keeps it: `eta <= 100 "%"` prints «100 %».
