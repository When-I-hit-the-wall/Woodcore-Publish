# 0.99 — The bibliography, the revision journal, and drawings you set yourself

This version also installs 0.97 and 0.98, which had no installer of their own: the air above a table's head (see the 0.97 page) and the reading of an RFEM 6 model from its own table export. What follows is what 0.99 adds.

## New

- **A bibliography at the end of the report.** `#show-bibliography()` prints, in three blocks — norms, literature, other documents — the designation, the full title, the edition and the publisher of every document the report actually cited.
  - Nothing changes in the right-hand column: «SIA 265 §4.2.1» stays exactly what it was. The citation is simply *registered* as it is made.
  - The engine knows thirteen documents (SIA 260, 261, 261/1, 263, 265, 265/1, SN EN 14592, SZS C5, STE Compact 02, bauforumstahl's Arbeitshilfe and the VKF directives 13-15, 14-15 and 15-15), every field read off the edition's own title page.
  - Your own documents — a manufacturer's approval, a course handout — go into a `references.bib` beside the report: `#show-bibliography(project: "references.bib")`.
  - `#cite-norm(norm: "VKF 14-15", norm-ref: "2.5.3")` cites a norm in the prose and registers it.
- **A revision journal.** A **Journal** tab (group Project) where you write, by hand and one per revision: the index, the date, your name, the office's short form, a label, a short description and a long one.
  - `#show-journal(form: "table")`, `form: "list"` or `form: "grid"`. The level governs the three texts alone: the number, the date, the name and the short form are printed at every level.
  - Your own words are never translated.
  - The PDF's properties name the latest revision, «Rev. 3 (2026-09-25)».
- **Every entry of a drawing has a thickness of its own.** In **Position plan**, **Static models** and **Schemas**, the selected entry carries a card of sliders — strokes, symbols, texts, from 25 % to 300 % — which **multiply** the drawing's own three.
  - An entry that sets nothing follows the drawing. The moment it sets a factor, the row turns orange with **«Take back»**, as an overridden b/h does.
  - **Schemas** gains the drawing's three sliders on the way, which it never had.
  - A load that sets its stroke factor also gets a longer arrow: that is what makes it stand out among its neighbours.
- **A schema's texts stand on a box of their own**, slightly translucent, instead of being surrounded by a cloud-like halo. And **a construction line breaks off** at the labels it crosses — a dimension line under its own value — in the figure and in the window alike.
- **Summary tables.** `#show-load-summary()` every area load in kN/m², `#show-line-load-summary()` the line loads in kN/m, `#show-result-summary()` the main results and an overview of the checks with their η and their ✔.
  - All of it grouped by domain, one bold row per group.
  - All three read the **whole document**: you may put them at the head of the report and a value computed fifty pages further down will be in them.
  - A load the export does not carry — a line load, a load read off a catalogue — enters with `#var(..., summary: true)` or `#eq(..., summary: true)`, and **its unit decides which table**.

## What changes in your reports

- **No number changes, and no page already printed moves.** That was the condition set: it is held by compiling the same text with and without each new thing, and by re-rendering the 293 example pages, of which only 8 moved — the eight detail schemas, whose labels gained their box.
- **Your schemas** are therefore the only drawings to look different: the cloud-like halo becomes a box, and a construction line breaks off under a label. Plans and static models are identical to the byte as long as no entry sets a factor.
- A misspelt `style` key in a plan, which was silently ignored, is now refused naming what a factor may be called.

## To do

- **Nothing is compulsory.** The five additions are additions; a report that does not call them is the report it was.
- For the bibliography, add `#show-bibliography()` at the end of your report — the project template puts it in `Sections/007-literatur.typ` — and write your own documents into a `references.bib` beside the report.
- For the journal, open the **Journal** tab and enter your first revision; the project template prints the journal last, in `Sections/008-journal.typ`. *File → Settings* moves it forward.
- For the summaries, call `#show-load-summary()` where you want them, and mark the loads you write by hand with `summary: true`.
