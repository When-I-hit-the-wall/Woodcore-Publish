# 0.113 — Headings at their real level, an editable title page, settings brought together, and loading that no longer freezes the window

## New

- **Headings are written at the level they print at.** In the service criteria agreement and the basis of design, a sub-section writes `== Projektspezifische Unterlagen` and no longer `=`: what you read in VS Code is what prints. **Every chapter has a file of its own** (`Sections/<chapter>.typ`), whose title — `= Grundlagen und Dokumente` — is changed by hand or in Woodcore, like any heading. The entry file now only includes the files, in the structure's order.
- **A shared section keeps each document's own title**: the title stands before the shared block, since one section need not lie at the same level in the agreement and in the basis. The block's text stays the same.
- **Choices and checklists print only what is chosen.** Seismic zone, ground class, structure class…: the page shows the answer, the right pane offers every option. `#doc-choice` and `#doc-checklist` now print the answer alone by default (`only-selected: true`, `only-checked: true`); `false` brings back every option with its box.
- **A «Whole document» row at the top of the tree**, with no box and without indenting the rest: one click shows the whole document.
- **Taking an object out no longer asks anything.** It stays visible in the tree, struck through, where it stood; a right click **puts it back right after the closing tag of the object before it** (even if you wrote something in between meanwhile), or forgets it. Its copy stays in `Sections/archives/`.
- **The title page is set in a file `title.typ`** at the top of the project, editable in VS Code: authors, client, architect, addresses… on one line, in bold or one per line, and rows of your own. It serves the three documents and is written in each one's language. With the BFH template **all three documents** have the BFH title page — logo, colours, department — and no longer the report alone, each in its document's language instead of French everywhere.
- **Settings brought together.** One «Templates» tab with a switch Service criteria agreement / Basis of design / Statical report, and three places: **Builtin**, **Shared** and **My templates**, into which **a folder can be imported**. A **Save** button, left of «Close», saves everything and closes. Templates are no longer created or edited by hand in the settings: they are saved from a project.
- **A loading indicator** in the middle of the window while a project opens, a document is switched, a project is created or exported: the window no longer freezes, tooltips still show on hover, but clicks are held meanwhile.
- **Recent projects that cannot be found disappear** from the start page instead of staying greyed.
- **«Convert an old folder» leaves the start page**: it only served folders laid out before Woodcore. `woodcore-cli convert <folder>` still does the same.

## What changes in your reports

- **A project made before 0.113 keeps its `=` headings** and its old entry file: the «Document structure» tab shows a banner **«Write the headings at their real level…»**. One click converts the agreement and the basis together; the printed PDF does not change, only the files do.
- **Choices and checklists in your documents print only the answer.** To show every option again, add `only-selected: false` or `only-checked: false` to the object.
- **Your projects' engine becomes older**: Woodcore offers to update it when the project opens.
- **A new project has a `title.typ` file.** An existing project keeps its title page until you copy that file in.

## What to do

- **Accept the engine update** when Woodcore offers it.
- **Click «Write the headings at their real level…»** in the «Document structure» tab of an existing project, then read the PDF through.
- **Fill in `title.typ`** of a new project: every row marked `EDIT HERE` is yours.
- **Keep your templates** in «My templates» or the office's shared folder, and import into it the folders you kept elsewhere.
