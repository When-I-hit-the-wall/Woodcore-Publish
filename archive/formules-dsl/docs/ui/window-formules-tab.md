# The «Formules» tab in docs/ui/window.md (§10quater), as it stood when it was retired (0.117)

**The «Formules» tab (v0.100).** *Formules* (group *Référence*, before *Commandes*; `calc/tools/dsl_tab.py`, its words under `dsl_`) is the keyword lines of §3.3septdecies: three panes — what he writes, the Typst it becomes (coloured, read-only) and **the page that Typst compiles to**, which is his own requirement. Under the first pane the grammar's own words, so it is read by being read; under the second the lines that were not understood, each with its number, and what a check needs and the block does not state. A level selector recompiles at 1, 2 or 3; *Copier le Typst* copies; *Insérer dans une section…* names the section, shows what will be appended and writes only on a yes. Ctrl+Return translates. ⚠️ The page is compiled **from standard input against the project's own report folder** as the Typst root, so `#member("T001")` reads the project's real positions and nothing is written into the project to render a preview.
