- *[v0.99 · 2026-09-29]* **What the journal does not do yet** (v0.99, §3.3terdecies). None is blocked on a decision.
  - **Nothing links a revision to what it changed.** A revision says what was done in the engineer's own words; no position, no drawing, no section is named, and nothing compares two exports. The archive of every export (§12) is what holds the state a revision was made on, and tying the two — an archive named by the revision it carries — is the obvious next step and was not asked for.
  - **The journal's page is last**, after the literature, and the engineer moves it forward himself (§13). A template could carry it where its author wants it, which is one line of its `Statischer Rapport.typ`.
  - **Two entries may spell one office two ways** («FLL», «FL»): the short form is typed at every entry (the engineer's decision) and nothing notices. A list of the short forms already used, offered in the field, is a combo box away.
  - **A revision cannot be marked as issued**: there is no «what this PDF was» beyond the newest entry, so a PDF compiled between two revisions carries the older one's keyword. A revision that named the archive it was exported into would close that.

Three shapes were set out and weighed: *(a)* nothing in the data, the position named in a comment beside the `show-*` call; *(b)* a `positions` field on the **calculation** — never on the position, so `Positionen.xlsx` stays untouched and §8 holds — which would make a completeness loop possible (`for id in position-ids()` … *no check on S001*); *(c)* a `domains` block on the position, the original idea of this section, which is dead and rightly so.

The engineer chose **(a)**, and judged **(b) unlikely to be implemented at all**, for a reason worth recording because it is about the *shape of the work* rather than about the mechanism: there are so many loads, so many combinations of them and so many checks that may come into play that being explicit in Excel about every action a calculation concerns would be very complicated — the statement would cost more to keep true than the completeness loop it buys is worth. What a calculation concerns is therefore a **comment beside the call**, which is prose and stays prose.

So this section stays open only in the narrow sense that `domains` is still `{}` and nothing populates it. It is not waiting on a decision about attribution: that one is taken.
