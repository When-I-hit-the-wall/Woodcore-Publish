# The level of detail -1, «Sélection manuelle», in the window and the docs

> Moved verbatim on 2026-10-22 by the spec 2026-10-22-retrait-niveau-manuel (issue #93): the paragraphs that described the level of detail -1 -- `level: -1, shown: (…)`, retired from the engine by the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (lot 19) and from the window by this spec -- as current. Each is under the file and the place it was taken from; what replaced it is said there.

## From `docs/ui/window.md`

**The level of detail -1 in «Affichage» (v0.117, issue #20).** Where a command takes `shown:` -- the member checks' `show-check-member`, `show-connector-resistance`, `show-calc` (connectors, polar moments, the variables' loads), `show-polar-moment` --, its level is a `display_view.LevelControl` (display_options kind `detail`, `also=("shown",)`): auto, the levels the command accepts, and **-1 «Sélection manuelle»**, under which every variable the object can print stands with its level as **four joined buttons `0 | 1 | 2 | 3`** (`chrome.SegmentedSwitch`, its `soft` variant: the button down lightly set apart in `accent-soft`), the variables **side by side as the pane's width allows** (`flowbar.FlowLayout`) -- 0 the variable left out of `shown:`; until issue #41 a row each, a box and a drop-down, the box unticked for 0. The engineer never types a name: the rows are what the tab hands over in `Entry.context["shown"]` -- lib/shown-variables.json's keys for the polar moment, the export's vars and tables for a connector or a load (`shown_variables.export_choices`, a var by its symbol, a table by its title), a saved check's own lines for the member checks (`member_check.shown_keys`) --, and a level chosen stays where its key is still offered; a check's verdicts are lines like the others, each named by the key of its utilisation (`eta`, `eta_s`, `eta_y`…) and shown by its row's symbol (`member_check.shown_choices`), `0 | 1 | 2 | 3` (issue #58, which retired the result of issue #39 locked ticked at `1 | 2 | 3`). The call writes `level: -1, shown: (…)`, `(:)` with nothing ticked. -1 is offered where the command's signature has `shown`, and 0 only where the command takes it: `show-calc`, `show-input`, `show-live-load`, `show-connector-resistance` and `show-polar-moment` refuse 0, and offering it made their preview fail. The member checks' own panel puts the same control in place of its level box, keeps its options by value through a rebuild, and greys its member line's level at -1, the one list being the member's too. 

## From `docs/ui/tab-member-checks.md`

 **At -1 the check's verdicts are offered beside its lines**, by the symbol of their row (η, η_s, η_c,y…), `0 | 1 | 2 | 3` like them (issue #58, `member_check.shown_choices`; issue #39's stress locked ticked is retired); a choice of the manual selection survives a draft a moment not calculable.

## From `docs/domain/member-checks.md`

⚠️ **Level -1 (v0.117, issue #20).** `#member`, the five checks a saved check calls -- `verify-tension`, `verify-compression`, `verify-compression-perp`, `verify-bending`, `verify-bending-normal` -- and `#show-check-member` take `level: -1, shown: (A: 2, sigma_c_0_d: 3)`: each variable named printed as a line of its level, none of the others; the title, the position's line, the parts that hold a verdict and the verdicts as at level 1; «Knicken um die y-Achse», «… z-Achse» and «Kippen», printed from level 2 only, where a key under them is chosen. `show-check-member` takes one list: a key its check prints goes to the check, the rest to the member, whose `member-level` gives way. The keys are lib/shown-variables.json's (docs/typst/report-engine.md §4); every `eq` of the checks passes `note: "line"` through `timber-eq`, so `#eq`'s inline default (v0.117) moved no line of a check.

## From `docs/domain/member-checks.md`

**A check's verdict is a key of -1 (issue #58, which retires issue #39's result).** The result of a check is its verdict -- the `verify` line, the ratio against 1 --, not its stress. At -1 each verdict is named in `shown:` by the key of its utilisation, `eta` (tension, compression perpendicular, shear), `eta_s` (the section), `eta_y`, `eta_z` (buckling), `eta_m` (lateral torsional buckling), `eta_m_t`, `eta_m_c` (the two sides at an angle), listed among the command's keys in `lib/shown-variables.json`: at 1 it prints its ratio and value, at 2 or 3 its numbers too, and nothing where `shown:` leaves it out, like any key (D-02). The stresses are ordinary keys. The side headings of a bending at an angle stand over their verdict where it is at 2 or 3. `_compute` gives the section's verdict along the grain its own row `eta_s` beside `eta`, as Typst stores it, so the window offers what the page prints (`member_check.shown_keys`, `shown_choices`; `test_member_check` holds the two equal). D-12 of issue #20 holds again for the member checks.

## From `docs/typst/report-engine.md`

**A per-call `level:` may be -1 since v0.117, which prints what `shown:` names** (issue #20; spec 2026-10-10-issues-github-19-20-21): `level: -1, shown: (A: 2, W_y: 1)` prints the variables it names, each as a line of the level it gives it, and none of the others — the title and the verdict as at level 1, a heading printed from level 2 only where one of its keys is chosen, a figure where a key is chosen at 2 or 3, a result not chosen not at all (D-09, D-12). It is taken by `member`, the five `verify-*` the member checks call, `show-check-member` (one list for the member and its check, a key both print being the check's, D-11), `show-calc` (the window offering it in the three load tabs too since issues #56, #57), `show-connector-resistance` and `show-polar-moment`. **What a command may name is written once**, in `lib/shown-variables.json` — every key it can print, in its order, axes written out (`k_c_y`, `k_c_z`), and the keys under each heading printed from level 2 only —, read by `lib/calc-engine.typ` (`json("shown-variables.json")`) to refuse a key a command does not print, naming those it does, and by `calc/shown_variables.py` for the boxes the window ticks; it travels with the engine (`project.ENGINE_FILES`). `show-calc`'s keys are the instance's own, its vars and its tables (`table-1`, `table-2`); `show-polar-moment`'s are groups of lines (`positions`, `centre`, `theta`, `n`, `I_p`, `forces`, `components`, `F_x_i`, `F_z_i`, `F_max`, `beta`, `forces_table`, `worst_table`). The list is a superset: a key a call does not print for its arguments — α = 0, a round section, no stability — is accepted and ignored. **One place decides the level a line prints at** — `level-plan`, `plan-at`, `plan-row`, `plan-part`, `plan-any` beside `resolve-level`, the only `-1` of lib/ being `selection-level` —, and the value of every line is computed and stored at every level as before (`calc/variables.py` reads the same registry). Like 0, -1 is a level of **one call and never of a report**: `resolve-level` hands it back as it stands, `--input uniform=N` still forces its level over it, and `report-level.update(-1)` is refused. `shown:` with any other level, an unknown key and a level outside 1–3 in it are refused.
