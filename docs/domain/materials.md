# Materials: the engineer's library and the ones Woodcore ships

> The library of materials for every project of the engineer's computer, the materials Woodcore ships read at their source, the design values deduced, and the copy a project keeps. The tab that shows them is `ui/tab-materials.md`.
>
> Issue #110, spec `docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-24-onglet-materiaux.md` (v0.125). Replaces the three libraries of a build-up of D-07 (issue #35, §3.3ter): docs/decisions.md §13, «The materials are the engineer's library and the ones Woodcore ships».

## 3.3sexvicies Materials

**What a material is** (`calc/domains/materials_contract.py`, ARCH-010). One shape for every material, written once: an `id`, a `type` -- `member` (a bar: solid timber, glulam, LVL), `panel` (a wood-based panel) or `other` (nothing load-bearing: a weight only) --, `names` in fr, de and en (one at least, shown in the window's language, the first there is otherwise), one of eleven `category`s (wood, boards, insulation, roofing, waterproofing and membranes, gypsum and plaster, concrete and masonry, steel metals and glass, floor finishes, fills and ballast, typed loads), a `comment`, a `weight` {value, unit kN/m³ or kN/m², ref} -- optional, what a layer of a permanent load is weighed with --, a `source` {kind none | url | pdf | norm | book, url, file, ref}. A member adds its `family` (softwood, hardwood, glulam) and its `values`: the six strengths f_m, f_t,0, f_c,0, f_t,90, f_c,90, f_v, each `_k` and `_d`, then E_0,mean, E_90,mean, G_mean and ρ_k. A panel adds its `product` (a row of SIA 265/1 Tableau 9), `isotropic`, its `columns` -- one per range of thickness, `[]` for one --, an optional `thickness`, `rho_k` per column and its `grid` {plate | shear | axial: {key: {par | perp | all: [a value per column]}}}, the shape of SIA 265/1 Tableaux 11 to 23. `check_material` holds every rule, naming the file and the field.

**Where they come from.**

| Origin | Id | Read from | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| SIA 265:2021 Tabellen 8, 9 | `sia265:<class>` | `norms/sia265.grades()` | the 11 strength classes, their design values the tables' (`f_c,90,d` the general value) |
| SIA 265/1:2018 Tableaux 11 to 23 | `sia265-1:<panel>` | `norms/sia265_1.panels()` | 20 panels: plywood F25/10 to F60/10 (3 plies and at least 5 apart), particle boards P4 to P7, cement-bonded, OSB/2, OSB/3, OSB/4, HB, MBH, MDF.LA, MDF.HLS -- characteristic (a) and design (b) values both the norm's |
| SIA 261:2020 Tabellen 28 to 30 | `sia261:<row>` | `norms/sia261` | every row with a value (94); Tabelle 29's «per 10 mm» gravel read as the volume weight it is |
| the workbook's library | `ext:<row>` | `calc/libraries/materials-library.json` | «Externe»: listed merged by name and weight under the first row's id, every row an id of its own (a layer naming a row the view merged is still weighed) |
| the engineer's library | `u-<8 hex>` | `%APPDATA%\Woodcore\materials\materials.json` | «Personnel», his own |

The shipped materials are assembled at their source by `materials_catalogue.py`: no value is copied. **Their names and categories** are `calc/libraries/materials-catalogue.json`, which holds no number -- the French names of SIA 261's rows read on the French edition (pp. 77-78), their German names the norm files' own --, and **the weight of a material of resistance** is a line it names: a class weighs SIA 261's line of its family (softwood, hardwood, glued softwood), a particle board SIA 261's, OSB the workbook's 680 kg/m³ (Kronospan) and plywood and cement-bonded boards the workbook's. ρ_k (resistance, connections) and the weight (loads) are two fields, two sources (T46). MDF and fibreboards have no weight: the tab says so.

**SIA 265/1** (`calc/norms/sia265_1.py`): Tableau 6 (γ_M per group), Tableau 9 (η_mod per product, moisture class and KLED; «na» null), Tableaux 11 to 23, one file each, read off `norm-library/SIA265-1-2018-FR.pdf` -- the one edition shipped. Every design value of a b table is held to η_mod · f_k / γ_M of its a table within the norm's rounding (`tests/test_sia265_1.py`), so a misread cell of either half shows. A cell the norm marks «valeurs non disponibles» is null and listed in the panel's `unavailable`. Plaques de plâtre fibrées: γ_M «à déterminer selon le produit», left null, never guessed.

**The design values** (`materials_derive.py`, REQ-008). A design value typed always wins. One left empty is, for a member, **set by the norm** for shear and tension perpendicular to the grain -- SIA 265 3.3.2.2 for solid timber (f_v,d 1.5 / 1.8, f_t,90,d 0.1 / 0.2, softwood / hardwood), 3.4.2.2 for glulam (1.8, 0.15; 3.4.2.4 repeats them for universal finger joints), `calc/norms/sia265-2021-3.3.2.2-…json` and `-3.4.2.2-…json`, held equal to Tabellen 8 and 9's classes --, else **deduced**: f_d = f_k / (γ_M/η_M), SIA 265 (3) and Tabelle 1, η_t = η_w = 1; for a panel f_d = η_mod · f_k / γ_M, SIA 265/1 Tableaux 6 and 9, moisture class 1, medium duration. Rounded half up to 0.1 N/mm² (`round_tenth`, decimal), as the norm prints. Checked: C24 24 → 14.1, 21 → 12.4; GL24h 24 → 16.0; OSB/3 18 → 10.5, 10 → 5.8, 1.0 → 0.6. **The limit** (T36): for a class outside the tables the general rule does not always give a table's value -- f_c,90,k 2.5 gives 1.5 where Tabelle 8 prints 1.8 for C24 -- and Woodcore applies the norm, the rule's words saying so. η_w and η_t are the check's, as they are for Tabellen 8 and 9.

**The state and the uses** (REQ-009, REQ-010). `status(m, others, lang)` says, as codes the tab words: complete; an example not checked; no weight (missing from the permanent loads' choice); n strengths missing -- a strength lacks when neither its characteristic nor its design value (typed, set or deduced) is there, a modulus and ρ_k when empty; no source (the engineer's own); a name another of his materials carries (red); no thickness, n values the norm does not give, n design values deduced or set (information). `available_in(m)`: the permanent loads need a weight; the **member checks** read a member complete in what their four tabs read (f_m, f_t,0, f_c,0, f_v -- a class of SIA 265 or one of the engineer's own) and a panel giving one of their resistances (issue #112, REQ-012: `member_check_material.usable_in_checks`, docs/domain/member-checks.md §3.3unvicies); the connectors a class of SIA 265, a member of the engineer's own «to come» there; plates and diaphragms are to come.

**The search** (`materials_search.py`, REQ-011): every word typed must be found (AND), without case or accents, in the names in three languages, the category's three names and words, the type's. Shared by the «Matériaux» tab and the permanent loads' choice of a material.

**The engineer's library** (`materials_user.py`, REQ-001 to REQ-003, REQ-012). `usersettings.materials_dir()`: `%APPDATA%\Woodcore\materials\`, `WOODCORE_MATERIALS` elsewhere, and beside the settings file when only `WOODCORE_SETTINGS` is set -- a test run, so the suite never writes the engineer's (`tests/conftest.py` removes it at exit). `materials.json` (`schemas/user-materials.schema.json` 1.0) is checked before every write, written whole (`engine.write_json`), read again just before (two windows lose nothing); a file that cannot be read stops naming it and the field and is never written over. An id is drawn once and kept by a rename; a shipped material is refused. A PDF chosen is **copied** into `sources/` under a clean name (`-2` for another file of that name, the same copy for the same bytes), and removed with the last material naming it.

**The copy a project keeps** (`materials_project.py`, REQ-013, REQ-014, D-11). `Woodcore-Datei/materials.json` 2.0 (`schemas/materials.schema.json`, 1.0 still read) holds a whole copy of every material of the engineer's own a case names, its PDF copied into `Woodcore-Datei/materials/`; the project computes from the copy alone, alike on another computer. A shipped material is not copied: it is read from the Woodcore that computes. **The cases that name a material** are the build-ups of the permanent loads and, since issue #112, the member checks (`used_ids` reads `material: "u-…"` of their sources; `member_check.export` calls `sync` before it computes, REQ-002). The copy is taken once, at the save of a case, between the case written and the export (`SourceStore.save` calls the domain's `before_export`, `dead_load_source.before_export` → `sync`); a copy no case names any more is removed. A draft names a material not yet copied: it is weighed from the library until its save (`for_calculation`). **«The library has changed»**: the copy is compared field by field with the library's material -- a value of the calculation (orange) or a word (only said) --, the values named before → after; «Reprendre la nouvelle version» replaces the copy, «Garder la copie» writes the fingerprint of the version refused (`kept_fingerprint`, SHA-256 of its calculation fields) so that the warning is not said again until the library changes once more; a material the library lacks -- a project from another computer -- is offered «Ajouter à ma bibliothèque». Numbers are compared with a relative tolerance of 1e-9.

**A layer names a material by its id** (dead-load-source 1.2, `library: "catalogue"`, ARCH-007): written only where a layer names one; `sia261`, `belastungsannahmen` and `project` stay read for the layers of before. `materials.weight` reads a shipped material or the project's copy.

**The project's materials of before** (`materials_migrate.py`, REQ-015, D-15). A 1.0 file -- the weights typed in «Poids propre» until v0.125 -- is poured into the engineer's library when the project is opened, **the library first** (each entry an `other` in kN, or the one already there of the same name and weight, weights compared in kN), **then the project**, where it is writable: the file at 2.0 with the copies, the layers that named an old id rewritten to `catalogue` and the new id, every file saved first into the archives (`commands_migrate.commit`). Read only, the project is left as it was and keeps reading its 1.0 file. A notice names what was poured. A second opening does nothing.
