# Steel members (SIA 263:2013, SZS C5)

> The steel profiles Woodcore knows (the SZS C5 tables and the engineer's free profiles), the cross-section class, and the member checks of SIA 263 computed once in Python and printed by `#show-check-steel`.

### 3.3quatervicies Steel sections and member checks (issues #50, #51, #52; v0.118)

The engineer, 12.10.2026: «une section d'onglets "Acier"», from the steel course (001-Stahl-Graber) and the reference steel workbooks, «également toutes les valeurs extraites du livre C5» (spec `docs/requirements/active/2026-10-12-section-acier.md`). Delivered in three lots (D-08): **lot 1** — profiles, section class, tension, compression of the section, bending with a normal force, shear; lot 2 — buckling, lateral torsional buckling, the N–M interaction; lot 3 — bolts, welds, splices. Out of scope: steel in fire, EN 1993 (D-07: SIA 263:2013 only).

**The profile tables** are SZS C5:2018's (*Stahlbautabellen*), transcribed once from the scanned edition `ExternalFiles/000-Normes/SZS-C5-2021.pdf` into fifteen files `calc/norms/szs-c5-2018-profile-<family>.json` (IPE, IPN, UPE, UPN, HEA, HEB, HEM, HD/HL, T, L, RND, VKT, ROR, RRK, RRW: 1038 profiles). Each file carries `_source`, the edition, `columns` (each with its unit and the factor to mm) and `families` → `rows` → the printed values, in the order and the units the book prints them. **The book is the source; the reference *Hilfstabelle* is the control** (D-04): `tests/fixtures/steel-reference-profiles.json` holds the reference values, `tests/test_steel_profiles.py` compares every one of them with the tables at the book's printed rounding, and the disagreements are listed in the fixture with the page that settles them (the reference UPE root radius, UPN 260 A_v, the angles' i_y, …). The eight values where the book is itself inconsistent with its own mechanics (HD 320x74 I_z, IPN 550 i_z, ROR 610x60 W_pl, …) are corrected in `szs_c5.ANOMALIES`, each with the value recomputed and the reason, and the check says so in a Note (`steel_c5_anomaly`). `calc/norms/szs_c5.py` reads them: `sizes(code)`, `profile(code, dims)` (plain mm units; a symmetric family's I, W and i copied to both axes; T sections from their parent, IPET → IPE), `check_tables()` in `check_all`.

**SIA 263's values** are read off `SIA263-2013-DE.pdf` into `sia263-2013-3.2.2-kennwerte.json` (E, G, ν, ρ_a), `sia263-2013-4.1.3-widerstandsbeiwerte.json` (γ_M1 = 1.05, γ_M2 = 1.25) and `sia263-2013-tab05-schlankheit.json` (Tabelle 5a/5b: the c/t limits of the classes, ε = √(235/f_y)); the grades are Tabelle 1's (`sia263.steel()`), already read by the connectors.

**Free profiles** (D-02, D-03, D-10, D-12): `Woodcore-Datei/profiles/<id>.json`, schema `profile-source` 1.0, a list of **rectangles without fillets** (`parts`: name, y, z, b, h, `role`), each wall with a role for the classification (D-11): `internal` (supported on both edges, Tabelle 5a), `outstand` (one edge, Tabelle 5b), `none` (not classified). `calc/domains/profile.py` builds them from a template (I, U, T, L, RHS, plate) or from a C5 profile without its root radii (`from_c5`), and computes their values: A, the centroid, I_y, I_z, W_el, W_pl with the exact plastic axis, i. A position whose section is `USR` names a free profile by its id (`position` schema 1.5, kind `user`); the export resolves it, and a missing profile is the Note `position_profile_missing`. **A free profile a position or a check cites can be neither renamed nor deleted** (D-16); a deleted one is archived in `profiles/archives/`.

**The steel section** (`calc/domains/steel_section.py`) is what the checks read: the values, the walls (each with c, t, its role and its ends about the centroid, named in German: Steg, Flansch oben links, …) and the shear areas A_v,z, A_v,y. `classify(sec, f_y, N, M_y, M_z)` gives each wall its class after Tabelle 5a/5b from the stress at its ends under the worst sign combination of the forces (a web in bending and compression with α = (1 + N·γ_M1/(c·t·f_y))/2, the reference convention), and the section's class is its worst wall's. **Class 4 is refused** with its reason: the effective section is not built.

**The checks** (`calc/domains/steel_resistance.py`, kinds `class`, `tension`, `compression`, `bending`, `shear`), SIA 263:2013 4.1–5.1:

- tension: (38) N_Rd = f_y·A/γ_M1 and, an A_net given, (39) 0.9·f_u·A_net/γ_M2, the smaller;
- compression of the section (without buckling, lot 2): N_Rd = f_y·A/γ_M1, the class ≤ 3;
- bending: class 1–2 plastic (40) f_y·W_pl/γ_M1, class 3 elastic (52) f_y·W_el/γ_M1; with N, the linear sum (44)/(54) or the reduced resistances (45)–(48) of the plastic class (I: a = (A − 2bt_f)/A ≤ 0.5, α = 2, β = max(5n, 1.1); RHS: α = β = 1.66/(1 − 1.33n²) ≤ 6);
- shear: (41) V_Rd = τ_y·A_v/γ_M1, the web's slenderness (42) (h − t_f)/t_w ≤ √(4E/f_y) — beyond, the Note that shear buckling (4.5.4) is not checked —, and (43)/(53) the moment reduced by the shear above half V_Rd.

**Computed once, in Python** (D-09): each check is a list of rows (`vars`, `steps` with their formula, clause and level, the ratio and the answer) that `data/steel.json` (schema `steel` 1.0) holds and `#show-check-steel` (lib/steel.typ, D-17) prints through `show-calc`; Typst evaluates nothing again. The timber checks' arrangement (the formula in Typst) is thereby reversed for steel, as the connectors' was: decisions.md, «Steel checks are computed once in Python». The ratio is registered as `<id>-verify-steel-<kind>`, so the summary (*Nachweisübersicht*) lists it with the timber checks.

**A check's source** is `Woodcore-Datei/steel/<id>.json`, schema `steel-source` 1.0 (1.1 with `links`): `kind`, the optional `position`, `profile` (null to follow the position, or `{type, dimension}`), `grade` (null to follow the position's strength class, else a grade of Tabelle 1, D-05), the forces `N`, `M_y`, `M_z`, `V_z`, `V_y` as `{value, unit}`, signed, tension positive, and for a tension `A_net`. What the project lacks never stops: `steel_check.resolve` gives `calculable: false` and its reason (Notes `steel_position_missing`, `steel_not_calculable`); a malformed source stops, naming the file and the field. The kinds of lots 2 and 3 are accepted by the schema and refused as «not implemented yet».

**Tests**: `test_steel_profiles` (the tables against the reference extract, the anomalies, the families), `test_steel_free_profile` (the templates and their values, the source, the citations), `test_steel_check` (IPE 360 S355 by hand: class, bending with N, shear; the refusals; the export and `#show-check-steel` compiling), and the window's `test_steel_tab`.
