> External PDFs, excerpts anchored on bookmarks, and the annexes that close a document.
>
> Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md (§3.3undevicies) on 2026-10-07. The § numbers are kept as stable anchors: `docs/README.md` maps every § to its file.

### 3.3undevicies Annexes: documents from outside, and the excerpts the report shows of them (v0.111 on Gian's branch, ported onto the three documents in v0.115)

The engineer: «un endroit dans le Woodcore dans la section Référence au dessus de Formules qui se nomme Annexes. Cette partie va servir d'entrepôt pour des documents externes comme des ETA [...], des fiches techniques de produit [...] ou des rapports d'impression sortis d'un programme de FEM. C'est ce dernier point que l'on va traiter en premier.» An **excerpt** («Extrait», the name he chose over «Aperçu», which the polar moments' «Aperçu des efforts» already uses) is a region of one page of a source — a diagram, a table — shown in the report as a figure, found again in every new version of its source, and linked to the page it comes from in the annexes at the end of the report.

**Source.** Two kinds of file under `Woodcore-Datei/annexes/`, written by Woodcore's «Annexes» tab and read by `calc/domains/annex.py` alone — the arrangement every other editor tab has (§13):

| file | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `sources/<id>.pdf` + `sources/<id>.json` | the PDF, **copied** into the project (his answer B3) -- in a project laid out by folders (v0.119, issue #64, D-11) into `Appendix/` under the name it was added under, which the record names (`file`, annex-source **1.1**, `annex.copy_pdf`; `<id>.pdf` where another source's PDF or a file of the engineer's has that name), a 1.0 record still finding its PDF beside it --, and its record (`schemas/annex-source.schema.json` 1.0): Nom · Description · ID, what the annexes carry of it (`mode`: `used`, the pages an excerpt is taken from and the default; `all`; `none`, his answer E15), the file it was copied from, and its SHA-256. A new version is written **over** it (B4). |
| `excerpts/<id>.json` | an excerpt (`schemas/annex-excerpt.schema.json` 1.0): its source, its **anchor** and its **frame**, in points — x from the page's left edge, y **down** from the anchor's height, w and h — drawn by the engineer, the title included or not as he chooses (C12). |

**The anchor is a bookmark of the source, and that was measured rather than assumed.** RFEM's own PDF export («Visionneuse du rapport», DynaPDF 4.0) draws every heading as vector paths: on a page of diagrams pypdf and QtPdf read the numbers inside the diagrams and **not one character of the titles**, so the title cannot be selected as text (the engineer's «glisser sur le texte», C7, became a click on the title). What it does write is an **outline**: one bookmark per heading of its navigator, 56 on his report, each with its page and the height of the heading (`/FitBH`, `/Top`). A click on the page names the lowest bookmark of that page standing at or above it (`annex.bookmark_at`); the bookmark list beside the page offers the same. An anchor is `{kind: "bookmark", path, occurrence}`:

- **its title is compared without its leading chapter number** (`annex.title_key`), a print report renumbering as soon as a chapter is added: the same diagram was «6.4 CC1» in his first report and «5.4 CC1» in the next;
- **a title used several times** — «Charges de barre», once under each load case — is told apart by the **path** of titles above it, narrowed one parent at a time while more than one candidate remains (`annex.candidates`), so a renamed parent costs nothing where the title alone is unique; and where even the path is ambiguous, by its **rank** among what remains (C8a);
- **nothing refuses a PDF without bookmarks**: an excerpt of one is anchored `{kind: "page", page}`, and since nothing can follow such a source into its next version, it is refused until the engineer has looked at it again and **confirmed** it on the new version (`confirmed`, the source's SHA-256 at that moment). His first report was one: printed through «Microsoft Print to PDF», 29 pages of JPEG tiles, 60 MB, no text and no outline — where the same report exported by RFEM is 4.6 MB, vector, with its bookmarks. The tab says so on the source's card.

**Found again, or refused.** `annex.resolve` gives the page, the anchor's height and the frame on the page, or an `engine.Note` saying why not — the title gone, its rank beyond what the new version carries, a page anchor not confirmed, the frame running past the page (the title moved too far down it), a page turned by `/Rotate` (not supported yet). ⚠️ **What cannot be found stops the report** (his answer C9: a diagram that is not the right one is worse than no report): the export warns and records `resolved: false` with the reason, and `#show-extract` panics with it — the plans' and the connectors' warn-then-refuse, applied (§3.3quinquies).

**The page is cropped by the export, never by Typst.** Typst 0.15.1 draws a PDF page made of clipped image tiles wrongly as soon as it is placed away from the origin — the content moves twice as far as its own clips (reproduced with Typst's own PNG export, so no viewer's fault) — which is exactly the offset a crop by `place(dx, dy)` inside a clipped box needs. So `annex.crop` writes each excerpt as a **one-page PDF whose MediaBox and CropBox are the frame** (pypdf), which Typst sets at the origin, vector and exact. ⚠️ **It declares PDF 1.7** (`annex.PDF_HEADER`): RFEM writes PDF 2.0, and Typst refuses to set a PDF of a higher version than the one it exports («the version of the PDF is too high») — raising the report's own target would have to be done in every build route at once. The pages are copied as they stand; the header is set just before writing, since pypdf raises it to the source's own as each page is added. ⚠️ `show-plan` places a plan's background the same way, and may meet the same defect on a raster-tiled PDF (§14).

**Export.** `annex.prepare(here, previous)` reads and checks every source and excerpt, resolves each excerpt against its source's **current** PDF and builds `data/annexes.json` (schema `annexes` 1.0, `validation.validate_annexes_business`) and `data/annexes/`: `<excerpt>.pdf`, the cropped page, and `<source>-annex.pdf`, the pages the source's `mode` asks for, with each page's size. A PDF whose inputs — the source's SHA-256, the page, the frame — have not changed is **kept** rather than written again (the plans' rule, §3.3sexies), and a file no longer needed is swept. In a project laid out by folders (v0.119, issue #64, D-19) these PDFs lie in `Appendix/Exports/`, named from the Typst root (`annexes` **1.1**), and only what the last export listed is removed (`engine.Figures`); a PDF written over in `Appendix/` from outside Woodcore has its SHA-256 brought up to date and said (`annex_pdf_replaced`) as the tab is shown and before the export (`refresh_sources`), and a source added before is copied there by the migration and every export after it (`adopt`). `export_all` runs it with everything else; the tab runs `annex.export` on every save; a new project is seeded an empty export (`annex.empty`), so its report compiles from the start. `annexes` is a folder of `project.SOURCE_FOLDERS`, so every archive of an export holds the sources.

⚠️ *Retired by the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (lot 19): `show-extract` -- write #figure(image-extract(id), kind: image, caption: [...]). What follows describes it as it was.* **In Typst** (§10bis): `#show-extract(id, width:, caption:)` prints the cropped page as a numbered figure, «Auszug aus Rapport RFEM, S. 24» by default, **with no levels** (D13: a picture is printed whole or not at all); `#show-annexes(heading:)` prints, for each source in file-name order — lettered A, B, C — a cover «Anhang A — its name», its description and the original's page numbers, then its pages **full page at their own size**, without the report's header or page number (E17), each excerpt's region framed. In `used` mode only the pages an excerpt **the report shows** comes from are printed, which Typst knows and the export cannot. ⚠️ **The two ends are linked by where each lands, not by a label**: a label written twice is an error in Typst, and an excerpt may be shown twice, so each leaves a `metadata` — `<woodcore-extract>` with its id, `<woodcore-annex-page>` with its source and page — and the other end links to the location `query` finds, where there is one (E18). An excerpt in a report without annexes is simply not a link. `ids("extract")` and `ids("annex")` are the data access. The report template's `Sections/009-anhang.typ` (v0.115) calls `#show-annexes()` under the engineer's own heading and an introductory paragraph, both tagged objects; the first cover stands under the heading, every later one opens a page.

⚠️ **Nothing is computed.** An excerpt is a picture of another program's calculation, so there is no `{vars, steps, checks}` record and nothing addresses one with `show-calc` — the rule a static model and a detail schema follow (§3.3octies, §3.3decies). What it shows is traced by its file and its page, not by a `source` field.

**In the three documents (v0.115).** Gian built the annexes for the report alone. On master a project is up to three documents compiled apart, and **an excerpt may be shown in any of them**: `show-extract`'s `query(<woodcore-extract>)` and `show-annexes`' `query(<woodcore-annex-page>)` are **per compiled document**, so `used` mode prints in each document the pages of the excerpts **that** document shows, with no change to the command; the lettering (Anhang A, B…) stays the project's, in file-name order, and a letter is skipped in a document that prints nothing of a source. An excerpt shown in a document that prints no `#show-annexes` finds no target and is simply not a link. ⚠️ **The headings.** `show-annexes(heading: 2)` takes the level its annex names are set at, which is already an argument and is what keeps it compatible with v0.113's headings written at the level they print at: a section written at depth 2 (`== …`) asks for `heading: 3`. Its cover and annex pages are `page(margin: 0pt, header: none, footer: none, …)`, which the three forms (neutral, compact, bfh) draw on a full page without their header and footer (the BFH form sets both as `set page(header:, footer:)`, which a `page()` call with its own `header: none, footer: none` overrides).

⚠️ *Retired by the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (lot 19): `doc-extract` -- a document is written in raw Typst since the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (REQ-019): write #figure(image-extract(id), kind: image, caption: [...]); `doc-annexes` -- a document is written in raw Typst since the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (REQ-019): write #show-annexes(). What follows describes it as it was.* **Two objects of the «Structure du document» tab** (§3.3octodecies, v0.115): `doc-extract(excerpt:, width:, caption:)` -- `show-extract` with a frame standing in its place while no excerpt is chosen, a caption the call gives while none is, and the right pane's choice of excerpt among those the export holds (`PaneContext.excerpts()`, data/annexes.json), its width and its caption automatic, a text or none --, and `doc-annexes(heading:)` -- `show-annexes` as an object a section holds, its pane the level of the heading or none. Both are in `typst_objects.SIGNATURES`, `OBJECT_TYPES`, `MODELS` (`ExtractModel`, `AnnexesModel`), `object_panes.PANES` and the «+ Objet» menu, **exactly as `doc-plan` is**.

**A section «Annexes» in each content template, switched off** (v0.115): the report's `009-anhang.typ` (its include line commented out in the entry, as the report marks a section not printed) -- `#pagebreak(weak: true)`, the engineer's «= Anhänge» and `#show-annexes()` --, the convention's `annex-pages` in its chapter Annexes, and the base's `annex-pages` before its journal, each a tagged `doc-annexes` object (`heading: 3` under the convention's chapter, `heading: 2` in the base).

**Three ways to make an excerpt (issue #16).** Besides a title clicked and a frame dragged, an excerpt is made in one gesture, worked out in this module without Qt: a **frame drawn** (`frame_excerpt`: anchored on the lowest bookmark of its page standing above the frame's top -- or under it by `FRAME_GRACE` (12 pt) at most, the title the frame is drawn round from a little above it, which a click's single point of `bookmark_at` anchored on the title before it --, the frame measured from it; on its page, confirmed on this version, where none stands above -- a source with bookmarks may then hold page-anchored excerpts, refused after a new version until confirmed again); a **page whole** (`page_excerpt`: anchored on its page, framed by its crop box); a **frame found** in the page's drawing (`find_rects`: `re` and four-sided straight paths, painted and never a clip, through the `cm` matrices and the forms `Do` calls with their `/Matrix`, at most eight deep and never twice into one form, larger than `MIN_FOUND` either way and not the page's own ground, from the top-left corner of the crop box; `rects_at` the ones under a point, the smallest first). The excerpt stays one page and one frame (schema 1.0). Its name is its title without its chapter number or «p. N», its id `<source>-<name>` made unique against the excerpts and the batch (`excerpt_name`, `free_id`).
