### 3.3undecies Member checks: SIA 265 chapter 4.2, written in the report (v0.88)

⚠️ *Retired by the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (lot 19): `show-check-member` -- write #include domain-file("check", id) -- its file, written out by the export, printed where the section includes it. What follows describes it as it was.* **Laid out by folders (v0.119, issue #64):** a member check has a file of its own in its kind's folder, `Timber-checks/<Kind>/<id>.typ` -- a stub, `#show-check-member("<id>")`, written when the check is made (calc/domains/domain_file.py), which a section prints by `#include` --, and a check printed unfolded is written over that stub where it is still as Woodcore wrote it, never over one the engineer edited (`unfold_stub_edited`); `unfold.json` lies in `Timber-checks/`; the export names the unfolded file (checks 1.4, `file`), which `#show-check-member` includes. In a project laid out before, `Formules/<id>.typ` as below.

The member checks are the one calculation of this engine that is neither read from a workbook nor computed in Python: the engineer asked for them «uniquement au-travers de commandes typst (sans passer par le katalog)», so they live in `lib/timber.typ` and are computed where they are written, like `#eq` (§10quinquies). ⚠️ **Untraced** (§1): nothing reaches `data/`. What they guarantee is that the printed formula, the printed numbers and the verdict are one statement and that every value of the norm is the transcribed one — each read from `calc/norms/` by the relative path `../calc/norms/`, as `lib/distance.typ` reads its tables (§2 rule 13, §11).

⚠️ *Retired by the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (lot 19): `member` -- write #position(id) for the member's line; its checks are #include domain-file("check", id). What follows describes it as it was.* **A member is written down once** (the engineer's option B). `#member(name, b:, h:, grade:, moisture-class:, position:, impact:, k-h:, level:, title:)` reads the position of the same name — its `dimension` as b/h (the lenient reading `schema.parse_section` makes, a second reader of that text, §7), its strength class and its moisture class — and an argument writes each over (the engineer: «les deux, l'argument l'emporte»); a member that is no position states all four. It stores, in a `var-domain` named after it: b, h, A, W_y, W_z, I_y, I_z, i_y, i_z, k_tor and W_t; eta_w for the resistance and eta_w_E for the stiffness (Tabelle 4), eta_t (2.2.6, 1.4 with `impact: true`); the design values f_m_y_d, f_m_z_d, f_t_0_d, f_c_0_d, f_t_90_d, f_c_90_d and f_v_d of Tabelle 8 or 9 times eta_w · eta_t — those tables being design values already (3.3.2.1) — and for glulam f_m by **k_h** (3.4.2.3, `k-h: true` by default: the norm allows it and the Bemessungsbeispiele apply it, 1.08 at h = 280 mm) -- **k_h,z on b too**, which 3.4.2.2 forbids only together with k_sys, which Woodcore never applies; the page cites «3.4.2.3, 3.4.2.2» for it (the engineer, 12.10.2026, spec 2026-10-12-audit-issues-47-48-49 D-12, REQ-014); f_m_k; and f_c_0_k and E_0_05 of **Anhang B, Tabellen 47 and 48**, E_0_05 times eta_w_E -- in the three moisture classes, 1.0, 0.9 and 0.75, the rule of v0.88 the engineer confirmed on 12.10.2026 against the reference workbooks, which compute η_w for the stiffness and leave it out (spec 2026-10-12-audit-issues-47-48-49 D-11, REQ-013). Tension, compression and shear take no k_h.

⚠️ **`#member` takes any position since v0.94, and computes where it can.** Its level-1 line is the position's own — the line `show-position` printed, which is gone — with `description:` and `axes:` as that command had them, so `Sections/003`'s list and `calc-block`'s header are `#member`'s too, and an unknown id is refused listing the valid ones. It **computes** only a timber of Tabelle 8 or 9 with a `rec` or a `cir` section and a moisture class: anything else — steel, a plate, a CLT panel, no moisture class — is written down `calculable: false` with its reason, and every `verify-*` refuses it with that reason. **A round section** (`cir`, or `d:` written) gives A = πd²/4, W = πd³/32, I = πd⁴/64, i = d/4 and W_t = πd³/16, k_h taken at d; its shear is 4/3·V/A, mechanics and footnoted as such; and **it has no lateral torsional buckling**, being as stiff about either axis: the page says so, and k_m is 1 in (37). **A rectangle wider than high tips about z** (spec 2026-10-12-audit-issues-47-48-49, REQ-011, D-09): `timber-strong` and its twin `member_check.strong_axis` give the axis -- y where h ≥ b, z where b > h --, k_m then divides the bending about z in (38) and (37), and (41) reads h²/(a·b); bent about y alone such a section tips about nothing, k_m is 1 and the page says so (D-17). The reference round-column template is reproduced within its E_0,05 difference (7333 against the norm's 7400). A record exported before the `section` object is refused, saying to export again.

⚠️ **N is signed, tension positive**, and chooses: `verify-tension` refuses a compression, `verify-compression` a tension, and `verify-bending-normal` takes (21) or (22) by its sign. A moment is taken by its magnitude. An action is a number (kN, kNm, mm, N/mm²), a value with its unit («40 kN»), the name of a variable declared above the line, or a var object; a length is also a Typst length (`3000mm`).

⚠️ **Two parts, set apart** — «Tragsicherheit» and «Stabilität», each a heading with its clause in the right-hand column where it differs from the check's own (the engineer: «clairement séparer la sécurité structurale et la stabilité»). **Levels** (since issue #12 every verdict is the ratio of the action to the resistance against 1, never η nor `σ ⩽ f`, §13): 0 prints nothing and computes everything; 1 the verdicts, one per part, the ratio's symbols and value, `σ_c,0,d / (k_c,z · f_c,0,d) = 0.78 ⩽ 1 ✔`; 2 the stresses, the slenderness, the factors and the norm's own relation — `σ_c,0,d = 2 N/mm² ⩽ 2.563 N/mm² = 0.258 · 9.92 N/mm² = k_c,z · f_c,0,d ✔` — each on its line; 3 every formula in numbers with its citation. From level 2 the verdict is the ratio in symbols, numbers and value, `σ_c,0,d / (k_c,z · f_c,0,d) = 2 N/mm² / (0.258 · 9.92 N/mm²) = 0.78 ⩽ 1 ✔`; a sum of ratios — (14), (21), (22), (37), (25), (28) — is its own ratio. A term whose action is not given is left out of the sum. `unit: "%"` prints the ratio in percent and nothing after it, `σ/f = 78 % ✔`. η is stored at every level (`eta`, `eta_s`, …) and printed nowhere.

⚠️ **The branches print the branch that holds**: k_c = 1 where λ_rel ⩽ 0.3 (Figur 10), else (30)/(31); k_m of (39) on one of its three branches, its condition beside it. Without a or σ_m,crit, lateral torsional buckling is **not checked and the page says so** in orange; in (37) k_m is then 1, said likewise.

⚠️ *Retired by the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (lot 19): `val` -- write #var("M_d") -- #var(name) reprints a variable declared above, #var(name, domain: ("snow-load", "SL")) reads one of the export. What follows describes it as it was.* **Published** in `"<member>-verify-<check>"` (the engineer's name), `name:` to override: every stress, factor and slenderness, and `eta` — the largest utilisation of the check — beside `eta_s` (Tragsicherheit), `eta_y`, `eta_z`, `eta_m`. `#val("eta", domain: "S001-verify-compression")`, `#find(object: "variable", domain: "S001-verify-bending")`.

⚠️ **Level -1 (v0.117, issue #20).** `#member`, the five checks a saved check calls -- `verify-tension`, `verify-compression`, `verify-compression-perp`, `verify-bending`, `verify-bending-normal` -- and `#show-check-member` take `level: -1, shown: (A: 2, sigma_c_0_d: 3)`: each variable named printed as a line of its level, none of the others; the title, the position's line, the parts that hold a verdict and the verdicts as at level 1; «Knicken um die y-Achse», «… z-Achse» and «Kippen», printed from level 2 only, where a key under them is chosen. `show-check-member` takes one list: a key its check prints goes to the check, the rest to the member, whose `member-level` gives way. The keys are lib/shown-variables.json's (docs/typst/report-engine.md §4); every `eq` of the checks passes `note: "line"` through `timber-eq`, so `#eq`'s inline default (v0.117) moved no line of a check.

⚠️ **The import runs both ways, and Typst does not see a cycle.** commands.typ re-exports formula.typ at the wildcard and a project's commands.typ is its own file, so a new command reaches the sections only through formula.typ; formula.typ therefore imports the nine public commands from lib/timber.typ by name (as it imports `distance`), and lib/timber.typ, which needs `var`, `eq`, `verify` and `var-domain`, imports them **inside each command's body** — an import that runs when the command is called, formula.typ being evaluated by then. A module-level import back would be a cycle Typst refuses. Checked on a three-file probe before it was relied on.

⚠️ **The branch is decided at the call, not read back.** λ_rel depends on the section, the strength class and a length, all known when the call writes the length out, so `timber-kc` and `timber-km` compute it in plain Typst from the member's record (`timber-static`) and print the branch that holds before anything is stored. Read back from the registry in a nested `context`, as the first version did, each check cost one more layout pass, and a check of bending with lateral torsional buckling did not converge in Typst's five — eight frozen examples failed on it. Only a length named by a variable of the report is still read back; and there a branch writes its value on every path, since in the first pass the read can come back empty and a branch that then stored nothing left the `#eq` after it with a name it could not find, a panic inside an update, fatal in any pass. Compiled once as a report of fifty checks at level 3 (25 members, `verify-bending-normal` and `verify-bending` each, some lengths named by a variable): 51 pages, converged.

⚠️ **«für» is an engine key, not a template.** The condition of a branch reads «k_m = 1 — für λ_rel,m ⩽ 0.75», its first word `t-str("timber.for")`. A template «für {condition}» would have matched any German sentence of the data starting with «für» and translated its first word alone — the risk §14 records for `tl-of`.

**Torsion is Saint-Venant's, from its series** (`timber-torsion-k`, Timoshenko & Goodier): τ_max = T/(k_tor · a · c²). The Bemessungsbeispiele read the same coefficient off a table (p. 114, h/b = 2.75: W_T = 2.95·10⁶ mm³); Roark's approximation, written first, gave 4.5 % more — on the unsafe side. It is mechanics, not the norm, and its footnote says so.

**What is held** (tests/test_timber.py, §11ter): every result re-derived in Python from the norm's equations; 16 cases of tests/fixtures/timber-oracles.json reproduced — Bemessungsbeispiele zur SIA 265 pp. 72, 73, 87, 114, 122, 125, 143, 166, 167, 263 and 265, and the reference beam template to its full precision — and the two k_c · f_c,0,d tables of the Holzbautabellen (HBT 1 pp. 53, 58), row by row. The other 30 cases follow another method and are recorded as such in the fixture: the simplified λ/60 and 0.06·√(a·h)/b (the book's usual glulam route, which this engine does not take), net sections with holes, 6.9.1.2's joint formula with its 0.8 for a force at an angle, Anhang C, a round column.

⚠️ **Saved checks, and the `var-line` of tension (v0.117).** `#show-check-member` and `#ids("check-member")` (§3.3unvicies, §10bis) are the two commands of `lib/timber.typ` that read a file, `data/checks.json`, by the relative path `calc-engine.typ` reads every export by; they are imported into `lib/formula.typ` by name beside the nine, and `tests/test_timber.py` holds the list of names. And `#verify-tension` sets its action **on one `var-line`** — N_t,d and, at an angle, α, `N_t,d = 20 kN; α = 10 °` — where the two inline `#var` (v0.87) ran into each other on the page («20 kNα = 10 °», the engineer's report of 07.10.2026); the `var-line` is imported inside the command's body, as `var`, `eq` and `verify` already are, and bending, which has set its actions that way since v0.88, was the model. Only the layout of that one line moved; the numbers and the verdicts did not (`examples/out/verify-tension-2.png` re-rendered). And `#verify-compression` gained **`stability:`** (07.10.2026): true by default, as it always was; false checks the section alone, σ_c,0,d ⩽ f_c,0,d, with no buckling length asked for, no «Stabilität» part printed and η the section's — what `#show-check-member` calls for a compression along the grain (`examples/out/verify-compression-3.png`).

### 3.3unvicies Member checks saved in Woodcore: resolved by the export, computed in the report (v0.117)

The member checks of SIA 265 4.2 (§3.3undecies) are written in the report with `#member` and a `#verify-*`; the engineer asked for the same checks **set up in Woodcore and saved**, so that a check is an object with an ID that the report names (07.10.2026). The tension (4.2.1), compression (4.2.2), bending (4.2.3, 4.2.4), shear (4.2.5), buckling (4.2.8) and lateral torsional buckling (4.2.9) checks are built -- the last three since issue #38 --; the other kinds are listed so that a source naming one is refused as «not implemented yet» rather than as unknown (`member_check.KINDS`: tension, compression, bending, shear, torsion, buckling, lateral-torsional, deformation, vibration; `IMPLEMENTED = ("tension", "compression", "bending", "shear", "buckling", "lateral-torsional")`).

**Source.** `Woodcore-Datei/checks/<id>.json`, one file per check, written by Woodcore's «Traction» tab and read by `calc/domains/member_check.py` alone — the connectors' arrangement (§3.3nonies, §13), `project.SOURCE_FOLDERS` and `Project.checks_dir` carrying the folder so that an archive and a copy of a project hold it. `schemas/member-check-source.schema.json` states its shape (`schema_version` **1.0**): the `id` (its file name, letters, digits and `_ . + -`), optional `name`, `label` and `group` (the folder of the left pane, additive, §13 v0.69), the `kind`, the optional `position`, then `grade`, `section` and `moisture_class`, each **`null` to follow the position** or a value, `impact` (η_t = 1.4, SIA 265 2.2.6), `alpha` (the angle between the force and the grain, 0 to 90°) and `N`, `{value, unit}` with the unit kN, N or MN, **tension positive**. ⚠️ **A compression** (07.10.2026) adds four optional fields, additive, the schema staying 1.0: `case` («generell», «mit_vorholz», «endauflagerung»; null is generell), `larger` (the bracketed f_c,90,d), `l` (the contact length along the grain, mm) and `w` (the contact width, mm; null follows the section's width b); `N` is then the force's magnitude, positive — the normal force along the grain, the force F on the contact area at an angle. The section is `{"type": "rec", "b", "h"}` or `{"type": "cir", "d"}` in mm, which is what `#member` computes. `check_source` stops the export on a **malformed** source and names the file and the field: an id that is not its file name, a kind outside `KINDS` or not implemented yet, an angle outside 0..90, a **stated** strength class SIA 265 Tabelle 8 or 9 does not give, a section side of 0, a unit outside the three; for a compression `larger` with the case generell, a `case` outside the three, `l` or `w` not above 0, and `l` missing at an angle.

**What the project lacks never stops (the v0.33 rule).** `member_check.resolve(src, positions)` gives the record of one source against the positions of the same export: `grade`, `section` and `moisture_class` resolved — **stated** wins and stays through the next *Import données*, otherwise the position's, read afresh at every export — each with where it came from (`grade_from`, `section_from`, `moisture_from`: `"stated"`, `"position"` or none), whether the position was found (`position_found`), and `calculable` with its `reason`. A position gone, a position with no section (or a steel one, which `#member` does not compute as a rectangle or a circle), no strength class, **a position's strength class SIA 265 does not know**, no moisture class, an N that is no positive force (a tension's, a compression's magnitude), for a compression at an angle a contact it cannot read (a round section with `w` null; a grade whose Tabelle 8/9 gives no bracketed f_c,90,d for the case — D30 with `larger`), and **C16 along the grain** of a tension (Tabelle 8 note 2, «Für Zugglieder nicht zulässig» — the very refusal `#verify-tension` makes, said here so that the record is not exported as calculable and refused later) each make the check exported `calculable: false` with the reason, and the report prints the reason. ⚠️ **A stated grade SIA 265 lacks stops; a position's does not**: the first is a defect in a file Woodcore wrote, the second a gap in the project. Each of those earns an `engine.Note` (`check_position_missing`, `check_not_calculable`) that the window writes in its own language (§4bis, v0.79).

**Export.** `member_check.prepare(here, positions)` reads, checks and resolves every source and builds **`data/checks.json`** (`checks` **1.0**, `schemas/checks.schema.json`, `validation.validate_checks_business`, which holds that a check exported as calculable carries a strength class, a section, a moisture class of Tabelle 3, a positive N and an angle in 0..90 and, for a compression at an angle, its `l`, `w`, `case` and `f_c_90_table` — the record and the report agreeing); `export_all.py` runs it after the connections, prints the warnings and writes the file, and **seeds it empty** (`member_check.empty`) so that `ids("check-member")` gives an array rather than stopping the compile on a missing file. Woodcore saving a check runs `member_check.export()`, against the positions the last export wrote (`connector.known_positions`), without the workbooks being read again. Two ids differing only by case are refused.

⚠️ *Retired by the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (lot 19): `member` -- write #position(id) for the member's line; its checks are #include domain-file("check", id). What follows describes it as it was.* **A check in the design situation fire** (issue #46) is a source of one of these kinds carrying `fire`, at `schema_version` 1.2, listed by the «Résistance au feu» tab and by none of the others; `#member(fire:)` writes its member down as the ideal residual section with R_d,fi = 1.8 · R_d: docs/domain/fire-resistance.md §3.3tervicies.

**A tension is printed unfolded** (v0.118, issue #62, spec docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-15-calculs-deplies-balises-figures-natives.md). The engineer, 15.10.2026: «on ne peut pas voir l'entièreté des calculs qui sont faits». A calculable tension that is not in the fire situation (`member_check.is_unfolded`, `unfold.UNFOLDED_KINDS`) is written by Woodcore, the first time it is saved or exported, as a domain of the «Formules» tab, `Formules/<id>.typ`, its id the check's as it is (D-14): the member's line (labelled `<id-member>`), the section's sides at level 0, every input of `compute` and every value of SIA 265 Tabelle 8/9 a design value is computed from as a literal checked by `domain: ("check", "<id>")`, every derived value an `#eq` citing SIA 265 and its clause, the part «Tragsicherheit» (labelled), the relation `#verify(…, id: "v-6")` and η in percent (`unfold.generate`, from `compute`'s rows alone -- `_row` carries `keys`, `clause` and `table` since then, ARCH-008 --, held equal to `compute` within 1e-6 at α = 0, 30 and 90°). The export publishes for it `vars` and `gen` in data/checks.json, 1.3. `#show-check-member` of such a check prints its domain and refuses its options (D-18), each line carrying its own level; `shown:` and the level -1 are, for it, the file's.
