# Building a tab of calculation

> The type «Calcul»: how a tab of calculation is built from its services -- the snow first, the permanent loads, the wind, the live loads, the seismic action and the member checks after it -- and what its domain declares (spec `docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-22-onglet-neige-type.md`, issue #94; spec `docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-23-onglets-types-et-services.md`, issue #95; `docs/decisions.md`, «a tab is built from a type and its services»). How every type is built is `building-a-tab.md`.

## 10quater. The tab of calculation, a described tab (v0.124)

The engineer, 22.10.2026: «la façon dont l'onglet Neige est construit doit être un onglet type. Le vent, le séisme, les charges permanentes, les charges utiles et les vérifications en reprendront le fonctionnement. Un futur onglet ne doit pas réinventer sa base : il repart d'une base saine, déjà écrite et documentée, et n'apporte que ce qui est propre à son domaine.» A tab of calculation is therefore **described by its domain and drawn by the services of its type** (`calc/tools/calc_tab.py` `CalcTab`, v0.125, issue #95): adding the wind is writing its declaration and its domain's functions, never copying a widget.

### What a domain brings, without Qt (`calc/domains/contracts.py` `CalcTabDomain`)

- **`declaration()`** -- a `Declaration(prefix, norm, sections, fields)`: the prefix of its tab's words (`sn_`), the norm file its excerpts are read from (`sia261-2020-5.2-schnee`), its sections in order, each `(id, field keys)`, and its `Field`s: key, kind, unit, choices (`Choice(id, word, value, excerpts)`: a word of the glossary, or of the tab where it begins with the prefix), the excerpts its ⓘ shows, a number's range, decimals and step, whether it may follow a variable, and for buttons the free value's id and key.
- **`visible_fields(src)`**, **`messages(src)`** -- `Message(code, field, severity, params)`, an `error` per field the calculation cannot read and an `info` for what is only said, every threshold and clause the norm file's --, **`results(instance)`** -- the values computed, never an input --, and its source's functions: `new_source`, `check_source`, `read_source`, `build_instance`, `stamp_version`, `LINKABLE`, `FOLDER`, `DOMAIN`, `ID_PATTERN`.
- **`Field.var`**, the variable of the instance a field fills (`correction` → `korrektur`), and optionally **`extra_dependencies(instance)`**, {result: [names]}, what no step of the instance records (the snow's μ read off Figur 2) -- what «Résultats» and the form light up together, never a step added to the export.
- Optionally **`figure_svg(src, lang=None)`** (the drawing the tab shows and the export prints: one code, no word in the report's), **`tile_svg(choice)`**, **`chart(src)`** (series, marks; the base reads a value under the pointer and knows nothing of what they mean).
- Optionally, since spec 2026-10-23-onglet-vent-briques (V2), **blocks** in its declaration (`Declaration(..., blocks=(Block(key, kind, field, pinnable, wide, min_width, children), …), modules=((id, block keys), …))`, a section naming a block's key as it names a field's, a `views` block the blocks it shows one at a time (`children`, placed in no section), `modules` what the right pane shows of them -- «results» under the values of «Résultats», another id a module of its own titled `<prefix>m_<id>` (D-34, D-35)) with **`blocks(src, instance)`** -- {key: data}, each kind's shape in `calc_blocks`' docstring, called with the instance None while the calculation waits, so that a block may say what the source holds --, and **`key_links(src, instance)`** -- (not `links`, the name of the module of variable links every load imports) -- {element id: {"lit": keys, "emphasis": keys}}, what each element of its tab is read from (a cell of the norm's table, a result `var:<name>`). The data of both come from one calculation of the domain's (the wind's `wind_forces.analysis`), never from `calculations.json`.
- **The excerpts of its norm** in the norm file it reads (`calc/norms/<norm>.json`): `editions` (each language's PDF in `norm-library/` and the chapter Ctrl+click opens) and `excerpts` (per id: kind, and per edition the clause as it names it, the page, the box in PDF points from the top-left corner, the words copied once and held to the PDF by a test), `map` where it opens a site.
- **The generator of its file** (`calc/domains/unfold.py` `GENERATORS`), the figure at its head through `unfold.figure_line`.

The tab itself is a class of parameters: `class SnowTab(calc_tab.CalcTab)` naming its key, its module, its ID prefix and its list's words -- not one method (`tests/test_tab_overrides.py`) --, a row of `tab_registry.TABS`, and its words module (`snow_tab_words.py`), merged by `woodcore_gui_words.py`. A domain that lacks a function a service asks (`NEEDS`) stops the tab as it is built, naming the tab, the service and the function. `tests/test_calc_tab_base.py` mounts the type on a domain invented for it (`tests/fake_domain.py`) and finds no word of the snow in the type and its services.

### The snow, a complete example

`calc/domains/snow_source.py` declares the building first (L, B: always entered, read by a flat roof for Figur 4), the site (h₀; Δh of Anhang D, whose ⓘ opens 261.sia.ch's snow map at Ctrl+click; C_e's three buttons and a value of the engineer's own; C_T), the roof (five tiles drawn as Figur 3, the slopes, the sliding, a height step). Its messages: the 2000 m of 5.1.4 and the 800 m of 5.3.7 in orange, C_T under 1, a slope of 60° free to slide, a large flat roof, a height step; a field it cannot read in red. Its drawing is the column of Figur 3 of the case's roof (`snow_svg.figure`), its chart Figur 2. See `docs/ui/tab-snow.md`.

### What the others will bring (the architecture review, spec §13)

- **The wind** (`calc/domains/wind_source.py`, 6.2 and the 37 buildings of Annexe C; spec `docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-23-onglet-vent-briques.md`, in five versions): `select` for the table, a number with a special value (`z` «= h»), b · d · h on one line, coloured tables of the norm's coefficients, hovers by keys, the 3D model and its two results pinnable, results in tables (`instance["tables"]`), excerpts spread over several norm files (`calc/norms/excerpts.py` reads any), the map `layer=wind`. `wind_source.messages` and `wind_forces.analysis` already give its messages and its numbers (V1), the bricks it needs are every tab's (V2).
- **The permanent loads** (v0.125, `dead_load_tab.py`): their table of layers (`layer_table.LayerTable`) in place of the declared form (`calc_tab.swap`) and, under it, the card of the engineer's materials changed since the project took its copy (`material_copies.MaterialCopies`, issue #110 -- the project's library `ProjectMaterials` gone); their drawing is the build-up, a module of the right pane, read off their model of layers (`dead_load_source.layers`); `PREFIX = "ld_"`, their domain declaring no form. See `docs/domain/loads.md`. **Redrawn by issue #111 (v0.125)**: the table at the keyboard (`layer_table.LayerTable`, `layer_cells.next_cell` pure, the cells a pose does not use greyed and skipped), «Couche n» (`layer_options.LayerOptions`) and «Coupe | Contributions» (`layer_section.LayerSection`, the section drawn at the module's width, a click opening its line, ⤢ a window that follows its size) given to the right pane in this order after the state, «⊞ Vue d'ensemble» (`case_overview.Overview`) and «Nouveau…» from a template (`template_picker.TemplatePicker`); `DeadLoadTab` stays a class of parameters (`calc_tab.before`, `calc_tab.swap`).
- **The seismic action**: a chart (the spectrum), numbers and lists.
- **The member checks** are on the type since issue #112 (`calc/tools/check_tabs.py`, docs/ui/tab-member-checks.md): one domain for four tabs (`member_check_view.CheckDomain`, each listing its kinds in the folder they share), the list grouped by position with each check's rate (`check_list.CheckList`, a CaseList whose order is its own rule: `KEEPS_ORDER = False`), the head «Élément» and its rows «Sollicitation» and «Facteurs» before the declared form (`check_member.CheckMember`, `load_row.LoadRow`, `factors_row.FactorsRow`), a field of buttons showing under each choice what the domain says it is worth (`choice_values(src)`, optional: the five cards of f_c,90,d, a choice without a value greyed), «Résultats» given to the pane in place of the type's (`check_results.CheckResults`: a service gives a module «results», calc_modules.Modules adds none of its own), «Affichage» writing a check's options into its source (`check_display.CheckDisplay`). What they brought the type, any tab may use: a domain's `owns(src)`, `save_case`, `export_cases` and `graph_id` (source_store.SourceStore, tab_host.TabHost), its `message_params(params, lang)` (the values of a message in the window's language), `Field.block` and `Field.zero_none` (a number written as a {value, unit} block, 0 as null), the rows following the draft at every computation and a section with no field shown hidden (calc_form.DeclaredForm). `MemberCheckTab` stays the fire's and the steel's base.
- The wind is on the type since issue #100 (`wind_tab.py`, `docs/ui/tab-wind.md`): `LoadTab` is gone.
