## 10quater (suite) — commands tab

**The commands tab (v0.64).** *Commandes / Befehle / Commands*, in the Référence group, lists every command the project defines, one condensed page each. It is described in **§10sexies** ([`docs/typst/command-docs.md`](../typst/command-docs.md)), apart from the tabs above it, because it documents the engine's own surface rather than this project's data.

**Since issue #115 (v0.126): one view, three panes** (spec `docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-24-onglet-commandes-pages.md`, the prototype the engineer validated on 24.10.2026 in `docs/requirements/prototypes/2026-10-24-onglet-commandes/`). No switch of views, no module of state, no bubble of a norm: the commands are fixed. Since issue #106 the tab (`commands_tab.CommandsTab`) is a class of parameters on the type «Données» (`data_tab.DataTab`, [`building-a-tab.md`](building-a-tab.md)), without the type's banner -- it reads `lib/`, not the export --: its three panes are mounted by one whole service, `CommandsBrowser` (`tab.service("browser")`), which builds them once with the state the window kept through a rebuild (`ctx.kept`: the search, the rows open, the command chosen, the examples' levels) and passes the frame's phases `revealed` and `hidden` to them; the command palette's `search` is exposed on the tab (`EXPOSES`).

- **On the left** (`calc/tools/command_list.py`): the field «Chercher une commande…», which reads the name and the lead of each command in the window's language and nothing else; the categories by task -- Calculer dans le rapport, Imprimer un dessin ou un objet, Lire les données du projet, Le document et ses citations, Écrire un modèle, then Internes, folded --, each with the number of its commands; under each, the name in the fixed font and its lead on a second line, cut by «…».
- **In the centre** (`calc/tools/command_page_view.py`): `#name`, its category and its lead; **the line of the command** in a read-only text browser, the positional arguments first and the named ones a line per group (at most four a line), the required in bold, every name underlined with dots and every default in a small box with ▾; the hint «Survolez un argument : ce qu'il fait…»; «L'essentiel» in three or four cards; **the arguments** as a table -- Argument · Statut · Ce qu'il fait, et des exemples -- in groups (the line's arguments, what is printed, label and description, citation, other) and a last row «Écrits par leur nom»; the examples, each with its code and the page it prints, the 1 · 2 · 3 buttons where the levels differ, one page and «Les trois niveaux impriment la même page.» where they do not (`calc/tools/command_examples.py`).
- **On the right** (`calc/tools/command_side.py`): «Lignes à copier» -- the minimal call and the complete call, each with «Copier»; «Exemples» -- the examples by their first line, a click scrolling the page to it and lighting it for a moment; «Dans ce projet» -- the calls of the command in the project that is open, read-only, `file:line` and the code, twelve at most and the number of the others (`calc/tools/command_usage.py`).

**The bubbles** (`calc/tools/hover_bubble.py`, a brick of its own, which the domains and variables tabs may use): hovering a name in the line or the table opens a narrow bubble -- the name in its box, «optionnel · par défaut auto» or «obligatoire» beside it, then its sentence; hovering a default opens a wide one (up to 860 px) -- every value with what it does, the current one lit, and for the value hovered its call (its set-up lines in grey) and «Ce qu'il imprime», its print cut out of the committed sheet of its command and language; it says «Rien n'est imprimé.», «Imprime la même chose que …», the role's own note, or gives an example of a free text. A bubble stays open while the pointer is in it and closes 300 ms after it leaves; a click keeps it; Escape, a click elsewhere or a scroll of the page close it. Nothing is compiled when a page or a bubble opens; a print missing or older than its call is said, never compiled behind the reader's back.

**What it reads.** The words of each page are `calc/i18n/command-pages.json`'s, in the window's language; the structure is lib/'s (`calc/tools/command_page.py` composes it, the website composes the same); the prints are `examples/out/values/`; every word of the page is `calc/i18n/reference.json`'s, the tab's STRINGS carry only its own chrome. A malformed `command-pages.json` is said in place of the page, naming the file and the field.
