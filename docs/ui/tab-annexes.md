## 10quater (suite) — annexes tab

**The annexes tab (v0.111).** *Annexes* (DE *Anhänge*, EN *Appendices*; group *Projet*, after *Journal*, **in the three documents alike** (`DOCUMENT_TABS`; the engineer's place for it of 06.10.2026 -- v0.111 had put it above *Formules*, in *Référence*); `calc/tools/annex_tab.py`, its words under `ax_`, the warnings of `calc/domains/annex.py` under `warn_annex_`, which `note_text` reads): the documents the report draws on from outside and the excerpts it shows of them (§3.3undevicies). **In its list pane** — «dans le deuxième volet», beside the side navigation and not in it — a tree of two fixed folders, *Sources* and *Extraits*, and what each holds; an excerpt the last export could not find is written in the danger colour, its reason in its tooltip. *Ajouter un PDF…* copies a PDF into the project and exports; *Nouvel extrait…* asks for its source where there are several; *Supprimer* deletes after a yes (a source with its PDF). **In the middle**, the source's bookmarks as its own program's navigator shows them beside **the page**, fitted to the pane's width and scrolled, with the title found as a yellow band, the frame in the report's own frame colour and the source's other excerpts dashed. An excerpt is made there: a **click on its title** takes the bookmark just above the click (or a click in the bookmark list), a **drag** draws the frame, kept relative to the title; on a source without bookmarks the drag alone places it by its page. A line above the page says which step comes next. **On the right**, cards: a source's Nom · Description · ID (`identity.py`), its original file, pages, bookmarks and whether it carries text — with a note where it carries no bookmark — what the annexes carry of it, *Remplacer le PDF…*, and each of its excerpts found again or not; an excerpt's Nom · Description · ID, its source, its title (and, for a title used several times, which occurrence), its frame, its state against the current version of its source, *Confirmer sur cette version* for a page anchor after a new version, a **preview** cut out of the page as the export crops it, and the calls to copy. *Enregistrer* (Ctrl+S) checks what `annex.check_excerpt` would refuse first, writes, and exports the annexes alone (`annex.export`); a source renamed takes its PDF and every excerpt naming it along. ⚠️ **The PDF is rendered by QtPdf from its bytes, never from its file** (a `QBuffer`): a document QtPdf holds open is one Windows refuses to let *Remplacer* write over, the plans' own trouble (§11ter's `project_copy`). *Ajouter un PDF…* and *Remplacer le PDF…* are refused in a project opened read-only (`WRITERS`). **Every page, and three ways to extract (issue #16).** The page of a source is no longer one page fitted to the pane: it is the plans' view of a document's pages (`annex_tab.SourcePages`, a `document_view.PageArea` with no strip under its pages and the excerpts drawn over them) -- every page, scrolled, zoomed with Ctrl and the wheel or the buttons −, +, «Largeur», two, three pages and more side by side when zoomed out, the frames of the other excerpts dashed on their pages. Under the list, the section «Extraire» chooses what a gesture on the pages does: **Cadre** -- every frame drawn makes an excerpt (`annex.frame_excerpt`: anchored on the lowest bookmark of its page above it, else on its page and confirmed), named after its title without its chapter number or «p. N», its id `<source>-<name>` made unique; Shift and a drag redraw the frame of the excerpt selected, and an excerpt begun by «Nouvel extrait…» still takes its frame as before; **Pages** -- «de», «à» and «Extraire les pages», each page an excerpt anchored on its page and framed by it (`annex.page_excerpt`), a click on a page setting the range there; **Cadre trouvé** -- the frames drawn in the page are found off the window's thread (`annex.find_rects`, pypdf: `re` and four-sided paths painted, `cm` and forms followed, clips ignored), dotted, the smallest under the pointer filled, Alt and the wheel or a right click climbing to the frame round it, a click extracting it. Every way checks all its excerpts before writing one, and exports once.
