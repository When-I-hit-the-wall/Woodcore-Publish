# The «Neige» tab

> The snow tab, rebuilt by issue #94 as the first tab of calculation on the common base (spec `docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-22-onglet-neige-type.md`; `docs/ui/calc-tab.md`; the domain: `docs/domain/loads.md` §3.3bis).

## 10quater. «Neige» (v0.124)

`calc/tools/snow_tab.py` `SnowTab(CalcTab)` -- a class of parameters on the type «Calcul» since v0.125 (issue #95, `building-a-tab.md`) --, words `sn_` (`snow_tab_words.py`), its form and its modules drawn from `snow_source.declaration()`. The engineer reviewed it on HTML prototypes in four rounds on 21-22.10.2026; the copy of the last is at commit `5f2b2dc` (`docs/requirements/prototypes/2026-10-22-onglet-neige/`, read by nothing of Woodcore), the issue outranking it where they differ.

**«Calcul».** On the left the roofs, in folders. In the middle the form, its three sections an accordion (v0.125: number, title, «L 15 m · B 10 m», a dot green, orange or red, ▾/▸): **Bâtiment** -- the length and the width, always entered, Figur 4 under their ⓘ, read by a flat roof (5.3.5: μ₁ increased where both exceed 20 m, on the larger) --; **Lieu** -- h₀, Δh of Anhang D (its five buttons and, right of them, a value of the engineer's own in metres, schema 1.4, v0.125; its ⓘ shows the map's excerpt and opens `https://261.sia.ch/?lang=fr&layer=snow` at Ctrl+click, `lang=de` for a window in German or English), C_e's buttons «abrité 1.2», «normal 1», «exposé 0.8» and a value of the engineer's own taken as it is (schema 1.3), C_T --; **Toiture** -- the five forms as tiles drawn in black and white in the style of Figur 3 (a tile's bubble shows its excerpt alone: the whole of Figur 3 for a duo-pitch, a shed and a curved roof, 5.3.2 and Figur 3 for a mono-pitch roof, 5.3.5 and Figur 4 for a flat one), the slopes the form has, the sliding, a height step (5.3.6 and Figur 5). Every datum has its ⓘ: hovered, the clause's words then its image cut out of SIA 261:2020 in the window's language (German for English); clicked, pinned on the right; Ctrl+clicked, chapter 5 in Woodcore's reader (pp. 26-29 FR, 25-28 DE), the clause framed. On the right the modules: **État du calcul** (red: a field the calculation cannot read; orange: above 2000 m «la norme n'autorise pas ce calcul au-dessus de 2000 m (5.1.4)», computed all the same and said without «calculé quand même»; above 800 m the cornice of 5.3.7, which left the report; C_T under 1; a slope of 60° free to slide; a large flat roof; a height step; green: «Toutes les entrées sont là, le calcul est fait.»; a click on a message takes to its field, which flashes), **Résultats** (h_ref, s_k, the μ, the q_k; compact), **Disposition (figure 3)** (the column of Figur 3 of the case's roof, models 1 and 2, «μ₁₁ = 0.80», «α₁ = 30°» -- the drawing the report prints, the models named in the window's language), **Figure 2** (interactive: under the pointer α and μ₁, μ₂, μ₃; the case's slopes marked), and the excerpts pinned.

**«Affichage».** The case's file `Variable-loads/Snow/<id>.typ`, written as the case is saved and at every export, printed by `#include domain-file("snow-load", "<id>")`: compiled with what the report hides pale, set line by line, the domain's level, the figure's width, caption, label and whether it is shown (masked by commenting its line out). See `docs/ui/calc-tab.md`.

**What the file holds** (`unfold._generate_load`, D-18): the figure at its head (`/Variable-loads/Snow/Exports/Figures/<id>-fig3.svg`, no word in it, its caption the glossary's); h₀, Δh, h_ref, C_e, C_T, the roof's form and slopes, the sliding, the μ; s_k an `#eq` of equation (10) (`max((1 + (h_ref / (350 "m"))^2) dot 0.4 "kN/m²", 0.9 "kN/m²")`), the q_k. No cornice line. A clause named by a word of its edition is printed in the report's language («Figure 3», «Annexe D»), a symbol written as a word too (`name-visible:`). **The file follows the case** (v0.125, issue #99, D-4 of the spec `2026-10-23-neige-correctifs-issues-96-99`): saved or exported with another roof -- other slopes, other μ, other loads --, the file is written again in the new form without a question, the display set in «Affichage» kept on every line the new form keeps, the figure as it was set, a sentence of the engineer's after the line it followed (`unfold.AUTO_REGENERATE`, `regenerate(…, keep_changed=False)`); «Régénérer» is gone from the tab, and «Calcul» left for «Affichage» saves the case. A file Woodcore could not write again says so in red, without a button.
