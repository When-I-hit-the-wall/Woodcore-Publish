# The fire resistance tab

> «Résistance au feu» / «Feuerwiderstand» / «Fire resistance»: the member checks in the design situation fire (issue #46, docs/domain/fire-resistance.md §3.3tervicies).

## 10quater (suite) — fire resistance tab

**`FireCheckTab`** (`calc/tools/fire_check_tab.py`, words in `fire_check_tab_words.py`, prefixes `mc_fire_`, `mc_building_`, `mc_aeai_`), the **last module of «Sécurité structurale»**, in the report alone (every `TABS` key is in `MODE_TABS["rs"]`), with its icon `tab_mc_fire` — a section under a flame. Being last, it took no number from the seven before it; the modules after the group moved one Ctrl+N further.

**One tab of five kinds** (D-01, D-09): built on `MemberCheckTab` and `BucklingLengths`, the same three panes and the same two views (Calcul · Affichage). The base was generalised by three hooks and nothing else (ARCH-RISK-101): **`owns(source)`** — the six tabs of normal temperature list a source of their kind *without* `fire`, this one every source *with* it —, **`current_kind()`** — the draft's kind, the tab's own, here the combo's — and **`resolve_draft(source)`** — here against the card's building. IDs `FE-01`…, unique across every kind (the folder is shared).

**The card «Situation d'incendie (SIA 265 4.5.2)»**: the **kind** (flexion, effort tranchant, flambage, compression de la section, traction), **closed once the check is saved** with a line saying so (a saved check keeps its kind: duplicate it for another); the **fire resistance** in minutes, which **follows VKF 15-15** — «60 min — AEAI 15-15, tableau 2» in the field — and, typed, stays written over: an orange line names what the directive requires (table, row, notes) with «Reprendre» (the pattern of the compression's contact width); without a building the line says to fill the card «Bâtiment» or write the duration, and where the directive requires nothing it says so and the check is not calculable; the **four faces** (dessous, dessus, gauche, droite — D-12), the **wood** (by the grade for softwood; hêtre, chêne ou robinier, revêtement en bois massif) and **«Dernier niveau»** (notes [1], [8]).

**The card «Efforts»**: every kind's rows built once and shown by the kind — N_d,fi (signed for a bending, a magnitude otherwise, the field's minimum following), M_y,d,fi and M_z,d,fi for a bending, V_z,d,fi and V_y,d,fi for a shear, the card «Longueurs de flambage» for a buckling; α hidden (along the grain). Each number may follow a variable (issue #42, `linked`).

**The card «Bâtiment (AEAI 15-15) — pour tout le projet»** (D-10): height category, use, concept, storeys — the directive's own lists, read from `vkf.requirement()` and named in three languages (the German to check against the German directive, issue #34) —, the line «Système porteur : R 60 — AEAI 15-15, tableau 2, ligne 1» and what the engineer still applies himself (notes [2], [3], [4], [6], [7]). **«Enregistrer le bâtiment»** (`save_building`, in `WRITERS`) writes `Woodcore-Datei/building.json` and exports the checks again, so every check that follows takes the new duration; Ctrl+S saves the building too where its card changed, then the check. An incomplete building is refused saying what is missing; a project opened read-only refuses both (`FireCheckTab` is in `GUARDED_TABS`, its `save` being its own). A building changed and not saved travels through a rebuild of the window (`view_state["building"]`).

What follows describes it as it was.* **The results** are `member_check.compute`'s, instant: the fire's rows first (t, β_n, d_char,n, d_red, d_ef, b_fi, h_fi, k_fi), then the check on the residual section, each design value «k_fi · η_w · η_t · …»; «Affichage» compiles the draft's `#member(…, fire: …)` and its check.
