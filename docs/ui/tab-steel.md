# The steel tabs

> «Acier» / «Stahl» / «Steel»: the free profiles and the steel member checks of SIA 263 (issues #50, #51, #52; docs/domain/steel.md §3.3quatervicies).

## 10quater (suite) — steel tabs

**One navigation group «Acier»** (`nav_steel`, D-13), after «Aptitude au service» and before «Référence», with two sub-titles (`NAV_SECTIONS`): **Section** — «Profilés» (`tab_st_profiles`), «Classe de section» (`tab_st_class`) — and **Sécurité structurale** — «Traction», «Compression», «Flexion», «Effort tranchant» (`tab_st_tension`, `tab_st_compression`, `tab_st_bending`, `tab_st_shear`); lot 3 adds the sub-title **Assemblages**. Each tab has its line icon in `chrome.ICONS`; the words are `calc/tools/steel_tab_words.py`'s (prefixes `st_`, `pr_`, `warn_st…`), merged into the window's `STRINGS`. Like the timber checks they are in the report's tabs alone (`MODE_TABS["rs"]`).

**«Profilés»** (`ProfileTab`, `calc/tools/profile_tab.py`, D-02, D-03, D-15): the list of the project's free profiles (`Woodcore-Datei/profiles/`), then the cards **Identité** (name, description, ID `PR-01`…), **Modèle** (a template — I, U, T, L, tube rectangulaire, tôle — and its dimensions, «Dessiner ce modèle»), **Charger un profilé SZS C5** (a family and a size, loaded without its root radii: the line under the button says the area and the inertia fall a little below the table's), **Rectangles** (a table: name, y, z, b, h in mm and the **role** of the wall — «élément plan appuyé aux deux bords (tab. 5a)», «… à un bord (tab. 5b)», «aucun (non classé)» —, chosen in a list while the cell is edited; «Ajouter un rectangle», «Supprimer»). The drawing beside it shows the rectangles and **a drag adds one** on a 1 mm grid. Under the drawing, **Valeurs de section** (A, I, W_el, W_pl, i, the centroid), computed by `domains/profile.py` at each change, and the **SZS C5 library** card, which shows a table's profile (m, A, A_v, I, W, i, K, h, b) for reference. Saving refuses a malformed profile; **renaming or deleting a profile a position or a check cites is refused**, naming who cites it (D-16); a deleted profile is archived.

**The five check tabs** (`SteelCheckTab` and its subclasses `SteelClassTab`, `SteelTensionTab`, `SteelCompressionTab`, `SteelBendingTab`, `SteelShearTab`, `calc/tools/steel_tab.py`) are built on the timber checks' `MemberCheckTab` (D-01): the same three panes, the list, the form and the view (Calcul · Affichage), the same saving, archive, duplicate and command buttons. The base was generalised by class attributes only — `DOMAIN` (the module that reads, checks and exports the sources: `member_check` or `steel_check`), `NORM_PREFIX` («SIA 265 » or «SIA 263 »), `POSITION_HINT_KEY` — and by the overridable `build_material`, `fill_material`, `draft_material` and `window_result`. IDs `QK-01`, `ST-Z01`, `ST-D01`, `ST-B01`, `ST-Q01`, unique across the folder `Woodcore-Datei/steel/`.

**The card «Profilé et acier»** (D-05): the family, the size (a C5 size or a free profile's id) and the grade (a grade of SIA 263 Tabelle 1). The first entry of each combo says what the position holds, «IPE 360 — S010»; another choice writes over it, with an orange line and «Reprendre» (the timber tabs' rule), and a position without a steel section or grade says so. **The card «Efforts de dimensionnement»** shows what the kind reads: N (a tension positive; for a compression the magnitude, stored negative), M_y, M_z, V_z, V_y and for a tension A_net. Each may follow a variable (issue #42). **The view** prints the result `steel_check.compute` gives, the rows `data/steel.json` will hold: the section's class with the governing wall, the resistances, the ratio and the answer, or the reason it cannot be calculated; the preview is the page `#show-check-steel` prints, built once the check is saved and clean.

**Tests**: `tests/test_steel_tab.py` (the group and its six modules, a check following its position saved and exported, a profile written over and taken back, class 4 said and not computed, the class tab, a read-only project, the profiles tab drawing, loading, saving and refusing to delete a cited profile, the words in three languages) and `tests/test_member_check_tab.py` (the timber tabs unchanged by the generalisation).
