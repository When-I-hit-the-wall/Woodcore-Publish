## 10quater (suite) — positions tab, and the list of the positions

⚠️ *Retired by the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (lot 19): `show-position-list` -- write #include "/Positions/position-list.typ" -- the list written once in raw Typst by the Positions tab's card «Fichier de la liste». What follows describes it as it was.* **The list of the positions in «Affichage» (v0.117): title, legend, columns and their widths.** The engineer, 08.10.2026: the table's caption is called **Titre** (it was «Légende du tableau»), a **Légende** is added — a free text set small under the table, as written (`legend:`, content, never looked up in the glossary) —, «les colonnes à afficher peuvent être choisies (à cocher ou décocher), il est également possible de fixer une largeur de colonne (valeur fixe ou en %)», and «le tableau ne doit pas dépasser dans la marge de droite». The panel of `show-position-list` is in three cards (the list, the table, the columns): the new kind of option **`columns`** (`display_options`, `display_view.ColumnsControl`) is one row per column of `POSITION_LIST_COLUMNS` — a check box and a width, `30` or `30mm` for millimetres, `20%` of the text width, empty for what the content needs (`display_options.column_width`) — and writes the two arguments `columns:` (the ticked ones, nothing when all nine are) and `widths:` (a Typst dictionary, `build_reference.typst_value` now writes one); the last column ticked cannot be unticked, and a width that is not one is marked and left out. **No plan ticked prints nothing**: `plans:` is left out of the call and `show-position-list(scope: "plans")` no longer stops the report (the table, its caption and its message are all absent; a storey that holds nothing still says so). **The options chosen are kept while the window is open** (`SpecPanel.state`, `DisplayView.snapshot` / `restore`, `MainWindow.rebuild`): every export ends in a rebuild of the tabs, which gave every panel its defaults back; they are in memory and in no file, the call pasted into the report being the durable form (D-012). ⚠️ **Nine columns do not fit an A4 portrait page at their least**, and the table now keeps to the margin (`render-table`, `fit`, see §3.3 of docs/domain/calculation-domains.md): the widest columns are narrowed first and a long heading or word is hyphenated, the short ones staying whole — choose fewer columns, or give a width, for a table that reads better. ⚠️ **The table's box keeps to the page, its text may not**: a word nothing allows to cut — a web address, an identifier of thirty characters, a legend typed with no space — that is longer than its column still runs out of its cell, and may pass the margin (Typst breaks a word only at a hyphenation point).
**The start, the notices and long work (v0.117, spec `2026-10-07-performance-erreurs-plans-legendes`).** The engineer, 07.10.2026: «Lorsque l'utilisateur lance woodcore.exe il doit immédiatement obtenir un retour visuel» · «Toutes les erreurs doivent être centralisées dans le système global de notifications d'erreurs de Woodcore». **The positions tab (v0.94).** *Positions* (group *Projet*, after the plan; `calc/tools/positions_tab.py`, its words under `po_`) lists the rows of `Positions Element` in sheet order, with a search, and edits the one chosen in one card: its ID — proposed from its type's symbol and a floor by `positions_workbook.propose_id`, and following the type until typed over —, the type, material and strength class from the workbook's own `data_type` lists, the section type from `section.py`'s list, the dimension read live for it with the reason under the field in the danger colour, the moisture class, the units and the description. *Nouvelle position*, *Dupliquer*, *Supprimer* — which asks, naming the plans and schemas that draw the id — and the *Annuler les modifications · Enregistrer* row, the dot of unsaved changes and `view_state` as every editor tab has them. **Every write goes through `calc/positions_workbook.py`** (§7), only the columns changed: an ID taken (ignoring case) or malformed, a missing type or a dimension that does not fit its type refused before anything is written; a rename asked first, the calls and drawings naming the old id being about to break; the `section type` column added at the first write where the workbook lacks it, said in the status line; a workbook Excel holds met with a box giving the reason and *Réessayer*; a project without a Positionen.xlsx given one, from the new-project template, on a yes — the question saying to answer No where the file may only not be synced yet. **After a write the window's own `reexport` runs**, as *Importer Excel* does, so `data/` and every tab follow; if that export fails, the row stays written and the exporter's message is shown, and where another export already runs, the status line says to import afterwards. The workbook is read again whenever the tab is brought to the front. **«Nouvelle position…»** opens the tab on a new row of a letter and a floor (`MainWindow.new_position(symbol, floor)`): from the plan's alert where a tool has no position of its letter (§3.3sexies), with the plan's floor, and from the «+» beside a schema element's Position. The connectors tab has none, a member reaching its position through its schema element. A project opened read-only refuses its *Enregistrer* and *Supprimer* (`WRITERS`). **Since v0.116 the type is shown in the project's language** (§4bis, §7): the combo's data is the STE key and its text the translation, the search finds a position by its translated type, a type typed freely stays as typed and the id's letter does not follow the language. ⚠️ **Since v0.117 two views** (*Saisie · Affichage*, above): *Affichage* offers the list of the positions, `#show-position-list` — all, those of the plans ticked, those of one storey, its caption —, the first call this tab gives. **Lengths and quantities (issue #14).** After the fields, a column «Longueur [m]» -- never a field, never written into positions.json -- gives the length each position is drawn with on every position plan, its elements added up (`quantities.length_by_position` on `quantities.rows`, the plans read by `plan.position_plan_sources`, the export's own reading): empty for a position drawn as a column or a slab, or on a plan without a scale. The list selects several positions (Ctrl, Shift), the form holding the current one. A card «Métrés» under the form adds up what the plans draw by material and section -- pieces, length, volume, and each material's total in bold -- for «Tout ce qui est dessiné», «Un plan» (chosen) or «Positions sélectionnées», read off `quantities.summary`, the summary the report's `show-plan-quantities` prints; its words are the report's own (`quantities.*` of calc/i18n/strings.json). A plan that cannot be read is a warning, and the card empty.

**The card of the list's file in a project open read-only (issue #106).** «Écrire le fichier» and «Ajouter» of the card «Fichier de la liste» (`list_file_card.ListFileCard`, shared with «Métré») are refused there with the window's one message, as «Enregistrer» is: the card declares them as its `WRITERS`, guarded where they are defined (`tab_registry`, `tab_guard.guard_service`). Until then they wrote the list's `.typ` in spite of the lock.

**The «Dimension» section (v0.117).** The engineer, 08.10.2026: «lorsque le type de section est donné, il doit y avoir une section «Dimension» qui apparait avec des textbox différentes à remplir en fonction du type de section: «rec» => largeur, hauteur; «plaque» => épaisseur». Under the section type a card **«Dimension»** holds a box for each number the type has — `rec` a width *b* and a height *h*, `pla` a thickness *t*, `cir` a diameter *d* (`section.dimension_fields`) — and the units; for a type whose dimension is not a few numbers (`clt`'s layers, a steel designation) or no type, the line of text it always was. The boxes are filled from the text where it reads as the type's form (`section.split_dimension`: «100/200», «100 x 200», «100,5/200»), and **write** the line the form reads — `dimension_edit`, which `form_values` still reads — only when one is typed in (`section.join_dimension`: «100/», half typed, keeps its place). **A dimension nobody touched is never rewritten** — a position opened and saved untouched keeps «100 x 200» byte for byte — and a text that does not read as its type (a descriptive one, «100/200» under a plate) stays on its line, with its problem said under it, where it can be mended: the mode (boxes or line) is decided when a position is loaded or its type changes, never at a key, so typing «100/» does not turn the boxes into a line. Nothing about the stored value changes: `dimension` is the same string, validated by `section.parse`, and the export is the same.

**A position saved does not rebuild the window (v0.117).** The engineer, 08.10.2026: «rafraîchir que les onglets qui les lisent». A position written imports **lightly** (`reexport(light=True)`, called by `positions_tab.reimport`): when the export ends well `MainWindow.refresh_lightly` reads the window's data again (`load`), refreshes the line beside the project's name (`meta`) and takes off the banner of workbooks changed since the export (`stale_banner`), then marks every tab but the Positions tab **stale** (`stale_tabs`) and builds the one in front again at once where it is another; a stale tab is built again **when it is next shown and before it is seen** (`refresh_if_stale` from `on_tab_changed`, `refresh_tab`: `removeTab`, the factory, `insertTab`, with the signals of the tab widget blocked), carrying its `view_state()` and its display's options, its views, its layout binding (`bind_layout`) and its mark of unsaved work. «Import données», a PDF, a reload and a language switch stay full rebuilds, and an exception in the light refresh falls back to one. Measured on the example project: the 32 tabs built again take 7 to 10 s, five of them Qt attaching widgets to a shown window; a light refresh builds none (0.01 s, the Positions tab in front) or the one tab in front (0.7 s), and the first visit of a stale tab costs its construction, 0.5 to 1.1 s. ⚠️ **A stale tab holds the old widgets until it is shown**: nothing reads them meanwhile but the autosave of a draft it holds, and the first visit of a tab costs its construction (0.5 to 1.1 s).
