# The plan tabs: the position plan, and what it shares with the fire-protection plan

> *Plan de position*: moved verbatim from [`window.md`](window.md) (§10quater) on 2026-10-10 (issue #31), the file having reached 218 KB. `window.md` keeps the window's frame and the index of the tabs; a reference to `docs/ui/window.md §10quater` finds this file there.

## 10quater (suite) — plan tabs: the position plan, and what it shares with the fire-protection plan

**The position plan tab (v0.34).** *Plan de position / Positionsplan / Position plan* is where a position plan is drawn (§3.3sexies). It lives in modules of its own, since `woodcore_gui.py` had grown past 2 600 lines: `calc/tools/plan_tab.py` holds the tab and its texts in the three languages, which woodcore_gui.py merges into its `STRINGS` and lists in `TABS`, and `calc/tools/plan_canvas.py` the drawing surface. **Moving, copying, construction aids (issue #18).** The first row of the bar carries «Déplacer» and «Copier» -- the object selected taken by a base point, then put where the next click says, Shift keeping 0°, 45° or 90°; «Copier» putting down a copy at every click until Escape, the original still selected, every copy one step of «Annuler» -- and four construction aids, «Aide —», «Aide |», «Aide 2 pts» and «Aide ∥» (parallel to the axis, aid or element side clicked, at a distance asked in metres where the plan has a scale, on the side of the next click): thin dashed blue lines across the sheet, snapped to behind «Accrocher aux axes», selected, dragged and deleted as an axis is, saved with the plan and never printed (`calc/tools/plan_canvas_edit.py`, `calc/domains/lines.py`, `plan.translate_entry`, `plan.copy_entry`). At the foot of the left pane, «Objet sélectionné» -- «Dupliquer» (a copy a fiftieth of the frame's diagonal down and right, selected) and «Supprimer» (the selection card's «Effacer» and Delete, one action) --, closed with nothing selected, in «Dessin» alone. The fire-protection plan has «Déplacer», «Copier» and «Objet sélectionné», and no aid (§13).

**What issue #9 changed in the position plan tab** (08.10.2026; spec `docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-08-plan-positions-cotes-numerotation-sections.md`). **A drawing tool chosen shows its element's options at once** in the right pane, as if one of its type were selected -- the number it will give («S104»), the *Section*, the variant, the angle, the length shown, a slab's diagonal --, and what is set there is the next element's (`PlanCanvas.tool_defaults`, `plan.apply_defaults`); while a drawing tool is chosen the pane is the tool's, with «Sélection» the selection's (D-014; `calc/tools/plan_properties.py`). **The *Section* stands above the position**: «Reprendre de» copies another position's type, dimension and units -- a copy, never a link --, or the type and its fields (`section_fields`); a new element proposes the section of the last of its type, and the choice is kept in the draft (`_pending_positions`, a step of «Annuler») and written with the plan. **A column turns by the circle its selection shows**, dragged (Shift by 15°, one step of «Annuler», the angle field following). **The ruler** (in the navigation tools, on the fire plan too) shows the distance of two clicks and writes nothing; Escape clears it. **The dimension tools** -- horizontal, vertical, aligned, a chain (Enter or a double-click ends its points), an angle -- with the static models' symbols; a dimension is taken by its line, its line dragged, deleted with the selection. The scale module's «Cotes» shows them or not in «Dessin» (the engineer's setting, `plan_draw_dimensions`); «Affichage» and «Export» say it in their command (`dimensions:`, `lengths:`, cases of the builder and of the sheet). **Numbers**: an element drawn takes the next number on its storey, nothing written until «Enregistrer»; a position no plan draws any more is offered for deletion (`renumber_dialog`: the numbers that move, every occurrence in the files to untick, «Copier les valeurs» for a cited one, «Garder la position»). The sheet editor adds **the quantities** («Ajouter les métrés», `sheet-quantities`). The Positions tab deletes and renames through the same renumbering.

On the left:
- the saved plans, with *Nouveau* (which asks for the background at once), *Dupliquer* (its numbers cleared, a copy being another plan) and *Supprimer*. A plan not named yet is listed with the file and page it is drawn on. Since v0.39, a PDF of several pages chosen for a new plan opens *Pages de …* (`PageDialog`): its pages as thumbnails, each over its number, rendered in QtPdf's thread as they come, of which one or several are clicked. Every page's size is read before the first thumbnail is asked for (v0.48): a size is read under the lock a render holds, so reading them one per thumbnail opened the dialog only once the thumbnails were nearly all rendered — 0.26 s now for a set of 25 sheets. One page opens as the draft, as before. Several make as many plans, `P01`, `P02`, …, saved and exported at once, since the tab edits one draft at a time, and the first is opened; the status line names each with its page;
- the open plan's elements and axes, the axes in the order of their names, with a ⚠ beside a number that needs a look;
- the numbers Positionen.xlsx lacks, with *Copier pour Excel*. Since v0.45 these can only be positions renamed or removed in Excel after they were drawn, and the plan does not save until they are replaced. ⚠️ Since issue #9 (v0.117) a number the project lacks is no longer refused: *Enregistrer* makes its position, with the plan (`plan_number_unknown`).

In the middle, the plan: a PDF page, an image or an SVG. Over it lie the report's own symbols and the crop's frame, with what lies outside it dimmed. The wheel zooms about the pointer (v0.39, `zoom_at`): Qt's own anchor follows the last point its `mouseMoveEvent` saw, which this view handles without it, so it zoomed about the sheet's corner and threw the plan there at every step. A PDF page is rendered twice over, in QtPdf's threads (v0.39), **the part in view first** (v0.48):
- the part in view, at the screen's own resolution, each time the view stops (`sharpen`, `PageTile`) — and at once, without a margin, when a plan is opened, that being the render waited for. It is drawn pixel for pixel, so the architect's lines are as sharp as in a PDF viewer at every zoom, and no render is much larger than the screen;
- then the whole page, 1200 px along its longer side (`BASE_RENDER_PX`), which shows while the view moves and wherever the sharp render does not reach.

**A render of an architect's sheet takes time, and the time grows faster than the pixels** (v0.48): 3.5 to 8 s for a page of this project's plan set at 2500 px, about 1 s at 1200, and 0.3 to 0.8 s for the part of it a plan's crop shows. What made a plan slow to open was therefore the order — the whole page at 2500 px first, the view behind it, so nothing was sharp for 4 to 9 s — and what made the window freeze is that **QtPdf runs every call into PDFium under one lock**, the renders in its thread included. Loading the next plan's document waited for the render on its way to end (7.9 s measured), and a render once asked for cannot be taken back. So (`PdfPages` in `plan_canvas.py`) one render is asked for at a time, the view before the whole page, a newer request takes the place of the one of its kind still waiting, and a document is loaded or closed only while nothing renders (`collect`): the document is kept when the next plan is on the same file — the four plans of this project are pages of one — and a plan on another file waits for the render on its way before its file is loaded, showing meanwhile whatever is kept of it. `clip_bias()` is measured when the canvas is built, for the same reason: it renders.

**A render made once is kept** (v0.48), under the file, its size and the time it was written, the page, and the size and part rendered — so a plan set the architect replaces is rendered afresh. In memory for the session (`RenderCache`, 256 MB, the oldest go first), and on disk in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Woodcore\renders` (`DiskCache`, PNG, ~0.3 MB a page, 300 MB at most, the least recently used removed), where the whole page and the first view of each page go. The folder is outside the project, which NextCloud syncs, and beside the settings rather than in them; deleting it costs a render. A plan seen before therefore shows in under a tenth of a second, in the session and after a restart alike, and where the window has changed size since, the nearest render kept shows at once, a little soft, while the view's own is rendered (`kept_tile`). The scroll bars are always shown, so that fitting a plan gives the same view every time and finds its render: appearing as a plan was fitted, they made the view smaller than the one fitted, and the same plan got two scales.

The whole page used to be rendered once more at each zoom, up to 9000 px, so it blurred at a close zoom and was scaled down with aliasing at a far one. One Qt quirk had to be worked round: QtPdf 6.11 scales a clipped render by its rectangle's `right() - left()`, a pixel short of its width, which put a 1000 px render two pixels off the page's at its far side. The rectangle is asked for a pixel larger, and `clip_bias()` measures once, on a line it draws, whether this Qt needs that. Checked against PDFium's render of the whole page at the same scale, on the architect's page and on one turned by /Rotate with a crop box: identical. The tools sit in two rows, and each row **wraps** onto another line where the pane is narrower than it (v0.67, `flowbar.py`):
- *Sélection*: click an element or an axis; drag a corner, an element or an axis, or a position number to move it clear of what it hides (v0.42, below); Suppr deletes. And *Vue*.
- *Axe vertical* and *Axe horizontal* (one click), *Axe biais* (two clicks). A new axis is named by where it lies (§3.3sexies, `plan.insert_axis`), and the status line names the axes it moved up (`B → C`); *Annuler* undoes the renaming with the axis.
- *Cadrage* (drag a rectangle) and *Calibrer* (two clicks, then the real length).
- **Accrocher aux axes** and **Accrocher aux éléments**, two toggles. A point being drawn, or a corner being dragged, snaps in this order of preference: an axes' crossing, a corner or point, a crossing of an axis or element with an element, a middle, then onto an axis or an element (plan.py's `SnapIndex`). A marker shows what it took.
- The eleven STE tools, each drawn with its own symbol, and the variant of the one chosen. Each can also be chosen by its letter while the plan has the focus (O for Ö). ⚠️ **Since issue #9 (v0.117) each element drawn takes the next number of its letter on the plan's storey** -- S101, S102 …, S201 -- and a number is one object; its position is made at *Enregistrer*, with the plan, and nothing is written while drawing (§3.3sexies, docs/domain/position-plans.md). Until then each tool drew the same number on every element, drew nothing where Positionen.xlsx had no position of its letter (v0.45) and opened an alert saying so (v0.47, `on_refused()`, removed with the rule).
- *Annuler* and *Rétablir* (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y, 200 steps; a step that changes nothing is not kept), and *Ajuster* (F).

**The bar sets its own width, and not the tab's (v0.67).** The three panes — the plans and the elements, the plan itself, the properties — are dragged by the handles between them, and until v0.67 they could not be. The bar was one grid, so it asked for the width of its widest row and the centre pane handed that to the splitter as its minimum: 892 px, beside 258 on the left and 202 on the right, which in a 1500 px window is about a hundred pixels of play. A handle then either refused to move or took its width from the pane on the far side — what the engineer saw as the display jumping. Each row is a `FlowLayout` of its own now (`calc/tools/flowbar.py`, shared with the static models' bar), so a row too wide for its pane takes a second line, the centre pane's minimum is the widest single button, and no tool is ever cut off. ⚠️ The side panes still stop where their own buttons do — 258 px on the left, 240 on the right — which is their contents' floor and not the bar's; narrowing them further would mean the three buttons under each list wrapping as well. ⚠️ A row wrapped costs the plan about 26 px of height, which is v0.66's trade made in the other direction: there the height was precious and the bar scrolled sideways, here the width is and the bar grows downwards.

A line ends on a double-click, on Enter or on a click on its last point. A beam's (T) or a bracing member's (A) line ends on its second click, and a second click on its first end is let go (v0.45). An area ends on a click on its first corner. Échap cancels and Retour arrière removes the last point. **Shift keeps a segment horizontal, vertical or at 45°**; with snapping on, only a target on that ray is taken, so a wall drawn with Shift stops exactly on the axis it reaches. Under the plan sit the pointer's coordinates (in metres from the crop's corner when there is a scale) with what it snaps to, the tool's hint, and the warnings. All three keep a fixed height, so the plan does not move under the pointer as they change.

**A number is moved by dragging it (v0.42).** With *Sélection*, the pointer turns into the move cross over a position number. Pressing on it selects its element and takes the number, not the element under it: numbers are drawn over every symbol, so a click lands on what is seen on top, and a corner of the selected element still comes first. Dragging moves the number alone, and Shift keeps the move horizontal, vertical or at 45°. Dropped back within a few pixels of its own place, the number goes back to following its element (`label_offset` removed). No snapping applies: it would pull a number onto the very things it is moved away from. The selected element's number is outlined with the element, and one drag is one step of *Annuler*. In the selection panel, a line under the number says it is in its own place, or offers «Remettre à sa place» (`reset_label`). *Enregistrer* writes the source as 1.1, whatever version it was read as. The element itself is grabbed away from its number, and its moved number goes with it.

On the right:
- the plan's id, label and floor;
- the background: *Choisir…*, which starts in `01_Pläne` and refuses a file outside the project, then its page and its visibility. *Pages…* beside the page opens the thumbnails (v0.39). The page turns as in a document: the down arrow, the Down key and the wheel turned down go to the next page, and up goes to the previous one (`PageSpinBox`);
- the scale (none, the PDF's 1:n, or calibrated) and the walls' lengths;
- **three sliders**, for the size of the strokes, of the points and of the texts;
- the crop;
- the selection. For an element: its type, variant and number, chosen from a list of Positionen.xlsx's positions of its letter with their summaries, the number given becoming the one its tool draws from then on (until v0.117: since issue #9 the tool proposes the next free number instead). Since v0.45 the number can no longer be typed, and the list offers the sheet's positions only; a number the sheet lacks shows as `T009 (?)` until one of them is chosen (⚠️ since issue #9 such a number is one *Enregistrer* will make, and the *Section* field stands above it); a slab's span (its short side by default) and diagonal. For an axis: its name, angle and bubbles, and, where there is a scale, its distance in metres from the previous parallel axis, editable, and *Axe parallèle à … m*, named by where it lies like one drawn;
**And the size it is drawn at** (v0.99): under the selection's own properties, a card **«Taille propre»** carries the factors the element or the axis chosen states over the plan's three — one row per factor that entry may state and the others not shown at all, a factor it does not draw being no field to type into (`figure_style.ENTRY_KEYS`). It is shown the way an overridden b/h is (v0.75, `calc/tools/stylefactors.py`): while the entry states none the slider stands at 100 % and the entry follows the plan; the moment it states one, the row's name and its readout are written in the orange a written-over value is written in (`theme.colour("override")`) and **«Reprendre»** appears beside it, which takes the factor off again. ⚠️ **Setting the slider back to 100 % does not take it off** — the b/h rule of v0.75, «typing the very value the position holds still writes it over» — and «Reprendre» is the one way back, which is what keeps an engineer from thinking he has undone something he has not. One drag is one step of «Annuler», as the plan's own sliders are, and the orange follows the slider while it is still held (`show_entry_style`), where rebuilding the panel would delete the very slider under the pointer. ⚠️ The card is a card inside the selection's card, as a static model's selection sets a support or a load (v0.95), and the selection's own rows became a `cards.form` with it, so a long label goes above its field rather than setting the card's width as the sum of the two: measured in the window's own fonts, in French, the properties pane's minimum is **263 px whether nothing is selected, an element is, or a factor is stated with «Reprendre» shown** — unchanged, and `tests/test_panes.py` still measures 584 px of play between the three panes.

- the calls to copy: `show-plan` whole, without legend, at 60% and without axes, the legend alone; for the selected position, the plan with its halo, the detail around it and a `calc-block`; and the loop and the import line.

*Enregistrer* checks the draft by plan.py's rules and refuses an id already taken, ignoring case. It asks before a rename, writes `plans/<id>.json` with the background's hash, and exports the plans at once by `plan.export()`; the warnings show under the plan. As in the polar moments tab, the draft survives a language switch, and leaving it or closing the window asks first.

**The plan tabs, v0.117 (same spec).** **Magnétisme** is a module of the right pane while nothing is selected (`DrawingTab.add_snap_module`: snapping to the axes and to the elements, the session's, in both plan tabs), no more two buttons of the tool bar. **A column turned** (source 1.4): its angle and «↻ 90°» in its card, R on the plan. **The lengths**: «Longueurs des éléments» in the scale's card — the walls, the beams and each side of the slabs —, and «Longueur: comme le plan / affichée / masquée» in the card of a wall, a beam or a slab (`show_length`). **«Coupe…»** writes the link and opens the section chosen in the Dessin view, the plan's storey chosen and the storey line armed — or the box, the line drawn already, or the line selected, both drawn —, «← P01» in the section's row going back. **The position list** of a drawn object names the type in the window's language (`positions_store.type_label`). The tab is «Plan de positions»; the view that shows the compiled `#show-plan` is named «Affichage», as every tab's display view (`views.WORDS["typst"] = "view_display"`, the key staying `typst`). **The Export view**: a document's folder chosen shows, in the centre, all its pages — its sheets' previews compiled where missing and put end to end, in a task, QtPdf's multi-page view — and, on the right, its id, its name, its description and its storey (`bundle_view.BundleCard`), each the bundle's own or, empty, its first plan's in grey (`sheets.bundle_fields`); the scale list stays open (an edit that changes nothing writes and rebuilds nothing); a list of the positions and a legend have a handle that sets their width.

**The plan tabs since v0.116 — one pane model for the position plan and the fire-protection plan** (§2 rule 16, §13; `calc/tools/drawing_tab.py` for what both share, `plan_tab.py` and `fire_tab.py` for what each adds). ⚠️ **The left pane** was, from v0.116 to v0.117 (boxes of their own height since, below), a vertical, non-collapsible splitter of its four boxes — *Documents*, *Plans de position* (*Plan de protection incendie* in the other tab; the engineer: «il faudra renommer plan en plan de position»), *Éléments et axes* and *Documents export* — inside the scrolling column, so each box's height is dragged and the pane scrolls up and down where they do not fit (`left_splitter` in `view_state`). ⚠️ **The tool bar no longer holds *Cadrage* and *Calibrer***: they are cards of the right pane. **The right pane follows the selection** (`DrawingTab.add_module`, `insert_module`, `update_modules`, `has_selection`, `visible_modules`, `MODULE_WHEN`): with **nothing selected** it shows *Plan* (name, description, ID, type, storey, numbering character), *Fond*, **Cadrage** with «Dessiner le cadre», then **Échelle** with «Calibrer…» — which sets the calibrated mode —, *Taille des symboles* (and, in the fire tab, *Légende*) and **Superposition**; with **an entry selected** it shows that entry's card, with its *Taille propre*, and *Taille des symboles* — and nothing of the plan, the background, the scale or the crop —, a storey line or box having a selection card of its own (type, storey, «Effacer»). The crop and the calibration are greyed only for a fire plan that follows a position plan. ⚠️ **Since v0.117 the tab has four views** (above), *Import · Dessin · Typst · Export*, each one of the stacks' kinds (`drawing_tab.VIEW_KIND`, one table, review A08) and each showing only its boxes on the left (`VIEW_BOXES`): the documents in *Import*; the plans and what the plan open holds in *Dessin*; the plans alone in *Typst* — a plan chosen there and not made, its list being the one choice of the plan the command shows, the builder's own drop-down hidden (review A09) —, the command and its page in the middle and its options on the right; the export sheets and their PDF bundles in *Export*. The «Commandes Typst» row is gone: it is the *Typst* view. **The left pane's boxes have their own height** since v0.117: the vertical splitter filled the height it was given — nothing scrolled — and a handle dragged took its height from the box beside it (T6, T8); each box is a `cards.SizedBox` whose grip along its bottom edge sets its height and its alone, never below what it needs, the column scrolling where they do not fit; the heights travel through a rebuild in `view_state` (`left_boxes`). **An arrow at the bottom left folds the pane away** (`chrome.FoldButton`, the navigation's own, its tips `pane_collapse`/`pane_expand`; `set_left_folded`), and back to the width it had. ⚠️ **Since issue #10 the arrow is every tab's** (`chrome.FoldablePane`, put round each tab's first pane by `woodcore_gui.fold_left_pane`; the plan tabs build theirs in `build_list`, `left_pane` being that object), remembered folded in the setting `left_folded`, and the pane narrows to `cards.PANE_FLOOR` (120 px) or what its contents still hold after `cards.loosen` -- a button's text cut with an ellipsis, a title wrapped, a number box squeezed to a few digits (`SPIN_LEAST`) --, never with a button cut at its edge: a row that wraps (`flowbar.FlowLayout`, Structure's buttons) narrows an item alone on its row and wider than it to the row, never below its own minimum (tests/test_left_pane_fold.py, measured on each tab shown -- a tab never shown keeps the widths its buttons had before they were squeezed).

⚠️ **Tracking, as the static models' tab has it** (`calc/domains/plan_tracking.py`, pure, and `PlanCanvas`; the fire canvas inherits it). Resting the pointer on a snap point for **350 ms** (`ACQUIRE_MS`; the static models' tab waits 220) **acquires** it — it is marked by a small cross —, and from an acquired point dotted rays run at the eight polar directions of the sheet's frame (y down) and at the axis and the normal of a wall or an axis meeting there; the cursor **locks** to a ray within a tolerance, and the preference is a plan point, then the crossing of two rays, then a ray through a plan point or the crossing of a ray with an axis, a wall or a storey edge, then the bare ray; three points are kept, the newest first. **Tab** types a distance (in metres where the plan has a scale, else in sheet units) and Enter puts the point there; Esc cancels the typing, then the drawing, then the acquired points, then the tool; a right click does the same and finally selects nothing. Shift keeps the old H/V/45° and tracks nothing; with both snap buttons off, nothing tracks. The same tracking applies while a vertex, a storey line's end or a storey box's corner is **dragged** (the entry dragged is not its own target), and the snap index (`PlanSnapIndex`) now holds the ends of the storey lines, the corners of the storey boxes, the middles of their edges and the edges. In the fire tab a surface's and a polygon's tools snap as well.

⚠️ **Plan type, load symbols and load level in the window** (§3.3sexies). A «Type de plan» combo under the ID chooses *Coupe en plan*, *Coupe en élévation* or *Plan de descente de charges*, and the tool bar follows it: a section offers the storey line, the storey box and the load transfer level (*Niveau de reprise*: two clicks, Shift horizontal, a red arrow at each end and its movable label) beside the usual tools, a load-path plan the six load symbols (each a tool with its sub-kind combo), and a selected load has a card with its number and «Direction horizontale». **Superposition** is a module of its own, `calc/tools/plan_superposition.py` («Superposition», «Überlagerung», «Overlay»), shown only when nothing is selected: a combo of the tab's other plans («(aucun)»), «Avec les éléments dessinés», an opacity slider (one drag, one step of «Annuler»), a colour combo, «Éléments dans cette couleur aussi», «Aligner…» (two clicks) and «Réinitialiser l'alignement». How the colour is read is the engineer's: **Gris alone** shows the plan's drawing grey with its elements in their colours, with the switch all in grey; **Rouge alone** shows the plan red and its elements in their own colours. The plan shown is drawn at 2000 px, multiplied over the background, **above the background and below the open plan's own symbols**, and nothing of it is selectable.

⚠️ **The document view and the layers** (`calc/tools/document_view.py`). Three tools, **Sélection** (the default; Esc returns to it), **Cadrer** and **Calibrer**. A frame is selected by a click and several by Ctrl and Shift, deleted by Delete, resized by dragging an edge or a corner (5 px), and **opened on a double click** (`planActivated`); choosing a frame makes its plan the open plan — the previous one is saved — and moves its *Plan* module into the document pane, where it is saved as each field is left and a rename is asked for when the frame is left. A layer toggled updates the PDF in place and the window shows it in under a second, a combination seen before in a fifth of that (§3.3sexies); the export of the plans on the document follows 1.2 s after the last toggle and is finished before anything else exports. **The documents are listed per tab**: the position tab does not see what the fire tab imported and the other way round (`DrawingTab.DOC_OWNER`).

⚠️ **Export sheets** (`calc/sheets.py`, `calc/tools/sheet_editor.py`, `sheet_list.py`, `lib/sheet.typ`). The editor's **right pane follows the selection**: with nothing selected a sheet shows *Feuille* (format, orientation, margin) and **Options** — one switch each for the title block and the legend, then buttons stacked one under the other that **add** a plan view, a section or a text (a fire sheet: a fire plan or a text) and a list of the positions, each asking which plan —, and **adding selects nothing**; with an object selected only **its** module shows: *Titre* for a title (*Texte* for a text), *Vue en plan* (the title's switch and a single highlighted position chosen in a drop-down of those on the plan, or none), the title block's cells, the legend, the section, the position list (its scope: plans, all, a storey). The kind combo is gone: a sheet is of one kind, chosen when it is made by the tab it is made in (`DrawingTab.SHEET_KIND`, `"position"` and `"fire"`). **The export list** (`SheetList`, a `GroupedList`) shows **PDF bundles as folders** — the look of v0.116's left pane, sheets in the bundle's order beneath, root sheets beside them, an empty bundle shown, **no Archives row** —, created, renamed and deleted from the folder button and menu, and a drop moves a whole selection of sheets into a bundle, within it, between bundles and back to the root. «Nouveau PDF groupé» and «Exporter le PDF groupé» (in a thread, `Plans/<name>.pdf`) and, in the position tab, «Créer une liste des positions» stand under the list. ⚠️ **A bundle is one extra PDF**: each sheet keeps its own PDF and the bundle merges them with pypdf, each sheet compiled with its own `.final()` states, pages in the bundle's order, the file named after the bundle; a bundle that cannot be read is never overwritten, a sheet's id and a bundle's name never collide, and archiving a sheet takes it out of its bundles.

**The Text tool** (issue #43, v0.117; `docs/domain/variable-links.md` §3.3duovicies-bis). After the construction aids in the first row, *Texte*, its symbol a T: a click puts a text down, selected, and the tool goes back to «Sélection»; a click on a text takes it before anything under it, and a drag moves it; «Déplacer» and «Copier» take it, «Effacer» and Delete remove it, and it turns with its page. Selected, the pane on the right shows its content (a line per line, an `_` setting a subscript, a brace typed twice), «Insérer une variable» — the «Variables» tab in its choosing mode, every number of the export and of a «Formules» domain offered whatever its unit, the one chosen inserted where the cursor stands as a token `{1}` — and a card per variable, its symbol and domain, «valeur» or «symbole = valeur», and «Retirer» (`calc/tools/text_pane.py`, the four engines' one). A variable that can no longer be read is in the warning colour and prints its last value. Its size is the selection's own `text` factor. A plan that holds a text is saved at 1.7.
