# The «Formules» tab

> *Formules*: moved verbatim from [`window.md`](window.md) (§10quater) on 2026-10-10 (issue #31), the file having reached 218 KB. `window.md` keeps the window's frame and the index of the tabs; a reference to `docs/ui/window.md §10quater` finds this file there.

## 10quater (suite) — «Formules» tab

**Since v0.119 the tab is «Domaines»** (DE *Domänen*, EN *Domains*, `tab_formula`; issue #64, spec docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-15-structure-projet-dossiers.md, UI-001): in a project laid out by folders (docs/architecture/layout.md §11) a domain the engineer writes lies in `Domains/<id>.typ` -- `Formules/` in a project laid out before, read as it always was -- and is printed by `#include "/Domains/<id>.typ"`. **The list shows besides, in groups of their own, the domains of the specialised tabs written out whole** -- a member check unfolded in «Traction» (`Timber-checks/Tension/<id>.typ`), and as each tab is unfolded in its turn «Neige», «Vent» … (REQ-016, `formula_domain.group_of`, `formula_tab_domains.group_label`): a group the engineer does not move, there being no general group of checks; a stub, which prints its domain by one call and holds no `#var-domain` (calc/domains/domain_file.py), is no domain of the tab and is not listed. An id is one for the whole project, a file of every folder of domains taken (`formula_domain._taken`). Everything below that names `Formules/` reads `Domains/` in a project laid out by folders.

**The «Formules» tab (v0.100, retired in 0.117).** v0.100's *Formules* turned a member's checks written in keyword lines into `#member` and `#verify-*` calls. It was retired on the engineer's request of 08.10.2026 and is kept in `archive/formules-dsl/` -- its paragraph as it stood is `archive/formules-dsl/docs/ui/window-formules-tab.md`. Its place in *Référence* is the new *Formule* tab (below).

**The «Formules» tab (v0.117, reworked by issue #15).** *Formules* (DE *Formeln*, EN *Formulas*; group *Référence*, first, before *Variables*; `calc/tools/formula_tab.py`, its words under `fm_`; specs docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-08-onglet-formule-domaines.md and docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-08-onglet-formules-editeur-dossiers-include.md). The engineer, 08.10.2026: «écrire des calculs de manière simplifiée et que ça en fasse des commandes Typst». **Every calculation is in a domain**, and a domain is one file, `Formules/<id>.typ` at the top of the Typst root, **its id the file's name** (docs/typst/formulas.md; calc/domains/formula_domain.py): the tab reads it again each time it is shown, so what VS Code wrote is what it shows, and writes it at every block validated, every option changed and every pause in the typing -- there is nothing else to save; a file written by hand in another form, or whose `#var-domain` names another id than its file, is said and written back in Woodcore's; one that cannot be read is shown read only. **On the left the domains**, by the list every editor tab shares (`grouped_list.GroupedList`, §10quater below): each under its name, unfolding into what it computes -- `q_d = 4.5 kN/m`, `M_d = 14.063 kNm`, a relation and its ✔ --; **folders**, made, renamed and removed as in *Schémas*, and **the order the engineer drops**, both kept in `Woodcore-Datei/lists.json` (calc/list_order.py) -- the files stay flat in `Formules/`, a folder being no directory --; **«Archives»** at the bottom, a domain deleted there (`Formules/archives/`), restored by a drop out of it. A click opens a domain; the left pane only navigates (the engineer corrected himself: «la commande domaine est en fait dans le volet de droite»). **In the middle the domain open, as one text being written** (issue #40, spec docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-11-formules-blocs-en-ligne-et-niveau-manuel.md): its name on the first line -- chosen, the right pane shows the domain's card; Enter or ↓ keeps the name and offers the commands --, then **one text** (`formula_tab_widgets.FormulaText`, a `QTextEdit`) of the engineer's own Typst (a title `= …`, a sentence, a formula `$ … $`), coloured as the «Python» tab colours code (`richtext.TypstHighlighter`), printed as typed and never computed, **and each command validated a pill in it** -- a small rectangle with rounded corners (`PillPainter`, the scheme's `accent-soft` and `line-strong`, `accent` for the one chosen, `danger` for one refused) holding its shown variable, `=`, its value and its unit: `q_d = 4.5 kN/m`, an equation's answer alone (its letters and numbers are a double-click and «Affichage» away, D-01), a relation, its two values and ✔ or ✘. **A pill stands where Typst prints its command** (calc/domains/formula_layout.py, derived from the call and never stored): inline in its sentence -- the text before it, the pill, the text after it on one line -- unless `note: "line"` on a `var`, `var-unit` or `eq` sets it on a line of its own; a `val` and a `verify` are always in their sentence. `note:` changed in «Affichage» moves the pill, nothing else rewritten. The file keeps its form -- one command a line, the text between them as typed --: a text line, a command printed inline and the next line are one sentence for Typst, so one paragraph of the pane; **a blank line beside a command is Typst's new paragraph**, kept as it is written (formula_domain.tidy_items), and **Enter beside a pill writes one**. A pill is **moved by a drag** -- dropped elsewhere in the text, its command moves there in the file; a drop inside an equation, a raw block, a call, a heading, a list's item or a comment is refused with its reason, and one above what the command is computed from with the domain's --; deleted with the text round it, it is the command taken away; a copy carries the text alone. What the file would not read back -- a `#var`, `#eq`, `#var-unit` or `#verify` typed inside a text (Typst would compute it, Python would not), a line of tags, a bracket too many -- is refused with its reason, nothing written. **A command the engineer wrote inside a line in the file** (v0.119, spec docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-14-commandes-en-ligne-domaine.md) -- `- $0°$ bis $30°$: #eq(…)`, `#var(…) --- Auflast` -- is read as a pill **glued** to the text beside it: it stands in that text's paragraph even in a list's item or a heading, keeps its line through every write (an option changed included), and is glued to nothing on a side where an Enter puts it at the start or the end of a paragraph (formula_layout.from_paragraphs). Dropping a pill into a list's item or a heading is still refused (D-03). A command of the palette goes **where the cursor of the text stands**, the sentence cut there; otherwise after the pill chosen, or at the end. **A double-click opens a pill as its row of labelled fields on a line of its own, the text parted round it** (D-04), closed into a pill again at the validation: `var` -- **the shown variable first**, then the computation variable, **written from it as it is typed** (`M_(y,d)` → `M_y_d`, formula_domain.key_from_symbol) until the engineer goes into it with Tab and writes his own, then the value and the unit --; `eq`: the shown variable `=` the calculation; `verify`: the relation; `val`; `var-unit`. Tab goes to the next field and **Enter -- or a click anywhere else in the middle -- validates** (a click on the right pane, the list or the name line validates nothing; a block that does not validate stays open with its reason, one added and left empty is taken away); a single click on a pill chooses it, its options on the right; Escape giving up. **A calculation is typed with the names the tab shows, numbers, and a unit after a number** -- `12 N / 3000 mm` is written `(12 "N") / (3000 "mm")`, a decimal comma a point -- and **computed at once in Python** (calc/units.py, held equal to the report's algebra by tests/test_units.py): a block the report would refuse is refused with the report's own reason and nothing is written; the domain's variables are in the «Variables» tab the moment they are saved, under the domain's id. **On the right, one column that scrolls** (the engineer: «les deux. le volet pour se défiler»): **the domain's variables**, always -- a click writes the one clicked into the calculation being typed, by its shown name (a click on a variable in the list on the left does the same); «Importer…» brings the «Variables» tab forward in its choosing mode --; **the options of the block chosen**, a single click away whether it is being written or validated -- since v0.117 (issue #19) only what the line *is*: its label, its description, its norm and its clause (display_options.SPECS of `var`, `eq`, `verify`, `val`, `var-unit`, `for_view("calc")`), and for an equation its **output unit** among the units of its result's dimension; for a block validated an option changed is written into the file once the typing pauses, over the options «Affichage» wrote, which stay (`merged_options`) --; **the domain's card** while its line is chosen: its **ID** -- made from the name until the domain is first written, or typed by hand; changed later, it renames the file and rewrites `#var-domain`, and Woodcore lists, without rewriting them, the lines of the project that still cite the old id (`#include "/Formules/<old>.typ"`, `domain: "<old>"`) --, its file, «Dupliquer le domaine» (a copy «… (copie)» written at once beside it, in its folder) and «Supprimer le domaine…» (into «Archives»); and **the commands to add**, always -- «Domaine» first, which makes a domain whose name is then typed. **Calcul · Affichage**: «Affichage» shows the page `#include "/Formules/<id>.typ"` prints -- the domain's title a heading under the section's, each command at its own level or the report's -- and copies that line. **A line chosen on the left** -- a `var`, an `eq`, a `verify`, a `val`, a `var-unit` -- comes first in the drop-down since v0.117 (issue #19): every argument of how it is printed -- its level of detail, its **type d'affichage** (`note:`, «Libellé et citation» until then), its **séparateur** (`sep:`, the separator printed after a value set inline, spec docs/requirements/active/2026-10-11-argument-sep-var.md), its style, the summary table, a `val`'s value and unit (`for_view("display")`) --, each change written into its call in the file at once (`write_display`, a writer the read-only guard refuses) and the page compiled again after it; the call to copy stays the `#include`, and the line stays chosen through the save. A line is found again by what it is -- its kind, its key, how many of the same before it -- and not by its place. **A `val` is in italics** in the list, in both views: a variable shown again, not one made.

**An unfolded check's card (v0.118, issue #62, UI-002).** A domain written by Woodcore from a member check -- its `#var-domain` carries `gen:` -- shows on the right, below the variables, the card «Vérification dépliée» (`formula_tab_unfold.FormulaUnfoldMixin`): the check it is written from, whether its form is still the export's, and every line that is not in step with its state -- «modifiée hors de Woodcore», «donnée changée», «conflit», «valeur de l'ingénieur», «ligne supprimée» (`unfold.status`) --, each pill's outline drawn in that state's colour (`override`, `warning`, `danger`; `PILL_STATE_COLOURS`). Its actions are the domain's (calc/domains/unfold.py), each a writer refused in a project opened read only: «Importer» and «Tout importer», «Reprendre», «Réinsérer», «Garder la mienne» and «Prendre celle de l'export» in a conflict, «Régénérer» while the form no longer matches. A domain written by hand shows no such card. The tab writes back only what it changed (docs/typst/formulas.md §10quinquies, D-17).
