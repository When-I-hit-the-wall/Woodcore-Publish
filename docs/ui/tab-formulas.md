# The «Domaines» tab

> *Formules*: moved verbatim from [`window.md`](window.md) (§10quater) on 2026-10-10 (issue #31), the file having reached 218 KB. `window.md` keeps the window's frame and the index of the tabs; a reference to `docs/ui/window.md §10quater` finds this file there.

## 10quater (suite) — «Domaines» tab (formerly «Formules»)

**Since v0.119 the tab is «Domaines»** (DE *Domänen*, EN *Domains*, `tab_formula`; issue #64, spec docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-15-structure-projet-dossiers.md, UI-001): in a project laid out by folders (docs/architecture/layout.md §11) a domain the engineer writes lies in `Domains/<id>.typ` -- `Formules/` in a project laid out before, read as it always was -- and is printed by `#include "/Domains/<id>.typ"`. **The list shows besides, in groups of their own, the domains of the specialised tabs written out whole** -- a member check unfolded in «Traction» (`Timber-checks/Tension/<id>.typ`), and as each tab is unfolded in its turn «Neige», «Vent» … (REQ-016, `formula_domain.group_of`, `formula_tab_domains.group_label`): a group the engineer does not move, there being no general group of checks; a stub, which prints its domain by one call and holds no `#var-domain` (calc/domains/domain_file.py), is no domain of the tab and is not listed. An id is one for the whole project, a file of every folder of domains taken (`formula_domain._taken`). Everything below that names `Formules/` reads `Domains/` in a project laid out by folders.

**The «Formules» tab (v0.100, retired in 0.117).** v0.100's *Formules* turned a member's checks written in keyword lines into `#member` and `#verify-*` calls. It was retired on the engineer's request of 08.10.2026 and is kept in `archive/formules-dsl/` -- its paragraph as it stood is `archive/formules-dsl/docs/ui/window-formules-tab.md`. Its place in *Référence* is the new *Formule* tab (below).

**The tab of v0.117 to v0.124** -- one text holding a pill for each command (issue #15, issue #40) -- is described in [`history/2026-10-25-tab-formulas-pills.md`](../history/2026-10-25-tab-formulas-pills.md), moved verbatim when issue #113 rebuilt it.

**The «Domaines» tab since v0.125 (issue #113, spec docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-24-onglet-domaines-formulaire-code.md; the prototype «Domaines de calcul» v2 the engineer validated on 24.10.2026, its copy in `docs/requirements/prototypes/2026-10-24-onglet-domaines/`).** A domain is still one file, `Domains/<id>.typ`, its id the file's name, **the file the one source** (REQ-012): the tab reads it each time it is shown and writes it at every change, nothing else to save; a file changed on the disk meanwhile is never written over (`formula_domain.check_unchanged`). `calc/tools/formula_tab.py` is the frame; everything a domain computes or says is `calc/domains/`'s, Qt-free (`formula_domain`, `formula_rows`, `formula_state`, `formula_infer`, `formula_args`, `formula_defaults`, `formula_links`, `formula_errors`, `formula_order`, `formula_undo`); the words are `calc/tools/formula_tab_words.py`'s (`fm_`), merged by woodcore_gui_words through `formula_tab.STRINGS`.

**Three modes on the same file** (UI-001, D-1, D-2; the `views.py` keys `form` and `code`, and the `display` every tab has): **Formulaire · Code · Affichage**. The pills and the mode «Les deux» of the prototype's first version are gone.

**On the left** (UI-002) the domains of the tab, each one row -- its name and its state, «✔ 70 %» after its last check, «✘ 127 %» where it does not hold, «1 ✘» for a fault (`formula_state.summary`) -- first, then the groups of the specialised tabs whose domains Woodcore wrote out («Neige», «Traction» …, `formula_domain.group_of`), **folded**, «Archives» last; a domain is no longer unfolded line by line (its lines are the form's), and nothing there is written in Typst. Folders and the order the engineer drops are kept in `Woodcore-Datei/lists.json` (calc/list_order.py), the files staying flat; a domain deleted goes into `Domains/archives/` and comes back by a drop. Under the list, **«Domaine»** -- a new domain, its name typed on the first line, its ID made from it until it is written, written on Enter **with `#calc-defaults(presets: "block", scope: "domain")`** (D-3: printed one line a value; a domain already written keeps what it has) -- and **«Dupliquer le domaine»**.

**In the centre** (UI-003) the line of the name and the **ID** -- changed, it renames the file and lists, without rewriting them, the lines that still cite the old one --, the file (`Domains/panne-p2.typ`), the legend of the linked hovers, the card of an unfolded check where the domain is one, the message «Annuler» offers, then the mode.

**The Formulaire** (calc/tools/formula_form.py, formula_row.py; ARCH-002: a column of rows in a scroll area) -- **a row an item of the domain** (calc/domains/formula_rows.py): its grip ⋮⋮, **the line of the file it begins on**, the label of its command, then `var`: its symbol as Typst sets it, `=`, its value, its unit, the ⓘ of its clause, its label; `eq`: its symbol, `=`, **its calculation typed with the symbols shown** (`sigma_(m,d) / f_(m,d)`, D-6, written with the keys, `sigma_m_d / f_m_d`, by `formula_domain.typst_formula` and the table of the lines above, `formula_infer.symbol_table`: a symbol typed as such where it is a plain name with one subscript, a composed one by its key), its unit (auto, or one of its result's dimension), `=`, its result, the ⓘ; `verify`: its relation, its two sides, ✔ or ✘; a variable linked from elsewhere its source's symbol and label and «↗ Neige · SL» (REQ-010); the engineer's Typst a row of text, printed as written -- a line of text that holds a command glued to it (`- $0°$ bis $30°$: #eq(…)`) one row of text whose command is a small block not typed in, its fields and arguments opened on the right (D-16), the file and the print unchanged; the line `#calc-defaults` alone a row of its own, «Options du domaine : réglées dans l'Affichage». **A row chosen** shows under it its shown variable, its computation variable (the key), its label, its norm, its clause (`norm-section:`, REQ-008: a file of before writing `norm-ref:` is read into it and written `norm-section:` once the line changes) and its description. **A value changed updates its row in place**; rows are built again only where they come or go. **A × on the right** deletes a row, its glued commands with it; the message that follows offers **«Annuler»** -- one step, the last deletion or the last gesture of several lines (a group moved, links validated), the file written back byte for byte, refused where the file changed since (D-17, `formula_undo`); no Ctrl+Z of the tab. **The + between two rows** offers var, eq, verify, texte, lier une variable…; **the line at the foot** (calc/tools/formula_new_line.py) takes «h = 240 mm», «M_d = q_d * l^2 / 8», «sigma_(m,d) <= f_(m,d)»: its **pill** says what it infers -- «var auto», «eq auto», «verify auto» (`formula_infer.infer_line`: a relation a verify, a symbol = a number and a unit a var, a symbol = an expression an eq, the rest a text) --, a click fixes it, the fields of the command under it, filled from what is typed; Enter or ✓ writes it, Escape or × gives it up. **The grip** drags a row; **Ctrl+click** adds a row to a group or takes it away, Shift+click a range (`variable_select.Selection`, the choice «comme dans Windows» of the «Variables» tab, ARCH-010) -- a dashed frame, a banner, Escape or a plain click letting it go --, and the grip of one of its rows drags the group whole, in its order; **a move that would put a line above what it is computed from is refused**, «Déplacement refusé : W_y utilise b, qui serait plus bas.», the file untouched (REQ-003, `formula_rows.order_refusal`). No key moves a line (D-10). **A row's stripe** is red at a fault, orange at a warning, grey for a line that only inherits a fault above it, the colour of its state for a line of an unfolded check (UI-009); its message is under it. **The linked hovers** (UI-018): a row under the pointer lit, the rows it is computed from in blue, those computed from it in green -- `formula_links.dependencies`, read off `formula_domain.compute`.

**A click writes a name into the calculation being typed** (UI-011): a variable of the module «Variables du domaine» or the name of a result of «Résultats» (dotted, «Clic : écrire ce nom dans le calcul en cours») is written as its symbol where the cursor of the calculation that last had it stands -- the field found again as it is now, after a redraw (the prototype's defect: the field kept was the old one, taken off the page) --, the cursor left there; in «Code» its key at the cursor of the buffer; with no calculation being typed, the status line says so. **The names a calculation may take are offered as they are typed** -- the domain's above the line by symbol or key, then the project's («q_k · Neige · SL · 1.115 kN/m²»), whose choice writes `#var("q_k1", domain: ("snow-load", "SL"))` above the line (REQ-013).

**«Lier une variable…»** (calc/tools/formula_link_dialog.py, UI-014): opened by the module «Variables du domaine» or by + → lier, the variables a link may take (`formula_links.linkable`, the «Variables» tab's source) grouped by tab and case, a search, a box on the left of each; a click chooses one without linking it, Ctrl+click adds or takes away, Shift+click a range, a click on a box toggles it; **«Valider (n)»** writes the links of all that is ticked, in the order of the list, above the row chosen, in one write «Annuler» takes back; Enter validates, Escape closes; a variable the domain holds already is greyed, «déjà dans ce domaine». The third way to the same link is **the fix of a message**: a name unknown that the export knows, «Lier q_k1 depuis Neige · SL». The resolution of a link and the guard against two domains reading each other (`formula_domain.resolve_import`) are unchanged (CON-004).

**The Code** (calc/tools/formula_code.py, code_editor.py; UI-019, ARCH-016): the `.typ` itself, coloured (`richtext.TypstHighlighter`), the number of a line red at a fault and orange at a warning, at its right its result -- «= 4.238 kN/m», «✔ 9.967 N/mm² <= 11.28 N/mm²» -- or its fault. **The buffer is the source while the mode is open**: read as the file would be (`formula_domain.parse_text`) once the typing pauses, written to the disk **only where it reads** -- a parenthesis forgotten breaks the line until it is corrected, the file of before untouched. **The command of the cursor is recognised at once** (`formula_args.command_at`) and fills «Arguments de la ligne»; a value set there replaces its line in the buffer by one edit, the cursor and the scroll staying.

**The Affichage** (calc/tools/formula_display.py: the view of the snow and the wind, calc/tools/domain_file_display.py, given the engineer's file as its source -- ARCH-009, D-19): in the centre the file compiled as the report prints it on an A4 page, every element the report hides shown pale (`show-masked`), Ctrl+wheel zooming; on the right the file and **the command to copy into the section**, `#include "/Domains/panne-p2.typ"`; **«Lire une valeur ailleurs dans le rapport»**, `#var("eta", domain: "panne-p2", display: "value-unit")`; **the domain's level**, `#domain(…, level:)`, auto, 1, 2 or 3; **the domain's options**, each ticked or not -- `presets: "block"` («En bloc : une ligne par valeur»), `label-position`, `descr-position`, `norm-position`, `style`, each with its value (proposed: after, footnote, line-right, normal) -- written on **one** line `#calc-defaults(…, scope: "domain")` under the header, nothing ticked taking the line away (calc/domains/formula_defaults.py, REQ-004: the arguments it does not set and a label after it kept); **the elements of the file**, each with its own level 0 1 2 3 (the domain's dotted, a second click giving it back) and, clicked, **every argument** of its line, «Rétablir la ligne», the line as the file writes it; «Voir le fichier entier». The hovers of the lines are the form's: the page has no rectangle per line (D-19). A file Typst would not compile says the state's fault, once (UI-022). Every write goes through `formula_domain.save` -- never the base of unfold, which a domain of the engineer's has not.

**On the right, the modules** of the common pane (calc/tools/formula_modules.py, module_pane.ModulePane; UI-004), in this order the first time, moved and folded as in the calculation tabs: **État du domaine** (UI-015) -- in red a fault of reading at the line and column Typst gives (`formula_errors.read_fault`: the delimiter left open, «Ligne 19, colonne 6 : la parenthèse de cette commande n'est jamais fermée»), a name unknown, two dimensions added, a link that finds nothing, **each said once** with «3 ligne(s) plus bas en dépendent» (REQ-006: a line that only inherits a fault is `blocked_by` it), «Ligne 13 de Domains/panne-p2.typ : …» (REQ-007); in orange a check that does not hold (D-7), a unit asked for that is not the result's, a domain of the tab read by its name and not printed above it in a document that prints the domain reading it (D-8, `formula_order`), a domain without `presets: "block"` with «Ouvrir les options du domaine» -- in the window's language (`units.FormulaError` and `formula_domain.DomainError` carry a code and its values, `fm_e_<code>`; the report keeps Typst's English, REQ-005); a click leads to the line: the row flashes and its field takes the cursor, in «Code» the line is selected. **Arguments de la ligne** (UI-012) -- in «Code» every argument of the command of the cursor (`eq`: key, symbol, calculation, unit, label, descr, norm, norm-section, as-unit, then the display's -- display-formula, display-equation, the positions, style, role, level, `calc/line_display.py`, their values lib/display-presets.json's: one list, a value added there offered at once; `verify`'s `display: "value"` left out as the engine refuses it, `line_display.EXCLUDED`), in «Formulaire» the others of the row chosen, *auto* writing nothing; under them the line as the file writes it. **Résultats** (UI-016) -- every eq and verify, a click unfolding its formula in letters and numbers, «Tout déplier», «Tout replier», «Copier les valeurs». **Variables du domaine** (UI-017) -- symbol, value, «ce domaine» or «↗ Neige · SL», «Lier une variable…». **The norm's excerpts**: the ⓘ of a line whose clause is relevé (`norms.excerpts.for_citation`: SIA 260 4.4.3.4 and Tableau 1, SIA 261 2.1 and 5.2.2, SIA 265 3.3.2.1, 4.2.3.1, Tableau 4 and Tableau 8) opens its bubble; a click pins the excerpt under the modules, a second takes it off (issue #96, `norm_bubble.pin_excerpts`); Ctrl+click opens the edition at the clause. A clause no file lists shows its name, without a bubble.

**An unfolded check** (spec 2026-10-15, UI-002; REQ-015): a domain Woodcore wrote from a member check keeps its card «Vérification dépliée» in the centre -- the check it is written from, the lines not in step, «Importer», «Tout importer», «Reprendre», «Réinsérer», «Garder la mienne», «Prendre celle de l'export», «Régénérer» (calc/domains/unfold.py) --, and each line not in step colours **the stripe of its row** (`formula_tab_unfold.item_states`, `formula_state.COLOURS`): a conflict and a line taken out red, a line changed outside Woodcore and data changed orange, the engineer's own value the accent.

**The graph's node** `formula:<id>` opens the domain here (`GRAPH_KIND = "formula"`, `lists_object`, `open_object`): what «Ouvrir l'onglet» of the «Variables» tab does (REQ-018). Every writer -- `write_field`, `write_new_line`, `write_delete_rows`, `write_undo`, `write_move_rows`, `write_links`, `write_argument`, `write_code`, `write_domain_option`, `write_domain_level`, `write_line_display`, `write_reset_line`, `duplicate_domain`, `rename_id` -- is one of `tab_guard.WRITERS`, refused in a project opened read only.
