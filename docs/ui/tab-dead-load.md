# The «Poids propre et surcharge» tab

> The tab of permanent loads, on the type «Calcul» since issue #95 and redrawn by issue #111 after the prototype v3 the engineer validated on 24.10.2026 (spec `docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-24-poids-propre-repense.md`; `docs/ui/calc-tab.md`; the domain: `docs/domain/loads.md` §3.3ter; the materials: `docs/domain/materials.md` §3.3sexvicies).

## 10quater. «Poids propre et surcharge» (v0.125)

`calc/tools/dead_load_tab.py` `DeadLoadTab(CalcTab)` -- a class of parameters, not one method (`tests/test_tab_overrides.py`) --, words `ld_` (`load_words.py`), its domain `calc/domains/dead_load_source.py`, a build-up written at dead-load-source 2.0 (`Woodcore-Datei/loads/dead/<id>.json`). Its services, in their order: «⊞ Vue d'ensemble» (`case_overview.Overview`) above the list of the build-ups (`CaseList`), the identity, **the table of the layers** in place of the declared form (`layer_table.LayerTable`), «Coupe | Contributions» (`layer_section.LayerSection`), the right pane (`Modules`), «Affichage» (`DomainDisplay`), the card of the engineer's materials changed since the project took its copy (`material_copies.MaterialCopies`), and «Nouveau…» from a template (`template_picker.TemplatePicker`).

**The table** (UI-001): one compact line a layer, 26 px -- n°, Matériau, Ép. [mm], Entraxe [mm], Largeur [mm], g/a, kN/m². The material is typed into its cell: the list keeps what the words find in three languages, the category they name first («isolation», «étanchéité», «béton», «bois»: `materials_search.ranked`), each line saying its category, its weight and its origin (SIA 261, SIA 265, SIA 265/1, Externe, Personnel); a name no material carries stays written and the line says it in red. Tab goes from the material to the thickness, the spacing, the width, g/a, then the kN/m² (`layer_cells.next_cell`, pure), a cell the line's pose does not use greyed and skipped, after the last the material of the next line; Shift+Tab back; g/a by ↑ ↓ or Space; Enter on the material goes to the thickness; Escape closes the line. The width a line fills between pieces is computed and written in italics; the kN/m² shows the load computed, and a load typed there replaces it, in orange, the warning offering «Rétablir». Under it: «+ Couche» (the cursor in its material), «+ Charge saisie» (red until its value is typed), «Retirer», «Monter», «Descendre», the sum and the retained value; the link «Les matériaux se créent dans l'onglet Matériaux», which opens that tab -- this one creates none --; «Enregistrer comme modèle»; then the retained value, «auto» the tenth above the sum.

**The right pane** (UI-002), in this order: «État du calcul» -- red, orange, information, green, each message about a line opening it when clicked --; **«Couche n»** (`layer_options.LayerOptions`, a module the table gives the pane through `ctx.pane_modules`): the material's card, the pose (continuous, spaced, per surface), «Remplir l'espace entre les pièces» on a line linked with pieces, g_k | a_k, the role (twenty, optional), «Lier à la ligne du dessous : même niveau» -- on the last line it makes the next one --, the load with its formula in numbers, «Remplacer» and «Rétablir»; **«Coupe | Contributions»**: the section at its true proportion drawn at the module's width (the legend mode under 560 units), a click on a layer opening its line, ⤢ a window whose body follows its size; the contributions, g_k alone, a_k alone and the two, each layer's load and its part; then «Résultats».

**«⊞ Vue d'ensemble»** (UI-004): in the centre in place of the build-up open, a line per build-up of the project -- a small section, its id and name, its layers, g_k, a_k, their sum, the retained value (bold, orange where written), «complète» or «n à corriger» -- and on the right the red messages of all of them; a line clicked opens its build-up. **«Nouveau…»**: «Modèles Lignum» (eight cards of *Holzbau mit System*, each its section, its layers, its sum in kN/m² and kg/m², its page, what it supposes, the book's weight where it gives one, «Créer depuis ce modèle»), «Mes modèles» (this computer's, `%APPDATA%\Woodcore\dead-load-templates\`, each deletable), «Composition vide».

**«Affichage»** is the case's file, `Permanent-loads/<id>.typ`, line by line, written again at every save (`unfold.AUTO_REGENERATE`): the section printed from level 2, the table of the layers at level 3, each line's level and a table masked or shown set there.

What is not done yet is in `docs/open-questions.md` («What the redrawn «Poids propre et surcharge» leaves open»).
