# The «Matériaux» tab

> The engineer's library of materials and the ones Woodcore ships, on the type «Bibliothèque». The domain is `domain/materials.md` (§3.3sexvicies); the frame of the window `window.md`; the type `building-a-tab.md`.
>
> Issue #110, spec `docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-24-onglet-materiaux.md` (v0.125); the prototype the engineer validated on 24.10.2026, `docs/requirements/prototypes/2026-10-23-onglet-materiaux/`, layout A.

## 10quater. «Matériaux»

**Where.** The last row of the navigation, alone in a group «Autres» (*Weitere*, *Others*), in the three documents, bound to none. **The first tab that is not the project's** (`TabSpec.project=False`, `tab_registry.PROJECT_FREE`): never greyed while a document is not made, never marked stale by an export, its writers not wrapped by the read-only guard -- a material is saved in a project opened read only, the library being the engineer's. No module of it reads `project.current()` (`tests/test_materials_tab.py`). The start page has no tabs: the library is reached from a project's window.

**The type «Bibliothèque»** (`calc/tools/library_tab.py`, ARCH-006, Q-005). A library is no case of the project: `LibraryTab` mounts `tab_host.Service`s in three zones beside `TabHost` rather than on it, keeps the draft in `LibraryContext` (the entries, the one selected, its draft, `dirty`, `loading`; select, start, save, cancel, delete, reveal) and runs phases in the order of its services: `listed`, `selected`, `drafted`. The domain is its `STORE` (`domains/materials_library.py`). It has **no `save`**: the autosave never writes a half-typed material into a file every project reads; leaving one unsaved -- another material, the window closed -- asks: save, discard, stay (`confirm_leave`). A rebuild of the window keeps the draft (`view_state`). The tab itself is a class of parameters: `MaterialsTab`, `KEY`, `STORE`, `PREFIX = "mat_"`, `SERVICES`.

**The list** (`materials_list.MaterialList`, UI-003). The search -- every word, three languages, the category and the type too, the domain's --, the origin (Toutes, Woodcore, Personnel) and the type (Barre, Panneau, Autre), the number found; the eleven categories, each with its number, folded but where a search or a filter is on or the material selected lies; a row a material -- its type's icon (`material_marks`), its name in the window's language, the label of its source (SIA 265, SIA 265/1, SIA 261, Externe, Personnel). A click on the label selects the material and shows its source. Under it «+ Nouveau» -- the type first, three cards --, «Dupliquer», «Supprimer» (the engineer's own alone, after a question; the projects keep their copies).

**The sheet** (`materials_sheet.MaterialSheet`, UI-004 to UI-006). A shipped material: a band «Livré avec Woodcore : en lecture seule», «Dupliquer dans ma bibliothèque» (the copy's comment «← SIA 265»), every field off. The header: the name, the label of the source. Folds (`module_pane.Fold`, the accordion of a module of a right pane, written once), each with its summary while folded: **Identité** (type -- a change builds the sheet again --, category, names in the two other languages); **Poids** (per volume, per area or none, its value with its weight in kg, its reference, «pour les charges»); **Résistances** -- a member: its family and γ_M/η_M, a grid of six strengths characteristic | design, then the moduli and ρ_k; a panel: its product and γ_M · η_mod, its thickness (optional, «toute épaisseur» empty), a grid with ρ_k «pour les résistances et les assemblages», then per loading its rows ∥ and ⊥ (or one, isotropic), a column per range of thickness, «caractéristiques | de dimensionnement»; **Commentaire**; **Source** (none, website, PDF -- «Importer un PDF…» copies it into the library). A design value Woodcore fills -- deduced or set by the norm -- is **in italics**, its rule and its numbers in its tooltip; a value typed over it wins, and the italic value left as it was stays deduced. A cell counted as missing is on the theme's `missing` ground; a value the norm does not give is «—». «Annuler» / «Enregistrer» close the sheet of the engineer's own.

**The right pane** (`materials_pane.MaterialModules`, UI-007, UI-008), a `ModulePane` of four modules: **État** (the domain's states, green, orange, red, grey); **La source** -- the excerpt of the table, cut from the edition shipped in norm-library/ (SIA 265 Tabellen 8, 9; SIA 265/1 Tableaux 11 to 23, French in every language; SIA 261 Tabellen 28 to 30: `calc/norms/*-materials-excerpts.json`), the PDF imported, the website with «ouvrir dans le navigateur», or a place to drop a PDF --, «⤢ Ouvrir en grand» over the window (`BigView`, Échap or × closes it); **Disponible dans** (✓, ✗ with what is missing, «à venir»); **Où il est enregistré** (the file, the PDF copied, «et une copie dans chaque projet qui l'utilise»). The label of a source makes «La source» unfold, scroll into sight and flash (`ModulePane.reveal`, `blink.Blinker`, the flash of a field named by a message, written once). A module's body sets the height its wrapped lines need: the scroll area's layout kept a stale height otherwise.

**Words**: `mat_` (`calc/tools/materials_tab_words.py`), fr/de/en, «valeurs de dimensionnement» and never «valeurs de calcul» (CON-002); `nav_others` and `tab_materials`.

**Poids propre** (REQ-016) creates no material any more: its card «Bibliothèque du projet» is gone, a layer chooses «Matériau» -- one list, Woodcore's and the engineer's with a weight, searched by the domain -- or «Charge saisie»; a card «Matériaux de votre bibliothèque» (`material_copies.MaterialCopies`) says a material changed since the project took its copy, with «Reprendre la nouvelle version», «Garder la copie», «Ajouter à ma bibliothèque» (§3.3ter, §3.3sexvicies).
