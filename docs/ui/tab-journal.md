## 10quater (suite) — journal tab

**The journal tab (v0.99).** *Journal* (group *Projet*, after *Positions*; `calc/tools/journal_tab.py`, its words under `jo_`) lists the report's revisions, newest first, with a search, and edits the one chosen in one card: its **Indice** — proposed as the largest plus one, a gap never refilled — the **Date** (ISO, typed and not taken from the clock), the **Nom** and the **Sigle**, both prefilled from the entry before it, then the **Désignation**, the **Description courte** and the **Description longue**, with a line under the card saying what each level prints. Beside it a *Commandes Typst* card with the calls to copy. *Nouvelle entrée*, *Dupliquer*, *Supprimer* — which asks — and the *Annuler les modifications · Enregistrer* row, the dot of unsaved changes and `view_state` as every editor tab has them; Ctrl+S saves. The form refuses what `journal.check_source` would refuse **before anything is written**, in the window's language and under the field it concerns: an index another entry holds, a date that is not a day, an empty name, short form or label. A write goes through `calc/journal.py` and is followed by an export of the journal alone (`journal.export`), so `data/journal.json` follows at once and the report sees the entry without the load workbooks being read again. A project opened read-only refuses its *Enregistrer* and *Supprimer* (`WRITERS`). ⚠️ **Since v0.117 two views** (*Saisie · Affichage*, above): the card of calls left the form; *Affichage* offers `show-journal` — its form, level and title — compiled as the document shown, and `journal-entries(document: …)` for the document shown, which prints nothing (the call copied named no document until v0.117, and so read the report's journal in a convention d'utilisation).

**The card of the list's file in a project open read-only (issue #106).** «Écrire le fichier» and «Ajouter» of the card «Fichier de la liste» (`list_file_card.ListFileCard`, shared with «Métré») are refused there with the window's one message, as «Enregistrer» is: the card declares them as its `WRITERS`, guarded where they are defined (`tab_registry`, `tab_guard.guard_service`). Until then they wrote the list's `.typ` in spite of the lock.

⚠️ **Its list is a flat one and not a `grouped_list`**: a journal is chronological, so there is no folder to file a revision in — and no `identity.py` either, an entry being named by its number and not by an id somebody types.
