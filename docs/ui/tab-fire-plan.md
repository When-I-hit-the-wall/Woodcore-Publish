## 10quater (suite) — fire-protection plan tab

⚠️ *Retired by the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (lot 19): `show-fire-plan` -- write #figure(image-fire-plan(id), kind: image, caption: [...]). What follows describes it as it was.* **The fire-protection plan tab (v0.112 on Gian's branch, v0.115 here).** *Plan de protection incendie* (DE *Brandschutzplan*, EN *Fire protection plan*; group *Projet*, **right under *Plan de position***, **in the base de projet and the report and not in the convention** (`MODE_TABS`: `"bp"` and `"rs"` carry `tab_fire`; the engineer's answer of 06.10.2026); `calc/tools/fire_tab.py` and `fire_canvas.py`, its words under `fp_` and the warnings of `calc/domains/fire.py` under `warn_fire_`): where a fire-protection plan is drawn (§3.3vicies). ⚠️ **It is the position plan's tab a second time**, which the engineer required in those words (*«extrêmement similaire»*), so it is built on the same code: `calc/tools/drawing_tab.py`'s `DrawingTab` holds what both share — the documents pane and the continuous document view, the list of plans in folders, the export sheets, the «Commande Typst» builder, the history, the entry sizes and their orange «Reprendre», the autosave on leaving, `view_state` — and asks each tab for its hooks (`sources_dir`, `source_for_page`, `turn_page_drawings`, `build_drawing_center`, `build_drawing_properties`, `export`, `save`, `make_builder`, …); `PlanTab` returns exactly what it did before. **WRITERS** (the read-only guard, wrapped by method name on the classes a tab's `vars()` reach) lists `DrawingTab` and `FireTab` too: a method moved to a base would otherwise silently lose its guard, and `replace_source`, the undo helper, is never a writer. `FireCanvas` subclasses `PlanCanvas` and reads its frame through `frame_source()`, so a plan that follows draws over the position plan's background and scale. **A page ticked** in the document view makes a fire plan: one that **follows** the position plan drawn on it where there is exactly one, otherwise one with a frame of its own on the page; following is also chosen by hand in the right pane's *Plan background* card (a combo of the position plans, with a note), and the *Crop* card shows «taken from the position plan X» with *Draw* and *Reprendre*. A **calibration drawn on a followed page is refused** with a word (calibrate it in the position plan). **Both kinds stay in step**: turning a page from either tab turns the plans of both (`plan_pages.rotate_page`), a layer switched or an archiving exports both, and the document view shows the other kind's crops muted. **Archiving or unticking a position plan that fire plans follow** — in `source_archive` (`archive_note`, `before_archive`) — names them in its question and **detaches** them first. A plan of Gian's (1.0) is opened **without writing** and is saved as 2.0 when left. The **bar** holds the tools of Gian's tab on the position plan's: *Sélection*, *Vue*, a surface with a combo of the three kinds, a wall with a combo of the fire resistances, a door with its swing or slide and its fire resistance, the length, the emergency exit, the width (since v0.116 *Cadrage* and *Calibrer* are cards of the right pane, §10quater, below); the selection's card changes an entry already drawn, for every entry selected at once and in one step of the history; the legend is moved by dragging and sized by its own slider. The tab's **«Commande Typst»** builder (`plan_command_builder.FireCommandOptions`, `fire_command_text`) writes `#show-fire-plan(…)` with every argument as a control and a live preview. The tab is exercised off-screen (`tests/test_fire_tab.py`); what no test reaches is the pointer itself.

**The Text tool** (issue #43, v0.117; `docs/domain/variable-links.md` §3.3duovicies-bis). The last tool of the bar, *Texte*: a click puts a text `X1` down, selected, and the tool goes back to «Sélection»; a click on it takes it before the concept's own texts and entries, a drag moves it whole, «Déplacer», «Copier» and Delete act on it, and it turns with its page. It is in none of the lists. Selected alone, the pane on the right shows its content (a line per line, an `_` setting a subscript, a brace typed twice), «Insérer une variable» — the «Variables» tab in its choosing mode, every number of the export and of a «Formules» domain offered whatever its unit, the one chosen inserted where the cursor stands as a token `{1}` — and a card per variable, its symbol and domain, «valeur» or «symbole = valeur», and «Retirer» (`calc/tools/text_pane.py`, the four engines' one). A variable that can no longer be read is in the warning colour and prints its last value. Its size is the selection's own `text` factor. A plan that holds a text is saved at 2.1.
