# The «Projet» tab

> *Projet*: written on 2026-10-22 (issues #87, #88, #90, spec [`requirements/completed/2026-10-22-ergonomie-palette-onglet-projet.md`](../requirements/completed/2026-10-22-ergonomie-palette-onglet-projet.md), REQ-012 to REQ-021). The window's frame is [`window.md`](window.md) §10quater; the documents and their templates are [`domain/documents.md`](../domain/documents.md) §3.3octodecies.

## 10quater (suite) — «Projet» tab

**Why.** The engineer, 21.10.2026: «lorsque l'on passe de la convention d'utilisation à la base de projet ou au rapport de statique, l'onglet qui doit s'ouvrir, et donc le premier onglet qui doit être affiché tout en haut des onglets, c'est l'onglet projet. De cette manière, il n'y a pas de compilation automatique de tout le rapport en TYPST qui se fait en fait à chaque fois et qui prend du temps». Until 0.121 the window opened on «Structure du document», which compiled the whole document.

**Where it stands.** *Projet* (DE *Projekt*, EN *Project*) is the first row of the navigation in the three documents, above «Structure du document» in the group *Document*; Ctrl+1 opens it. The window opens on it, and **every change of document brings it forward**, even where the tab in front is one of both documents (`MainWindow.on_mode_changed`). The group that was called *Projet* — *Plan de position*, *Plan de protection incendie*, *Positions*, *Métré*, *Journal* — is called *Données* (DE *Daten*, EN *Data*); its key stays `nav_project`, so a group folded under its old title is found unfolded once. Its module is `calc/tools/project_tab.py`, its words `pj_` and `tpv_why_`, merged into the window's.

**Two views** (`views.py`): **«Informations»** (`details`, DE *Angaben*, EN *Details*), the default, which compiles nothing, and **«Templates»** (`templates`, DE *Vorlagen*).

**«Informations»**, in cards:

- **Page de titre** — the name, number, client, address, office, author and checker of the `.woodcore`, read by `Project.detail` and written by `project.update`, only what changed, a name never empty. Saved, the title page is **reported at once** into `data/project.json` (`export_all.refresh_project_info`, Q-003): the name and DETAILS alone are taken from the project file, what produced the last export — Woodcore's and Typst's versions, the workbooks' digests, the journal's revision — kept as that export wrote it; no stamp is touched, `data/project.json` being an input of every document, so the next compile sees a document changed. The window's top bar and title name the project again (`MainWindow.project_changed`).
- **Langue du projet** — shown greyed: chosen in «Nouveau projet» and never changed (T49 of the spec, the engineer, 22.10.2026: «le choix de la langue se fait lors de la création du projet. Cette option est visible, mais grisé»). Read by `templates.project_language`, the one reader of it.
- **Template de forme** — the form in use (`forms.current`), common to the three documents, and «Appliquer au projet…», which asks and then applies another one (`home_page.apply_form`: `Template/` and `title.typ` replaced, the old ones archived, the engineer's adjustments in a `config.typ` of the new format kept). The tabs that show a compiled page are told (`MainWindow.properties_written`).
- **Documents** — for each document its content template (`Project.content_template`, the `.woodcore`'s `content_templates`): its name, «inconnu» for a document made before 0.122, «introuvable sur ce poste» for one this machine does not have; and its folder, read only, with «Ouvrir le dossier». A document **not made yet** says «pas encore créé» and offers the content templates of the project's language and «Créer le document…», which asks, then makes it (`project_template.create_document`, below).
- **Version de Woodcore** — the version pinned, as *Propriétés du projet* pinned it until 0.121.
- **Dernier export** — its date and the engine's version (`project.last_export`: the time of `data/calculations.json`, which only an export writes, not of `data/project.json`, which this tab rewrites), and «Ouvrir dans VS Code».

**Saving.** As every tab (`autosave.py`): the title page and the version pinned are saved without a question when the tab is left, the document changed or the window left for another application, by Ctrl+S and by «Enregistrer»; «Annuler les modifications» reads the file again. *Fichier → Propriétés du projet* brings the tab forward, what every tab holds unsaved saved first; the dialog it opened until 0.121 is gone, «Nouveau projet» keeping its own (below). **Read-only**: the tab is shown with every field and button off, and its writers — `save`, `write_apply_form`, `write_create_document` — are refused by `tab_guard.WRITERS`.

**A document made later** (issue #90). «Nouveau projet» makes the project without any document (`project_template.create_skeleton`): the three folders are stated in the `.woodcore` (`documents.MODE_FOLDERS`, the same in every language since 0.119) and a document is «not made» while its entry file is not there (`Project.created_modes`, `is_created`; Q-004). In a document not made, **every tab but *Projet* is greyed** in the navigation, its tooltip saying why, left out of Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+9, and reached by no other door — `open_object`, the palette — the window bringing *Projet* forward instead (`MainWindow.available`, the redirect in `on_tab_changed`); the PDF button is off and «Exporter les trois» leaves it out. «Créer le document…» makes it from the content template chosen, in its folder, its sharing with the documents already there settled, its journal and the export's files seeded, `content_templates` written; the window is built again for it. A content template is chosen **once**: «Changer de template de fond…» was retired with it (`docs/decisions.md`, «Changer de template de fond…» is retired).

**«Templates».** On the left the document to look at (CU · BP · RS), the content template — of the project's language, the document's own chosen, fixed where the document is made — and the form, with «Appliquer au projet…» for the form and «Créer le document…» for a document not made; in the centre the document compiled with them. Choosing in a list changes the preview and nothing else. The preview is a **throw-away project in Woodcore's cache** (`calc/template_preview.py`, `usersettings.cache_dir("template-preview")`), made by the very functions that make a new project — `create_skeleton` and `create_document` — with the real one's name, title page and language and the export of a project holding nothing (`export_all.seed`), so that where a calculation is missing the document shows what a report shows for a missing value; nothing of the real project is read but those, and nothing is written into it. It is built off the window's thread, the last request alone counting, in a numbered folder per build so that a build never writes where Typst may still read the last one; the ones before are taken away (`template_preview.clean`, `project_template.remove_tree` — the engine's copy being read-only). A preview that cannot be made says why in a banner, by a code (`TemplatePreviewError`: `no_language`, `bad_mode`, `template_unusable`, `build_failed`), Typst's own message where it is a compile that fails.

**Not done** (spec §8): a content template or a form is created or edited in *Paramètres → Modèles de document*, not here; the language and a document's content template are not changed once chosen; one form serves the three documents.

`tests/test_project_tab.py`, `tests/test_template_preview.py`, `tests/test_project_dialog.py` and `tests/test_new_project.py` hold it.
