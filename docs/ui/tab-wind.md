# The «Vent» tab

> The wind tab, rethought by issue #100 as the second tab of calculation on the type «Calcul», built with the bricks of issue #95 completed for every tab (spec `docs/requirements/completed/2026-10-23-onglet-vent-briques.md`; `docs/ui/calc-tab.md`; the domain: `docs/domain/loads.md` §3.3quater).

## 10quater. «Vent» (v0.125)

`calc/tools/wind_tab.py` `WindTab(CalcTab)` -- a class of parameters, not one method --, words `wi_` (`wind_tab_words.py`), its list's `ld_` (`load_words.py`); everything it shows is its domain's (`calc/domains/wind_source.py`, which takes its declaration, its blocks, its links and Figure 9 from `calc/domains/wind_view.py`). The engineer reviewed it on an HTML prototype in five rounds on 21-23.10.2026; its copy is `docs/requirements/prototypes/2026-10-23-onglet-vent/` (commit `8e88677`), its numbers the tab's golden values (`tests/fixtures/wind-prototype-golden.json`). It replaces the frame of issue #37, `load_tab.LoadTab`, removed with it.

**«Calcul».** On the left the buildings, in folders. In the middle the form in four sections, an accordion:

1. **Données de base** -- q_p0, whose ⓘ opens 261.sia.ch's wind map at Ctrl+click (Annexe E), the terrain category (Tableau 4's words), b · d · h on one line, z with «z = h» ticked by default (null in the source: q_p at the highest point, 6.2.2.3), c_d (6.3.5).
2. **Bâtiment (annexe C)** -- the table among the 37 buildings, what it says (its number and its title in the edition's language, h : b : d, the roof in French, the proportions entered), then «Norme | Modèle 3D», one at a time (`views`, D-35): its figure cut out of the norm, or the building in 3D in the table's own proportions (never the typed ones), turned by a drag, zoomed by the wheel about its centre, Face A, Face C, Iso, Dessus, the wind's direction φ on a slider, pinnable to the right pane. A building without geometry shows its figure alone, and no switch. Every letter of the figure is drawn, the stand's m and o too, though Tableau 50 gives them no coefficient (D-37).
3. **Coefficients de réduction et de force** -- c_red of Figur 9 for b·h, d·h, the oblique directions and the roof, each row lighting its face on the model; the force coefficients coloured; the global forces Q_k per φ in kN with their c_red, the extremes marked ◆, A_ref under them; formula (15) with the case's numbers; the result in 3D of the forces, one button a direction of the table and «Max, toutes directions», max (+) or min (−).
4. **Coefficients de pression** -- c_pe and c_pi of the table coloured, the openings' column chosen marked; ĉ_pe · q_p; the detail per φ, q_k of each surface at each direction, as wide as the pane and scrolling below its width; the openings on the line of their words, offered only by a table that has them (Tableaux 46-50 take each surface's own c_pi, 57a-d, 63 and 64 none); the formula of q_k; the result in 3D of the loads, a plane held off each face by arrows, red a pressure, blue a suction.

**Hovers** (UI-021): a cell of the detail lights its surface on the model and frames the c_pe and c_pi that gave it; a result Q_k lights its column of forces and frames its extreme; a reference area lights its face alone; a surface of the model lights its column of coefficients. On the right the modules, declared by the domain (`Declaration.modules`, D-34): the state (red, orange, green, each message on its field); «Résultats» -- c_h, q_p, c_red, then Q_k,1-3 max and min in kN at their φ, and the tables Murs, Toiture, Points de mesure and Surfaces localisées, pressure max and suction max q_k [kN/m²] at their φ, «—» where there is none, a cell hovered lighting its surface and framing its c_pe and c_pi (`qx:<surface>:<max|min>`) --; «Bâtiment», the facts and «Norme | Modèle 3D» as in section 2, its choice the same; Figure 9 as an interactive chart with the case's two facades marked; the excerpts pinned from a bubble and the blocks pinned by their 📌.

**«Affichage».** The case's file `Variable-loads/Wind/<id>.typ`, written again at every save (`unfold.AUTO_REGENERATE`), printed by `#include domain-file("wind-load", "<id>")`: the figure of the norm at its head, cut at the export into `Variable-loads/Wind/Exports/Figures/<id>-fig-fr.pdf` and `-de.pdf` and printed from level 3 on (`#from-level(3)[…]`), the values, the global forces in Q_k [kN] and the local pressures of the openings chosen, transposed in blocks of twelve surfaces. The two tables are elements of the file -- shown or not, with or without their title and their note, no level --, the figure's width, caption, label and «Afficher» set as every figure's. See `docs/ui/calc-tab.md`.
