## 10quater (suite) — polar moments tab

**The polar moments tab (v0.31, v0.32).** *Moments polaires / Polare Trägheitsmomente / Polar moments of inertia* is where a connection is defined (§3.3quinquies). On the left, since v0.75, the calculations in folders of their own `group` — the `GroupedList` of every other editor tab, with its drop. From v0.33 to v0.74 it was a tree of the connectors with their calculations beneath, and took no drop; in v0.75 a calculation of no listed connector was marked in orange, which ended with the link (v0.76). The buttons: ⚠️ **Since v0.117 two views** (*Calcul · Affichage*, above): the calls left the right pane; *Affichage* offers `show-polar-moment` — N, V and M starting from the trial forces of *Calcul*, its level, title and width —, `show-calc` and the `I_p` read by `var-of`.
- *Nouveau calcul* creates a calculation on the schema of the calculation selected (else the form's, else the first there is), named after that schema (`SD_01`, then `SD_01 2`, …), its members the schema's linear elements and every one of its markers ticked — or, on a schema carrying none, every point ticked «connecteur» (`schema.default_fasteners`), so it calculates the moment it opens (v0.76). Nothing gates it: without a schema it opens on none, and says so.
- *Dupliquer* copies the form under the next free name made from its own.
- *Supprimer le calcul* deletes one.

In the middle, the form, **rebuilt in v0.74 around the schema it names**:
- **Identité** — four rows and nothing else, in this order (v0.76): **Schéma**, a combo of the schemas there are with a line under it saying what the one in force holds (`3 élément(s), 8 repère(s)`) or, in orange, that it is gone or that none is named; **Nom**, in free text; **Description** (*Désignation* until v0.75, the same `label`); and the **ID**, made from the name (`connection.id_from_name`) and writable over, the connectors' own rule — an id that is not what the name makes reads as written over, which a 3.x id carrying its variant does. ⚠️ The schema is **named and not drawn**: it replaces the member form of v0.31–v0.73 — b, h, view, angle, through, against, overhang, and the direction the page was turned in — a polar moment having no business holding a second drawing of a joint the schema already draws; it had a box of its own until v0.75. ⚠️ **Gone from the form in v0.76**: the connector and the variant, which left the source; *Groupe*, which a drop in the list sets and the source keeps; and the reference with its clause, saved as the file has them, a new calculation citing the bauforumstahl handbook with no clause.
- The **members**, from two to six, one row each in the card *Éléments* (v0.93; a box each until then): a numbered badge, a **name**, the **element of the schema** it is, and a ✕ where more than two remain — and nothing else; *+ Ajouter un élément* under them.
- The **Bezugselement**, under the members in the same card, which the report counts the other members' angles from — «Élément de référence (angles θ)», where it said «(axes x, z)» until v0.93. ⚠️ It no longer fixes the frame: x and z are the schema's, which its tooltip says.
- The **fasteners**: every connector marker **and every construction point ticked «connecteur»** of the schema (v0.77, `connection.fastener_places`; a point says what it is in its tooltip), with a tick against the ones this calculation carries and its place beside it, read only. A marker the schema has lost is still listed, ticked and marked `(?)`, so it can be seen and untangled rather than vanishing with the number it carried. *Tout cocher* · *Tout décocher*.
- The **rotation centre**: the centroid (shown), a fixed x and z (starting where the centroid was), or **a centre drawn in the schema**, chosen from its point-shaped aids.
- The directions of N and V, with a note on the sign conventions.

⚠️ **«Grille…» is not here any more.** A rectangular group of markers is placed in the «Schémas» tab, where markers are placed: a second tab writing `details/<id>.json` could overwrite an unsaved draft of that very schema in the tab that owns it (§13). On the right, the figure, redrawn as the form changes (the selected table row's fastener ringed), n, the rotation centre, I_p,x, I_p,z, I_p and r_max, a force preview for trial N, V and M — the most stressed fastener or fasteners, the components and force-grain angles of the first, and every fastener's arrows drawn into the figure as the report will draw them — and the calls to copy — `show-polar-moment` with the trial values, at the report's level and at levels 1–3, `show-calc("connection", id)`, a `var-of` binding of I_p — with a notice while the connection is not saved, Typst knowing only saved ones. The figure, the polar moment and the preview are `calc/domains/connection.py`'s and `connection_svg.py`'s, the modules export_all.py runs, never a copy of them.

*Enregistrer* (Ctrl+S) checks the draft with the same rules the export applies, refuses an id already taken — ignoring case, the file system doing so — asks before renaming a saved connection (a call using the old id would stop compiling), writes `connections/<id>.json`, and exports the connections at once, in the window's own process, by the same `connection.export()` export_all.py runs: `data/connections.json` and the figures are written, and nothing of the loads is touched or re-read. A connection whose export fails — another source that no longer validates — stays saved, and the window says why the report still sees the previous data. *Supprimer* moves the source into «Archives», its ID removed, and exports again, which removes its figure (v0.77, `source_archive.py`). The draft being edited, unsaved changes included, travels through a language switch, *Recharger* and a re-export (each tab's `view_state()`, kept by title key, now that more than one tab has one); choosing another connection, *Nouveau*, *Dupliquer* and closing the window ask first — save, discard or stay. ⚠️ **From v0.74 to v0.77 *Enregistrer* saved nothing and said nothing**: it still read the fastener table as the typed x/z of v0.31 — a tick and a marker id since v0.74 —, found every row «bad», and raised a `KeyError` on a message long removed; the exception was lost under pythonw, so the file stayed as it was and the tab kept its dot. The check is gone (`check_source` checks the ticks), and `woodcore_gui.install_error_box` now shows any exception raised inside a slot in a message box, one at a time, so a handler that fails can no longer look like a button that did nothing. Found alongside: `connection._table` unpacked a centre that is `None` where no fastener resolves, so a calculation on a schema that is gone stopped the connections' export with a `TypeError` instead of warning.

**Numbers that follow a variable, and the trial forces saved** (issue #42, v0.117; `docs/domain/variable-links.md` §3.3duovicies). The rotation centre placed by hand (x, z), the two directions and the trial forces N, V, M of «Calcul» carry the button 𝑥 (`PolarTab.linked`); a centre that is the centroid or an aid takes no variable, its buttons closed. **The trial forces are data of the calculation** since this version (D-12): `trial` is saved in `connections/<id>.json` with it, an edit of one marks the calculation modified, and a variable may fill them — a load's, a script's or a «Formules» domain's, never a joint's (`links.JOINTS`). `draft()` carries the links and 4.1 where there is one.
