The description of the tabs before it -- `MemberCheckTab`'s three panes, the «Flambage» and «Déversement» tabs, the shear's list -- is `docs/history/2026-10-25-tab-member-checks-before-112.md`, moved verbatim. `MemberCheckTab` stays the base of the «Résistance au feu» tab and of the steel tabs.

## 10quater (suite) — member checks tabs

**The group «Sécurité structurale»** (`nav_safety`, in the report alone): Traction, Compression, Flexion, Cisaillement, Torsion (a placeholder), Résistance au feu; «Aptitude au service» keeps its two placeholders. **«Flambage» and «Déversement» are no tabs any more** (Q-001 A, REQ-011): the stability is the last accordion of the compression and of the bending, «vérifier la stabilité», unticked by default; the kind of the check stays (`buckling`, `lateral-torsional`) -- the box turns the draft's kind (`member_check_view.CheckDomain.stamp_version` → `member_check.change_kind`) and the save moves the check's file to the folder of its new kind (`save_case` → `member_check.save_kind_change`, `unfold.move_domain`), then writes it in the new form (`export(moved=)`). The compression tab lists `compression` and `buckling`, the bending tab `bending` and `lateral-torsional`; the «Variables» tab opens a check of either in its tab (`variable_calc.CHECK_TABS`). The window's bar names the project and the tab open alone, where the navigation stands it: «Musterprojekt — Sécurité structurale › Cisaillement — Woodcore» (`chrome.SideNav.path`, `update_title` at every change of tab; tests/test_home.py). `tab_mc_shear` is «Cisaillement» / «Schub» / «Shear».

**Each tab is a class of parameters** (`calc/tools/check_tabs.py`): its key, its domain -- `member_check_view.DOMAINS[<tab>]`, a `CheckDomain` that lists its kinds (`owns`) in the folder the four share, an id unique across them (`SourceStore.all_ids`) --, the prefix of a new id (`Z_`, `DR-`, `BI-`, `SC-`), its list's words (`mc_`). Its services are the type's (docs/ui/calc-tab.md) with six of its own: **the list** in place of the type's (`check_list.CheckList`), **the head «Élément»**, **the row «Sollicitation»** and **the row «Facteurs»** before the declared form (`check_member.CheckMember`, `load_row.LoadRow`, `factors_row.FactorsRow`: three services that never read each other, what they read alike in `check_rows`), **«Résultats»** given to the right pane in place of the type's (`check_results.CheckResults`), **«Affichage»** keeping its options in the check's source (`check_display.CheckDisplay`). The domain saves and exports its cases itself (`save_case`, `export_cases`), names a check by its id in the graph (`graph_id`) and its messages' values in the window's language (`message_params`): hooks of `source_store.SourceStore` and `tab_host.TabContext` any domain may give.

**The list** (UI-003): the checks of the tab **grouped by position** («T001 · Poutre», «Sans position»), each with its rate in percent -- green at 100 % or less, red above, no orange --, computed as the tab computes it off the last export (`member_check_view.CheckDomain.list_rows` → `member_check.utilisations`); a check that cannot be calculated shows «—» and says why in its tooltip. «Par position» and «Par taux» sort it: the groups, or one list the highest rate first. The order is the rule's (no order kept in lists.json, `CaseList.KEEPS_ORDER`) and a drop files nothing: the position is said in the head. «Archives», «Nouveau», «Dupliquer», «Supprimer» are the type's.

**The head «Élément»** (UI-004 to UI-006, UI-015): the position (a list); **the material**, a bubble -- grey «C24 — T001» while it follows the position, orange with «Reprendre» once written over -- whose click opens **the menu of the library** under the row (`material_menu.MaterialMenu`, a brick written once): a search «C24, GL, OSB, panneau…» (the names in three languages, the group, the source: `materials_search`), the position's material first («C24 — suit T001»), then «Bois massif · SIA 265, tableau 8», «Lamellé-collé · SIA 265, tableau 9», «Panneaux · SIA 265/1», «Personnel» and «Bois sans résistance : un poids seulement» greyed, each line with the resistance the tab reads -- f_m,d in «Flexion», f_t,0,d, f_c,0,d, f_v,d -- or a panel's values by loading («p∥ 10,5 · p⊥ 5,3 · v∥ 5,8 · v⊥ 4,2») and its source (`member_check_material.offered`); **the row «Sollicitation», always there** -- a panel's bubbles as the engineer validated them for OSB/3 (en plaque ∥ / ⊥, en voile ∥ / ⊥ in «Flexion»; en traction / compression ∥ / ⊥; en plaque ∥, en voile ∥ in «Cisaillement»), each its value of the b table at the section's thickness, greyed without one; a member's one bubble ticked and closed, «flexion f_m,d = 14,1» --, beside them the ⓘ of the material's table (Tableaux 19a et 19b for an OSB/3, Tabelle 8 or 9 for a class of SIA 265: the material's `excerpt`), pinned under «Résultats» by a click (UI-012); **the line «La vérification lit …»**, read off the row of the clause `compute` writes (its value, norm, table and range, the panel's thickness, the factors), in red a thickness out of the ranges; the section (following or written over, b × h or d, each linkable); **the row «Facteurs»**: a member's moisture class with η_w (Tabelle 4) and its action, η_t 1,0 or 1,4 (2.2.6), γ_M/η_M shown (Tabelle 1); a panel's moisture class (those its product is admitted in), its load-duration class (Tableau 7, Tableau 8 in the tooltip) and its situation (normal, or accidental γ_M = 1,0), η_mod and γ_M shown. A panel shows no angle: its direction replaces it (D-19).

**The forms** (`member_check_view.SECTIONS`, words `vc_`): «Traction» -- N_d, α; «Compression» -- N_d, α, «Appui» at an angle (**five cards of f_c,90,d** (UI-008): «en général», «avec bois en saillie» with and without consequence (the bracketed value), «appui d'extrémité» with and without, each its value of SIA 265 Tabelle 8 or 9 for the draft's class under its words, a card the table gives no value greyed with its reason -- D30's bracketed values; the draft's `support`, written into the file as `case` and `larger` and never held itself (`member_check_view._apply_support`), the values the domain's `choice_values`, which the declared form shows under any field of buttons --; l, w «= b»), «Stabilité» (l_y, l_z, β_y, β_z); «Flexion» -- N_d signed, M_y,d, M_z,d, α, «Stabilité» (a, or σ_m,crit given; the buckling lengths with a compression); «Cisaillement» -- **three figures side by side** in place of Q-010's list (D-7): «Effort tranchant» (V_z,d, V_y,d, the pith), «Cisaillement d'about» (F_a,d, l_a, w_a), «Avec contrainte ⊥ au fil» (V_z,d, σ_90,d). **Each field's ⓘ** opens and pins the clause and figure of SIA 265 it follows (UI-012, `member_check_view.EXCERPTS`, read off the editions into `calc/norms/sia265-2021-checks-excerpts.json` and held to the PDFs by tests/test_norm_excerpts.py): 4.2.1.1 and 4.2.1.3 in «Traction», 4.2.2.1, 4.2.2.3, 4.2.2.2 with Figuren 9 and 46, 4.2.8 with Figur 10 in «Compression», 4.2.4.1/4.2.4.2, 4.2.3.3 with Figur 7, 4.2.9 with Figur 11 in «Flexion», 4.2.5.1, 4.2.6, 4.2.7.1 with Figur 8 under the three figures of «Cisaillement». A section none of whose fields shows is hidden (`calc_form.DeclaredForm.update_rows`, at every computation). A number written as a {value, unit} block, or 0 as null, is `Field.block`, `Field.zero_none`.

**The right pane**: «État du calcul» -- red what stops (the reason of the record, said by its code: `vc_msg_material_lacks`, T39's words and the quantity that lacks; `thickness_out` with the ranges; `class_not_admitted`; `unknown_material`…), orange what is only said (a class written over the position's, a panel's side, σ_90,d outside −f_c,90,d … f_t,90,d, a position gone); no message of the stability. «Résultats» -- the verdict first, η in percent, green at 100 % or less and red above, then the rows in the order of the calculation, η last, the clause at the right, the row that governs a maximum framed with a dashed line (`compute`'s `governing`), a click unfolding a row's formula. «Schéma» -- for a bending the scheme in the style of SIA, black and white: the bar and its moments, the normal force, the grain at α, the section with y, z, b, h, M_z,d, and σ over the height with its two edge values read off `compute` (`member_check_view.bending_scheme`, `edge_stresses`); the member and its force for the others. «Figure interactive» -- k_c over λ_rel (Figur 10) for a buckling, k_m over λ_rel,m for a lateral torsional buckling, equation (28) over σ_90,d for the shear across the grain, the check's point on it.

**«Affichage»** (UI-013): the file `Timber-checks/<Kind>/<id>.typ` compiled by Typst, the command to copy `#include domain-file("check", "<id>")`, the elements of the file; the level (auto, 0 to 3), the member's line (always, none, from a level), the verdict (in % or as a ratio), the description and the title -- **written into the check's source** (`display`) and the check saved at once; the file follows (`unfold.check_header`, `unfold_display.apply_check_display`).
