## 10quater (suite) — member checks tabs

**The member checks: two navigation groups and the «Traction» tab (v0.117).** The engineer, 07.10.2026: the checks of SIA 265 chapter 4 as modules of the window, **in the Rapport de statique document only** — the nine modules are in `MODE_TABS["rs"]` (every `TABS` key) and in neither `DOCUMENT_TABS` nor the base's list. **Two groups** of `NAV_GROUPS`, after *Assemblages* and before *Référence*, **one navigation entry per check**: «Sécurité structurale» (`nav_safety`: Traction, Compression, Flexion, Effort tranchant, Torsion, Flambage, Déversement) and «Aptitude au service» (`nav_service`: Déformations, Vibrations); each module with a line icon of its own in `chrome.ICONS` (`tab_mc_*`, `tab_sls_*`: a bar pulled, pressed, bent, sheared, twisted, bowed, tipped, a beam sagging under its original line, a wave). Their words and the form's are `calc/tools/member_check_tab.py`'s, in three languages under the prefix `mc_` (and the groups' `nav_safety`, `nav_service`), merged into the window's `STRINGS`. The engineer's grouping, said in the placeholders' words: **«Flexion» is bending, alone or combined with a normal force** (SIA 265 4.2.3, 4.2.4) and **«Effort tranchant» holds the shear (4.2.5, 4.2.6, 4.2.7) and the compression perpendicular to the grain (4.2.2.2, 4.2.2.3)**; «Déformations» includes fatigue (4.3, 4.4). **«Traction», «Compression» and «Flexion» are built** (`TensionTab`, SIA 265 4.2.1, `#verify-tension`; `CompressionTab`, 4.2.2, and `BendingTab`, 4.2.3 and 4.2.4, below); the six others are `SoonTab`s — a title, «à venir», what the module will hold and the commands of the report that do the work meanwhile (`#verify-compression`, `#verify-bending-normal`, `#verify-shear`, …) — and have no state.

**`ShearTab`, `BucklingTab`, `LateralTab` (v0.117, issue #38; IDs `SC-`, `KN-`, `KI-`)** — «Effort tranchant», «Flambage», «Déversement» are member-check tabs now, `SoonTab` keeping *Torsion*, *Déformations* and *Vibrations*. All three are along the grain: the angle's row is hidden. «Effort tranchant» holds V_z, V_y and the pith, no N (its row hidden), and says which shear checks stay commands of the report (`#verify-shear-off`, `#verify-shear-perp`). «Flambage» holds N and the card «Longueurs de flambage» -- l_y, l_z, beta_y, beta_z, `BucklingLengths`, one class with «Déversement» (D-09) --, which «Déversement» shows only with a compression, keeping what it holds; «Déversement» also holds M_y, M_z, N signed and a card «Appuis latéraux»: σ_m,crit by the distance a (41) or given. The manual selection that offered a check's verdicts beside its lines at -1 is retired (spec 2026-10-22-retrait-niveau-manuel; `docs/history/2026-10-22-niveau-manuel.md`).

**`MemberCheckTab`** — the base of `TensionTab` and `CompressionTab`, each listing only the checks of its own kind, an ID being unique across the kinds (the folder is shared) — is three panes, the connectors' own (`QSplitter`, 2 : 5 : 5): **at the left** the checks of the project in folders (`grouped_list.py`, a drop writing the `group` field; «Supprimer» archives into `checks/archives/`, `source_archive.py`; *Nouveau* hands out `Z_01`, `Z_02`… (`DR-01`… for a compression)); **in the middle the form in cards**, Nom · Description · ID (`identity.py`) and the position it concerns (a combo of the positions of the project, or none), the material (strength class), the section (rectangle b/h or circle d, mm), the moisture class, the effort (N in kN, the angle α, the impact η_t) — ⚠️ **a value that follows its position says so in its own field** («C24 — T001» since v0.117, the v0.77 rule) **and one typed over it stays written over**, an orange line naming what the position holds with «Reprendre» beside it (the connectors' rule, v0.70); the first entry of the strength class, the section and the moisture class follows, any other writes the value into the source, and choosing the first again is «Reprendre»; **at the right the result**, in the order of the engineer's decision of 07.10.2026: the card *Résultats* — **an instant Python calculation** (`member_check.compute`, §3.3unvicies), recomputed at every edit with no wait: each value as the report computes it, `symbol = value unit` with the symbol set as mathematics, the clause («SIA 265 4.2.1») and the equation, and the verdict -- since issue #12 the ratio of the action to the resistance, `σ_t,0,d / f_t,0,d = 0.41 ⩽ 1`, in percent `σ/f = 41 %`, never η (`member_check.compute`'s `ratio`) -- with ✔ in the new theme token `ok` or ✘ in `danger` —, and, **since v0.117, in the «Affichage» view** (until then the right pane's call card and two collapsible sections, `options_open` and `preview_open` in `view_state`, gone with them): at the right *Options de la commande* — the check's level (auto, 0 to 3), the member line (none, 0 to 3, default 1), «avec la description de la position», the title (automatic, none, free text), the unit of the utilisation (ratio or percent), a panel of the tab's own (`display_view.OwnPanel`) — then the call coloured as the site's code with «Copier» («Copié ✓» for 1.4 s; copy only: **no insertion into a section**, the engineer's decision); in the middle **the page the command prints, compiled from the DRAFT** (`member_check.draft_typst`, through the display's `snippet_fn`, Typst's own message in the pane's callout and never in a modal) **only in «Affichage»**, so that nothing waits on a compile while editing in «Calcul». A check that cannot be calculated shows its reason in the results instead of a page. ⚠️ **The call names the check by its ID, and Typst only knows the checks the last export wrote**, so a banner over the page says to save the check first (with «Import données» beside it, v0.117); the preview, which writes the draft's values into the call, needs no save. A check that cannot be calculated shows its reason in the callout instead of a page. *Enregistrer* (Ctrl+S) checks the draft with `member_check.check_source`, refuses an ID already taken (ignoring case) or invalid, asks before a rename (a call naming the old ID will stop compiling) and exports the checks at once by `member_check.export()`; a project opened read-only refuses it and *Supprimer* (`MemberCheckTab` is in the tabs `WRITERS` wraps). The draft, the options and the panes' sizes travel through a rebuild of the window (`view_state`). ⚠️ **`TensionTab` and `CompressionTab` have no `on_mode`**: they are shown in the report alone, so the window never tells it another document is shown (§14). `tests/test_editor_buttons.py` clicks its list buttons with the five other archiving tabs. ⚠️ **Since v0.117 the tab has two views** (above): *Calcul* — the checks, the form, and at the right the Python result alone — and *Affichage* — the same list, the page of the draft in the middle (compiled from the draft, saved or not, through `snippet_fn`), and at the right the options (`OwnPanel`), the call and «Copier». The order of the engineer's decision of 07.10.2026 (results, the call, the options folded, the preview folded) is replaced by the two views (docs/decisions.md).

**`CompressionTab`** (07.10.2026; IDs `DR-`): the same three panes. The effort card is «Effort»: the angle α, the force in kN — **labelled N at α = 0 and F above** — and the impact η_t; above 0 a card **«Appui»** appears (shown and hidden, nothing rebuilt, so a field under the pointer stays, and what it held stays in the source): the resistance f_c,90,d among the **five entries** (Général · Avec avant-bois, avec ou sans conséquence sur l'élément porteur · Appui d'extrémité, avec ou sans conséquence — the two «sans conséquence» entries greyed, with a tooltip, for a grade whose Tabelle 8/9 has no bracketed value, D30), the contact length l and the width w, which **follows the section's width** and is written over in orange with «Reprendre» (`w_stated`). The results are the tension's card, with equation (10), (11) or (12)/(13) according to α.

⚠️ *Retired by the spec 2026-10-21-commandes-typst-refonte (lot 19): `verify-bending` -- write #include domain-file("check", id) -- the member check of the «Flexion», «Déversement» tabs, its file, written out by the export, printed where the section includes it. What follows describes it as it was.* **`BendingTab`** (08.10.2026; IDs `BI-`; «Flexion», SIA 265 4.2.3 and 4.2.4, **the section alone** — «Déversement» and «Flambage» are their own modules): the same three panes. The card «Effort» holds the angle α — labelled «Angle α entre l'axe de l'élément et le fil» (`ALPHA_KEY`) —, the normal force **N signed**, «Effort normal N (traction +, compression −)» (`N_MIN` −10⁶: `effort_card` takes the kind's lowest force, 0 for the other kinds' magnitudes), and the moments about y and z in kNm, their magnitudes. At **α > 0** the field of the moment about z is set to 0 and **closed**, a muted line under it saying why — (17) to (20) read the moment about y alone, the one about z is checked with α = 0 — and the draft writes `M_z: null`; the normal force stays open, (21) or (22) then taking the strengths at that angle (the engineer's reading, §3.3undecies). A moment of 0 is written `null`. The results are `member_check.compute`'s: (14), (21) or (22) along the grain, (15)/(16), (17)–(20) or (21)/(22) with (9) or (13) at an angle, each side's η labelled «Côté tendu» / «Côté comprimé», and f_m,d with its k_h for glulam under 600 mm. «Affichage» compiles `#verify-bending(…, stability: false)` without N and `#verify-bending-normal(…, stability: false)` with one.

**A field that follows a variable** (issue #42, v0.117; `docs/domain/variable-links.md` §3.3duovicies). Beside every number of the forms — N, V_z, V_y, M_y, M_z, α, b, h, d, l, w, l_y, l_z, β_y, β_z, a, σ_m,crit (`member_check.LINKABLE`) — a button 𝑥 opens the «Variables» tab in its choosing mode, listing only what that field may take (a force for N, a length for b). Taken, the field is read-only and shows the variable's value now, converted into its unit; under it the variable's symbol and domain, a factor and an offset (value = factor × variable + offset), and «Délier», which leaves a typed field keeping its value. A variable that can no longer be read keeps the last value, in the theme's warning colour, «Introuvable : …». The strength class, the moisture class and every other list take no variable; a section that follows its position takes none either, its buttons closed until the section is stated. `MemberCheckTab.linked(spin, key, unit, label)` wraps a form's spin (`linkable_spin.LinkableSpin`) without changing how the form reads it; `fill_links` and `draft_links` read and write the source's `links`, and `member_check.stamp_version` writes 1.1 where there is one. A form row holds the wrapper: `row_of(spin)` is what `setRowVisible` names.

**«Traction» since v0.118 (issue #62, UI-003).** A tension is printed unfolded (docs/domain/member-checks.md §3.3unvicies): the tab stays the form of its inputs, writes its source through `member_check.write_source` (checked, moved into place), its page in «Affichage» is the domain of the draft (`unfold.preview_body`: the writing, its literals unchecked, no `gen:` -- a draft is in no export), its call `#include "/Formules/<id>.typ"`, and none of `#show-check-member`'s options is offered (`MemberCheckTab.UNFOLDED`). «Ouvrir dans Formules» brings the «Formules» tab forward on the check's domain; a value imported there into the check's source is what the tab then shows.
